Differences from the C12 pre-run
--------------------------------
1. §10.1 real store, cold start. Improvement, from waves 5 and 6.
   - Before: shell 16.3 s, first Overview card 57.9 s, first project row 58.4 s, settled 59.2 s.
   - Now: shell 2.6 s, first card 3.5 s, first project row 3.5 s, settled 6.1 s, over a slightly larger store.
   - Cause: this is wave 5B's progressive paint (record events batched per 16 ms) together with wave 6's single cold
     scan.
   - Consequence: the owner's first open at the sitting should now take seconds, not about a minute. prerun.txt's note
     at §10.1 and the owner's script step 1 still describe the minute.
2. §10.1 real store, warm start. Small improvement, partly noise.
   - Before: shell 2.1 s, first card 2.4 s, settled 3.8 s.
   - Now: shell 1.4 s, first card 1.4 s, settled 2.2 s.
   - Direction is consistent with the batching; the size is within what the load (about 6 now, about 9 to 17 before)
     can explain.
3. Real-store counts. Noise.
   - Before: 275 projects, 3,274 sessions, 13 foreign-live.
   - Now: 276 projects, 3,279 sessions, 14 foreign-live.
   - The owner's store kept growing, and live terminals come and go.
4. §10.4 monitor piece: failed in the full pass, passed alone. Not a product regression; a defect in the spec.
   - What failed: in the full §10.4 pass the monitor piece timed out, because the Message textbox was not in the main
     region.
   - Why: the viewer piece just before it leaves the one.txt viewer tab active in the main strip. The workflow piece
     sends through `session.send`, so it does not need the composer. The monitor piece types into the composer, so it
     does.
   - The product has not changed here: the C12 pre-run's own viewer screenshot also shows one.txt as the active main
     tab.
   - Why it never failed before: the monitor piece was added at the M6 review, and until now it had only run alone
     (MAWS_C12_PRERUN_PIECES). This is the first time the monitor piece ran after the viewer piece in one pass.
   - Re-runs:
     - Alone in the main root, it failed at the row's Resume. §10.5 had already archived the deep session, so it had
       no row, which is a precondition of the spec.
     - In a fresh root, §10.2 and then §10.4 with MAWS_C12_PRERUN_PIECES=monitor passed. The result was the same as
       the C12 review re-run: Monitor unavailable.
   - Captures: /tmp/maws-prerun-master-artifacts/pw-10.4/ (full pass) and pw-10.4-monitor/ (the failed solo re-run),
     each with Playwright's error-context.md. The screenshot of the state is
     /tmp/maws-prerun-master-artifacts/screenshots-master/prerun-10.4-viewer-light.png.
   - Fix for the spec (not applied): the monitor piece should reopen the deep session's tab first, or send through
     `session.send` as the workflow piece does.
5. The resume stall is not reproduced. Not evidence of a fix.

   - Now: none of the 16 launches stalled; the first output always came within 0.42 s. The C3 corrective's 30 s bound
     and its relaunch never fired.
