E11 final gate, packaged-suite regression on 3d09cd4b: diagnosis and gates at the fix 3e20ea32 (2026-10-08)

Symptom (gates/final2-e2e-packaged-3d09cd4b.txt and its two retries): 1, 1 and 2 click timeouts of ~31 s on the
hover-revealed "New chat in <project>" (e2e/support/sidebar.ts newChatIn), in side.spec cases 1, 2, 7 and sessions.spec
"an unstarted tab with a draft survives a relaunch"; each the first newChatIn after a fresh launch.

Cause: neither a product regression nor specific to the packaged build. The desktop's own pointer reaches the quiet e2e window:
setIgnoreMouseEvents(true) lets clicks through, but the real cursor crossing the window still arrives in the page as a
mouseover and a document mouseleave at the cursor's own coordinates, which drops :hover from the project line between
the hover and the click; the click then waits on a display:none button until it times out.
Not F2b 2.1: window 1280x820 in both builds (screen 1512x982), the project line at y 190 and the button at 263,197
(22x22), html/body scrollTop 0, no scroll event between the hover and the click.
