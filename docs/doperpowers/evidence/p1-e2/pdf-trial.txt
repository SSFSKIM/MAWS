E2 M3, the PDF trial (E2 execution document, Design §3, Decision Log 14), 2026-10-06
Electron 44.4.5 (its Chromium), macOS; the product app (MAWS_HARNESS=0 per launch, a MAWS_HARNESS=1 build), MAWS_E2E=1.

What ran: e2e/deliverables.spec.ts, case "the PDF trial", under `pnpm e2e:deliverables` through
/tmp/maws-review/waitload20.sh && /tmp/maws-review/with-slot.sh. The case writes a hand-made one-page PDF (a 200 pt page
that is mostly a filled black square) and its X12 record (kind file) into the scratch userData, then mounts an <iframe>
pointed at maws-deliverable://<recordId>/app-e2-deliverables/1 at the window's top-left (600 x 500 CSS px) in the app
page, with the scheme handler, the request and download filters, the frame guard and the app's CSP all installed as
shipped, and reads from main: the frame rect's near-black pixel count (capturePage, r, g, b < 40; the viewer's own grey
#282828 does not count) and every frame URL in the window. A frame "draws" at 2,000 or more dark pixels within 8 s.

Outcome (the final run, /tmp/maws-e2-m3-e2e-3.log):

b sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin"  draws=false darkPixels=0       frames=[app, maws-deliverable://…/1]
c no sandbox attribute                       draws=true  darkPixels=246264  frames=[app, maws-deliverable://…/1,
                                               chrome-extension://mhjfbmdgcfjbbpaeojofohoefgiehjai/index.html,
                                               maws-deliverable://…/1]
Screenshot of (c): docs/doperpowers/evidence/p1-e2/pdf-trial-c.png (the viewer's toolbar, the thumbnail and the page).

Chosen: (c), the PDF frame without a sandbox attribute; pdf.js (d) was not needed and no dependency was added. The e2e pins
it: (c) draws and (a) and (b) do not.

Why a and b fail: in a sandboxed frame Chromium never instantiates its PDF viewer (no chrome-extension frame appears in
the subtree, the frame stays white), with or without allow-same-origin; a scratch Electron probe with none of MAWS's
policies (/tmp/maws-e2-pdf-probe/main.js, SANDBOX=allow-scripts and SANDBOX="allow-scripts allow-same-origin") shows
the same, so it is the sandboxed plugins flag, not a MAWS policy.

Two policy findings the trial forced (both in src/main/deliverables/policies.ts, unit-tested):
1. The first run of (c) instantiated the viewer but drew an empty dark box. The scratch probe with request logging
   showed the PDF frame itself (frame and initiator maws-deliverable://…) asking for
   chrome-extension://mhjfbmdgcfjbbpaeojofohoefgiehjai/pdf_embedder.css, which the request filter cancelled as "not the
   scheme". The filter now also passes that one origin, the viewer's own bundled files inside Electron (never the
   network); any other chrome-extension: or chrome: URL from a deliverable frame is still cancelled.
2. Still empty after that: the app-wide CSP override (window.ts) put the app's policy (script-src 'self') on the
   viewer's own index.html, which then refused the chrome://resources scripts it is built from (the probe logged
   "Loading the script 'chrome://resources/lit/v3_0/lit.rollup.js' violates … script-src 'self'"). The override now
   leaves the viewer's pages alone as it leaves the scheme's answers alone (isCspExempt). With both, (c) draws in the
   probe and in the product app.

What still covers the unsandboxed PDF frame (Design §3, "whatever its sandbox"): it is its own origin
(maws-deliverable://<recordId>), never the app's; the session's request filter cancels anything it asks for outside the
scheme and the viewer's files; will-frame-navigate prevents its navigation away from the scheme; will-download cancels a
download whose URL or redirect chain is the scheme's (the viewer's Download button was not pressed in this trial); the
window denies new windows and top-level navigation; the WebRTC
policy is disable_non_proxied_udp. The viewer frame's sandbox stays allow-scripts for HTML.
