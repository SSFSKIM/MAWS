Your group will pump 20 more strokes of air into a sealed ball. Its mass will:

increasedecreasestay the same

Measure

Room temperatureK
Tip

Let the reading settle for three seconds before you record it.

C

Analyze

  1. Find the moles of air added. Use M(air) = 28.97 g/mol.
  2. The ball holds 5.00 L at the room temperature you recorded. Use PV = nRT to find the pressure increase, in atm.

What the cobalt may do

Fill the part tab (A, B, C on paper, the current step on a slide) and nothing else. It is the only filled colour on the page.

Copier first

Every meaning survives a black-and-white copy: the tab prints as a dark block with a white letter, write-in boxes are 1.5 px grey lines at 3.1 : 1, and no tint carries meaning.

Lecture room first

Room light roughly halves a projector’s contrast, so muted text is held at 7.8 : 1 and nothing is lighter. Slide body is 48 px (24 pt) and nothing goes below 36 px (18 pt).

Why Seravek

Chemistry puts l, I and 1 side by side (HCl, 1 L). Seravek’s tailed l keeps them apart, and so does Trebuchet MS, its stand-in on Windows.
