What the cobalt may do

Fill the activity disc (①②③ on paper, the current activity on the slide) and nothing else on a worksheet. On a slide it may also set the one word the class must say back.

Copier first

Every meaning survives a black-and-white copy: the disc prints as a dark circle with a white numeral, answer boxes are 2 px grey lines at 3.1 : 1, and no tint carries meaning. The light panel tint drops out on the copier and is only a screen nicety.

Projector first

A lit classroom halves a projector’s contrast, so muted text is held at 7.8 : 1 and no text is lighter than it. Slide body is 56 px (28 pt), captions never below 40 px (20 pt); one idea per line.

Hangul

Body 18 px at 1.8 on paper, 56 px at 1.45 on the slide; word-break: keep-all so a word is never split across lines.

Slide: the same activity, read from the back row
