What terracotta may do in a document

Mark the reader’s one action: a 2 px underline under the action phrase, or the small disc beside “할 일”. One per page.

Set text only at large sizes (24 px, or 19 px bold) because it measures 3.7 : 1 on the ground. A body-size action stays in ink and takes the underline.

On a slide it may set one figure or one phrase at deck sizes, and mark where the talk is in the footer.

What it may not do

Headings, table heads, rules, panels and status. Status uses the four status colours with a word beside them, never colour alone.

Print

The ground prints white and shadows drop. The page reads correctly in black and white: the underline turns grey and still points.
