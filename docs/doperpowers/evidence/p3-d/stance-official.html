Why there is no accent hue

The only colour a Korean official document carries is the 직인, the school’s red seal. This system leaves that red to the seal and draws none of its own, so nothing on the page can be mistaken for the school’s authority. Everything survives the staff-room laser printer.

What the accent token does

It holds the ink and is used reversed: white on ink marks the one thing the reader must do (회신 기한) or decide (결정 필요). One per page.

Myeongjo to read, gothic to find

Body in myeongjo, as HWP-era school paperwork is read; titles, table heads, captions and emphasis in gothic. On a projected deck the body switches to gothic, because thin myeongjo strokes wash out on a projector.

Status

Symbol and word first, colour second: ○ 완료, △ 진행, × 미제출.
