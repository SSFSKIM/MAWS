## Recheck B: design-workbench follow-up (eadd6bb7d, 410d15358, 2f8113ea4)

**Findings:**
- **[P3] Forced settle can revert all of the owner's re-orders when the parent kept them** ([order.ts:94-95, 110](/Users/new/Developer/GitHub/MAWS/.claude/worktrees/agent-af4cd2993bc56e015/src/artifacts/design/order.ts#L94)).
  - **Cause:** the new rule counts a pair all three orders agree on as lost from the first round. That includes inversions created only by the initial moved-board placement, which settling the agent's own pairs would have fixed anyway, so extra boards get settled into the sent order.
  - **Example:** `mergeBoardOrder(abcde, adcbe, cedab, [], true)` now gives `a,d,c,b,e`, the whole sent order, which reverts all 5 pairs only the owner re-ordered. The parent gave `d,c,e,a,b`, which keeps 4 of those 5 and also violates neither invariant.
  - **Frequency:** this happens in 36 of the 14,400 forced five-board cases, and none at three or four boards.
  - **Possible fix:** a two-phase settle, which counts agreed pairs only once no agent pair is lost. I prototyped it: zero violations at 3, 4 and 5 boards. It keeps owner-only pairs better in 39 cases and worse in 1 (20,581 vs 20,498 of 36,000).
  - **Why only P3:** it is a forced publish only, at five or more boards, and "the agent wins" is the stated force semantics. Logging it as debt is reasonable.
