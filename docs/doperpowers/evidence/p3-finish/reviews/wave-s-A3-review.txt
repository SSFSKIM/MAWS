## Verdict
correct — I found nothing blocking. I traced the hold and its release by reading documents.ts, service.ts, replica.ts, store.endTurn, session-maws and the host outbox.
- **Timer cleanup:** the timer is unref'd and fires at most once, 4 s later. On a closed session or replica, or a closed service, it does nothing (`setPresence` returns on a destroyed replica, and closing the connection removes the agent's awareness on the host).
- **Refused batches:** a refused batch never reaches the publish.
- **`clearPresence`:** a replica that is not held is still cleared at once.
- **Concurrency:** each session has its own replicas and held-replica entries, and a second batch within 4 s clears the earlier timer and holds afresh.
- **The "erase newer presence" case the worker logged as low:** it cannot happen when calls run one after another, because every new batch clears the old timer. It needs two overlapping `artifact_edit` calls from the same session on the same document (for example parallel subagents). That leaves a stray timer, a race that already existed before this change; the newer outline is cut by a few milliseconds, and the name-only state it may leave draws nothing in the editor.
- **What I ran:** documents.test.ts and service.test.ts pass (109 of 109). Their fake timers are sound: only `setTimeout`/`clearTimeout` are faked, and the batch commit goes through `setImmediate`.
- **Not run:** e2e/document-presence.spec.ts itself; its result depends on the Linux-lane run.
