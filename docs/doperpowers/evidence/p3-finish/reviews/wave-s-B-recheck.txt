**[minor] Undock can cut off a composer-card continuation already in flight** ([session-context.ts:109](/Users/new/Developer/GitHub/MAWS/.claude/worktrees/agent-af4cd2993bc56e015/src/renderer/src/artifacts/session-context.ts#L109))
- The trigger: the owner presses "Trust and start" on the record's composer card, `continueRecord` is running, and the comment's resume reaches ready first and undocks the card.
- Then `continueRecord`'s `current()` turns false (setup-actions.ts:150-161). Depending on timing, one of two things happens:
  - the message is silently not sent; or
  - it is sent but the composer is not cleared, which invites a duplicate send.
- This needs presses on both surfaces at nearly the same time, so it is unlikely.
- Fix: skip the undock while `useSetup.getState().continuing.has(appSessionId)`. Logging it in the tracker is reasonable.
