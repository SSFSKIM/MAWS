Your choices

  • fix: fix it in P3-F now.
  • P4: real, but defer it to the next phase.
  • keep: keep it as built.
  • dismiss: not an issue; drop it.

The sitting session's reading (the coloured tag)

  • defect wrong whatever the preference: against the spec or the design system's rules, a broken layout, misleading text, or lost input.
  • taste works as built; whether it should change is your preference.
  • ruling a design or authority question the spec doesn't settle; you decide.
  • unverified may be the automation driver rather than MAWS; needs your own hands.
  • environment caused outside the sitting build.
