Owner request, 2026-10-10, relayed by the cua_repl session (its brief after a read of master 9548a40a); received by the architect during P2-F. Routing: an early P3-era item under C5 (the shell: panel width, collapse, maximize), designed in P3's brainstorming pass; the P2 spec's §9 Deferred list points here.

The owner's words: when the person works in the in-app browser or a deliverable, the panel is the main thing, not the session; dragging the session/panel divider far enough to the left should turn the session into a card popped over the panel's bottom-left, like Claude Docs' chat popover (a card with the transcript, the composer, an expand button and a close button); the session survives the small width because of the to-human output style.

The owner's answers to the cua session's four questions: trigger = a drag past the threshold snaps into the card, and a chord plus a toolbar/panel-header button toggle it; close (×) leaves a small session pill at the bottom-left with an unread dot, click or the chord reopens; the card is anchored bottom-left, resizable by its top and right edges, size remembered per tab; routing = this brief to the architect as an early P3-era item under C5.

The cua session's design brief (a starting point; the architect owns the design):
1. Model. A third panel state beside collapsed and maximized: `regions.panel.floating` (schema `src/shared/ipc/schema/layout.ts:80`, a RegionsV4 migration). Floating implies the panel takes the width as maximized does (`gridColumns`, `Shell.tsx:24-27`, main at 0 px and inert) plus a Shell-level card (a sibling of ToastHost and PanelRegion, never inside region-main, which is inert then) drawing the focused pane's active tab's session with the same `SessionPane` the Side pane reuses (`R/side/ThreadView.tsx`; narrow widths already measured at 280-640 by C8a). The one-composer-per-session rule (`useComposerStore.focusTargets`, `CardThread.tsx:70-73`) means the main pane must not also mount that session's SessionPane while the card has it: while floating, the main pane for that tab renders nothing (it is at 0 px anyway) and the card is the only mount. `visibility.ts:29` counts the session visible while the card is open, not when closed (then the pill's unread dot applies). Expand (⤢) = un-float: `maximized:false`, `floating:false`, the panel back to its restore width, the card unmounted, the main pane remounts the session. Overview (`setOverviewRect`, `OverviewPanel.tsx`) is the pattern: `role=dialog`-free (see 4), draggable no, resizable yes by top/right/top-right, `placeRect`-clamped to the panel region, MIN 360×240, default 420 × min(60vh, 640), margin 16; the rect per tab stored on the tab record (`main.tabs`), not in `regions`.
2. Triggers. `Resizer.tsx` has only the min-side `collapseThreshold` (:99); add its mirror, an `expandThreshold` on the max side: when the drag goes ≥ 40 px past `panelMax` the resizer calls `onExpand`, which PanelRegion maps to `panel.float`. Actions: `panel.float` (toggle; keymap action in `src/shared/keymap/actions.ts`, chord to choose, ⌘⇧B/⌘B/⌘\/⌘0/⌘L are taken; the page shadows only ⌘L and ⌘⇧B), a Float button beside Maximize in the panel header (`PanelRegion.tsx:52-62`), and the toolbar toggle unchanged. The pill after × is a Shell-level bottom-left chip with the session title, the unread dot from the session's unread rule, click = reopen.
3. Keep the docked request visible: the card replaces the composer surface, so `DockedRequest`/`SessionTabNotes` stay in the card's stack (`SessionPane.tsx:62-95` already stacks them).
4. The browser constraint, the one real design fork. A DOM card over a Browser tab sits under the native WebContentsView unless it raises cover, and cover freezes the whole page to a still for as long as the card is up (`covering.ts`, `cover.ts:46-52`: a `role=dialog` layer covers while mounted; a measured layer covers while it overlaps a pane). A persistent card cannot live on a frozen page. Recommendation, following P2-E2's precedent for the comment card (P2 Decision Log 25, `placement.ts:51-60`, `browser/Pane.tsx:17-20`: the view is never under the card and no cover is raised): over a Browser tab the card reserves its space instead of overlapping: the pane's placed rect excludes a left column of the card's width (full pane height; the card fills that column, so for Browser tabs the card is a column, width-resizable only), and the page stays live beside it. Over DOM tools (deliverables, IDE tools, terminal, side) the card floats as the owner pictured. The alternative, a floating card that covers the page to a still while open, is the Overview's trade and is wrong for a card the person keeps open. The owner has not seen this fork yet.
5. Charter and ledger. A dated §19 line amending §5.2/§5.6 and the C5 residue (:404: C5 owns width, collapse, maximize; floating joins them), X5's host prop (`maximized` → the host sees floating as maximized), the layout schema version in the ledger, the keymap action. e2e in `e2e:panel` and `e2e:browser`: drag past max floats; chord and button toggle; × leaves the pill with an unread dot; expand restores the column and the composer focus; over a Browser tab the view bounds exclude the card column and `covered` stays false; over a deliverable the card overlays; no focusTargets collision (one composer per session).

Architect's take on the fork (2026-10-10, recorded for P3's design pass, not a ruling): the column over a Browser tab and the floating card over DOM tools, as recommended; a card that freezes the page it sits over defeats the owner's picture of working in the browser with the session beside it. The owner decides at P3's brainstorming; if the owner raises it earlier, the answer is recorded here.
