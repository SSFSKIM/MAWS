## Brief (the spec's section, verbatim)

### P2-D: Resume chooser — brief (M; M+ with the lazy preview)

- **Purpose:** one chooser for `/resume` and the sidebar's Resume…, built to Desktop's picker: the owner finds a session by what they remember (a word of the title, a folder, a branch, when) and sees what it was before opening it.
- **Acceptance:** **G1** `ResumeWidget` and `ResumePicker` become one component with one data path; both entry points open it. **G2** search over title, folder and branch (branch joins `sessionMatcher`). **G3** groups Today, Yesterday, Earlier (then by week) with project and branch badges; order and shown age agree (one activity time). **G4** the preview pane: cached title, folder, branch, last activity and the owner-aware last answer at once; then, for the settled selection only and after a short debounce, a bounded read of the recent turns (one new read in the ledger, no engine launched, no read-state or unread change); a terminal-only session previews from disk the same way. **G5** Enter opens in the current tab through F2b 2.5's replacement, ⌘-Enter in a new tab; the scope is the current project with All projects one click away (owner decision 5); Current branch and All worktrees stay as toggles; archived and hidden sessions stay out unless asked. `e2e/resume-picker.spec.ts` and the widget tests cover both entries.
- **Edges:** blocked-by: —. blocks: —. (Opening shows the transcript; the first Send continues the session through A once A lands.)
- **Contracts:** extends X2 (one bounded read, named in the ledger); consumes X19 (the chooser with its preview pane is wider than the 560 px utility size); reads X17 when present [advisory].
- **Design inheritance:** owner decision 5 [binding]; report F §4.2 and the plan's 3.D [advisory]; Desktop's picker rows (title, branch, folder badge, time; no message count) [advisory].
- **Required:** required.
- **Status:** not-dispatched (dispatchable at approval).

## Decisions

1. 2026-10-09, `session.recentTurns` (confirmed by the architect, ledger §1 at 02510523): main-owned, `{appSessionId, limit 1..20}` → `{turns: {prompt: {text, at} | null, answer: {text, at} | null, toolCalls}[], earlier, source: 'transcript' | 'none'}`, oldest first; one read of the last 256 KiB of the engine's transcript at the index's `transcript_path` (else the path derived from the record), current chain only, classified by the host's pure `recordToEvents`; the same path for live, exited and terminal-only sessions; immediate (no host call); no launch, no `setVisible`, no unread or index change, no follow. Rejected: main's read model (digests, not events) and the host's `session.events` (a stream-less session starts a follow, a long-lived watcher per selection). The answer goes through master's owner-aware `previewLine` (`src/shared/owner/preview.ts`, E11 F2b 2.6), not a reimplementation of C's grammar (the architect's correction); C's module may replace it when C lands.
2. 2026-10-09, groups: Today, Yesterday, "Earlier this week", "Last week", then one group per local calendar week ("Week of Sep 14"; the year added outside the current one), weeks starting Monday. The brief's "Today, Yesterday, Earlier (then by week)" read as weekly groups after Yesterday. Claude Desktop 2.16120.0 groups the seven daily buckets by date and then by month (`c042dc97b-CRGVyvdk.js:3-91`); weeks are the brief's word. A row shows the clock time under Today and Yesterday, the weekday and clock under the rest of this week, the date under older weeks; the preview shows the relative age ("2 hours ago"); all from the one `lastActivity` time that orders the rows.
3. 2026-10-09, "archived and hidden sessions stay out unless asked": a fourth toggle, **Archived** (no chord: the CLI has no such toggle), brings in archived sessions and a hidden project's; such a row carries an "Archived" badge when archived.
4. 2026-10-09, scope: the project the chooser opened on, its main checkout (the CLI's default, kept); a session with no project opens on All projects (report F §4.2: "current project when context exists, All projects otherwise"), and All projects off then shows Other sessions. `/resume` leaves out the session it was typed in.
5. 2026-10-09, gestures: a click or the arrows select and preview, Enter or a double click opens in the current tab (`openSession`, F2b 2.5's replacement), ⌘Enter in a new tab (`openTab`); no separate Resume button (the old widget's C10 Resume): opening shows the transcript and the first Send continues the session once A lands.
