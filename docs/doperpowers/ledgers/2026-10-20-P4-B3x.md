## Decisions

- Dragging the divider to float folds nothing: the drag moves the split's own edge, not the panel's.
- Expand over a maximized or floating panel folds it too, and leaving Expand brings it back docked: a maximized panel would hide the document again, and B3's un-maximize before a float was never restored either.
- A tab-set switch keeps the fold with its float, so the panel stays folded in the other set.
- The fold persists as the float does, in `main.tabs` at version 2 with no X15 slot, at B3's Decision Log 1 downgrade cost.
- Expand stays where B3 offered it: the panel's Maximize, View › Comments › Expand and a document alone in main fold nothing.
- 2026-10-20 (the architect, at the landing): the three owner-facing calls above are accepted, each open to the owner's overturn (P4 Decision Log 43).
