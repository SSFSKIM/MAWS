## Purpose

After E9 the owner holds a key, speaks, and the words land in the composer: hold ⌥Space (or press-and-hold the microphone button in the composer's toolbar), say "rewrite the summary row to show the PR state", and the composer shows the words as they are recognised, greyed while provisional, then settles them at the caret when the key is released. Nothing leaves the Mac: recognition runs through a small Swift helper the app bundles, over Apple's Speech framework, on-device wherever the chosen language supports it. The first hold asks macOS for the microphone and for speech recognition with MAWS's reason in the dialog; a refusal is shown in the toolbar with the exact System Settings pane that grants it. This closes the third item of the owner's start-of-phase list (P-3: "voice" sent them to a terminal, or rather to another app) and §2 item 3 of the parent ("The owner holds a key, speaks, and the text lands in the composer").

To see it working: `pnpm build:helper && pnpm dev`, open any session tab (a New chat tab is enough), hold ⌥Space, speak, release. Without a microphone or with permission refused, the toolbar's microphone control states the reason and offers the System Settings pane. The automated proof runs the same renderer and main code over a scripted recognizer (`pnpm e2e:voice`), so no test ever opens the owner's microphone or raises a consent dialog.

## Terms

- **Capture**: one hold of the dictation key, from the accepted keydown to the final text (or its cancellation). Identified by a **capture id** the renderer mints (`crypto.randomUUID()`), carried on every command and event of that capture, so a late event of an earlier capture is recognisable and dropped.
- **Helper**: `maws-voice-helper`, the Swift command-line program under `native/voice-helper/`, built to `build/voice-helper/maws-voice-helper` (gitignored) and bundled at `Contents/Resources/voice-helper/maws-voice-helper`. It owns the microphone and Apple's `SFSpeechRecognizer`; main spawns it and talks JSON lines over its stdin and stdout.
- **Backend**: main's object behind `voice.*`: the **helper backend** (spawns the helper) or the **scripted backend** (the e2e stand-in, driven by `MAWS_E2E_VOICE`). Both implement `VoiceBackend` (Interfaces).
- **Partial** and **final**: Apple's provisional transcript while audio flows (`bestTranscription.formattedString` of a non-final result) and the transcript delivered after the audio ends (`isFinal`). In the composer a partial is **interim text**: drawn at the caret in the muted colour, replaced by the next partial, and replaced by the final on release.
- **Hold action**: a keymap action whose binding runs on the accepted keydown and is told when the hold ends (the physical key or any modifier of the chord is released, or the window loses the keyboard). `composer.dictate` is the first and only one.
- **Dictation sink**: what the composer's editor hands the dictation controller for one capture: `partial(text)`, `final(text)`, `cancel()`; it owns the interim range in the document.
- **Probe**: the helper's non-prompting self-report: supported locales, the system locale, whether on-device recognition is supported for a locale, and the current permission states. Reading a permission state never prompts; only `start` does.
- **TCC**: macOS's privacy consent system. Two services are involved: Microphone and Speech Recognition, each with its own dialog on first use and its own pane in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

## Design (the residue §3 H leaves open, with reasons)

### 1. The helper and its protocol

`native/voice-helper/main.swift` is one Swift file (a second, `Recognizer.swift`, if the executor prefers to split the audio and recognition code), compiled by `scripts/build-voice-helper.sh` with `swiftc -O -target arm64-apple-macosx13.0 -framework Speech -framework AVFoundation` to `build/voice-helper/maws-voice-helper`, with `native/voice-helper/Info.plist` embedded as the binary's `__TEXT,__info_plist` section (`-Xlinker -sectcreate -Xlinker __TEXT -Xlinker __info_plist -Xlinker native/voice-helper/Info.plist`). That plist carries `CFBundleIdentifier dev.maws.voice-helper`, `CFBundleName`, and both usage descriptions. The reason for embedding: TCC reads usage descriptions from the responsible process's bundle, which for a child of the packaged app is MAWS.app (whose `Info.plist` gets the keys through `electron-builder.yml`'s `mac.extendInfo`), but under `pnpm dev` the responsible app is `node_modules/electron/dist/Electron.app`, whose plist lacks the speech key; the embedded plist is the belt to that brace, and costs one linker flag. If the dev-mode request still terminates the helper (the process exits with a privacy-violation report), the helper backend reports `helper_crashed` and the toolbar says so; that is a development limitation recorded in Surprises, not a gate.

The helper speaks newline-delimited JSON, one object per line, commands on stdin and events on stdout; stderr is for logs only and is never parsed. It prints `{"type":"ready","protocol":1}` once at launch. Commands:

| stdin | What happens | stdout, in order |
|---|---|---|
| `{"cmd":"probe","locale":"en-US"}` (`locale` optional; `null` or absent = the system locale) | Non-prompting reads: `SFSpeechRecognizer.supportedLocales()`, `Locale.current`, `SFSpeechRecognizer(locale:)?.supportsOnDeviceRecognition`, `SFSpeechRecognizer.authorizationStatus()`, `AVCaptureDevice.authorizationStatus(for: .audio)` | `{"type":"probe","locales":["en-US",…],"systemLocale":"ko-KR","locale":"en-US","supportsOnDevice":true,"permission":{"microphone":"authorized","speech":"notDetermined"}}` |
| `{"cmd":"start","id":"<captureId>","locale":"en-US"}` (`locale` as above) | The capture is reserved at once (a second `start` answers `busy`). Then, in order and each checking that the capture was not cancelled meanwhile: the recognizer is created and must report `supportsOnDeviceRecognition` (else `on_device_unavailable`), `AVCaptureDevice.requestAccess(for: .audio)`, then `SFSpeechRecognizer.requestAuthorization` (each prompts once, only while `notDetermined`); then the audio engine starts (`AVAudioEngine`, a tap on the input node appending buffers to an `SFSpeechAudioBufferRecognitionRequest` with `shouldReportPartialResults = true`, `addsPunctuation = true`, `requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true`) and the recognition task starts | `{"type":"permission","id":…,"microphone":"authorized","speech":"authorized"}`, then `{"type":"started","id":…,"locale":"en-US"}`, then zero or more `{"type":"partial","id":…,"text":"…"}` |
| `{"cmd":"stop","id":…}` | Stops the engine, removes the tap, `request.endAudio()`; waits for the task's final result | `{"type":"final","id":…,"text":"…","timedOut":false}`; when no final result arrives within 3 s, the last partial (or `""`) with `"timedOut":true` |
| `{"cmd":"cancel","id":…}` | Valid in **every** phase of the capture: before the permission callbacks answered (the flag is checked after each, so audio never starts for a cancelled capture), while audio runs (`task.cancel()`, stop the engine) or while a `stop` waits for its final (the final is dropped). Always answers within one queue turn | `{"type":"cancelled","id":…}` |
| `{"cmd":"quit"}` | exits 0 | nothing |

Errors are `{"type":"error","id":"<captureId or null>","code":"<code>","message":"<Apple's description or the helper's>"}` with codes `microphone_denied`, `speech_denied` (either `.denied` or `.restricted`; the renderer names the pane), `recognizer_unavailable` (`SFSpeechRecognizer(locale:)` returned nil or `isAvailable` is false), `locale_unsupported` (not in `supportedLocales()`), `on_device_unavailable` (supported, but no on-device model for it on this Mac), `audio_failed` (the engine would not start: no input device, or `AVAudioEngine.start()` threw), `recognition_failed` (the task ended in an error with no final result, before or after `stop`, except the two non-failures below), `busy` (a `start` while a capture runs), `bad_request` (an unparseable or unknown line, an empty or whitespace-only line included). Two task errors are not failures: Apple's "no speech detected" after `endAudio()` (`kAFAssistantErrorDomain`, code 1110), which is what a silent hold produces and settles as `final` with the last partial (usually `""`), and the cancellation error of a task the helper itself cancelled (codes 216 and 301), which is already answered by `cancelled`. A `start` whose permission request is refused emits `permission` then the matching `*_denied` error and no `started`. One capture at a time; the helper never spawns a second task. **One serial `DispatchQueue` is the state owner**: every command, every permission callback, every recognition callback and every stdout write runs on it, so a `cancel` and a permission answer cannot interleave and the "was this capture cancelled" check after each asynchronous step is exact. Stdin is read on a background thread and hands each line to that queue; the main thread runs the run loop. EOF on stdin exits 0 (the parent is gone).

Local only: `requiresOnDeviceRecognition` is always `true`, so no audio and no transcript leave the Mac, which is what G2 ("recognition runs locally") and A-6 ("no cloud dependency") promise and what the purpose, the permission reasons and the Settings copy say. A locale Apple supports but without an on-device model on this Mac is refused at `start` with `on_device_unavailable`; the toolbar notice and Settings › Voice say so and point at where macOS downloads dictation languages ("System Settings › Keyboard › Dictation"). `probe.supportsOnDevice` tells Settings which languages qualify, so the select marks the others. The alternative, falling back to Apple's speech servers for those locales, was rejected in the adversarial review (Decision Log): it would make "nothing leaves the Mac" false for exactly the languages a bilingual owner would pick.

Why `SFSpeechRecognizer` and not macOS 26's `SpeechAnalyzer`: the brief and A-6 name it; it is available on every macOS MAWS targets, needs no model asset download, and push-to-talk utterances are short. `SpeechAnalyzer` is noted as a later upgrade in Outcomes.

### 2. Process lifecycle in main

`src/main/voice/helper-backend.ts` spawns the helper lazily on the first `start` or `probe` (`child_process.spawn`, stdio piped, no shell), waits for `ready` within 5 s (else kills it and answers `helper_failed`), and keeps it alive for later captures: a warm helper answers a hold in tens of milliseconds, a cold one costs a process launch and the recognizer's first-use setup. After 10 minutes with no capture it sends `quit` and the next use respawns; `dispose()` (at `will-quit`) is synchronous and final: it writes `quit`, ends the helper's stdin (EOF is an exit for the helper) and sends SIGTERM at once, since the helper holds nothing worth a grace period and a timer armed at `will-quit` cannot fire after main exits. The helper is the second line: at launch it installs a `DispatchSource.makeProcessSource(identifier: getppid(), eventMask: .exit)` watch on its own global queue that calls `exit(0)` when the parent is gone (and exits at once if it is already orphaned, `getppid() == 1`), so a helper stuck on its serial queue still dies with the app. A helper exit while a capture is active surfaces as `error { code: 'helper_crashed' }` for that capture; the next `start` respawns. `probe` waits at most `HELPER_PROBE_MS` (5 s) for its answer; past that, or when the helper has already exited by the time the ready wait returns, the probe answers `helper.state = 'failed'` ("probe timed out" or "helper exited") and the helper is killed so the next call respawns it. Stdout lines are parsed with zod (`HelperEventSchema`); a line that fails validation is logged and dropped, never thrown into the service. The helper's path: `MAWS_VOICE_HELPER` when set (an absolute path, for tests and a developer's own build), else `join(process.resourcesPath, 'voice-helper', 'maws-voice-helper')` when `app.isPackaged`, else `join(process.cwd(), 'build', 'voice-helper', 'maws-voice-helper')` (`process.cwd()` is what main already uses as the development app root). A path that does not exist or is not executable answers `voice.status.helper.state = 'missing'` with the path, and `voice.start` with `{ accepted: false, reason: 'helper_missing' }`, which the toolbar renders with the path and, in development, the hint "run `pnpm build:helper`" (G2: "the helper's absence or failure shows a reason").

`src/main/voice/service.ts` (`VoiceService`) holds the one active capture app-wide (one microphone): `{ captureId, senderId, phase: 'starting' | 'listening' | 'stopping', startedAt }`. The reservation is **synchronous**: `start` records the capture before its first `await`, so two invokes racing through the router (which dispatches handlers independently) see each other, and the second answers `{ accepted: false, reason: 'busy' }`. A `cancel` for the active capture clears it at once and calls `backend.cancel(captureId)`, whatever the phase; a `start` still awaiting its backend then resolves `{ accepted: false, reason: 'cancelled' }`, and every later backend event for that id is dropped as stale. Events from the backend are forwarded as `voice.event` only while their capture id is the active one and the phase admits them (a `partial` after `final` is dropped; a `final` or `cancelled` or `error` clears the capture). Sender ownership: the capture remembers `sender.id`; `stop` and `cancel` from another sender are ignored with a warning; the service subscribes to the sender's `destroyed` and cancels. Emission uses main's `emit('voice.event', …)` (every window; a single-window app, and the renderer filters by capture id). `voice.openPrivacySettings` opens `x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.preference.security?Privacy_Microphone` or `…?Privacy_SpeechRecognition` through `shell.openExternal`; under `MAWS_E2E=1` it records the call and opens nothing.

The helper backend carries the same invariant across its own asynchrony: `start` registers a pending entry `{ captureId, cancelled: false }` before waiting for the helper's `ready`; `cancel(captureId)` flips the flag and, if the `start` line was already written, writes `cancel`; after the ready wait, a flagged entry skips the write and resolves `{ accepted: false, reason: 'cancelled' }`. So a hold released during a cold spawn or during the permission dialogs never opens the microphone afterwards: the renderer cancels, main clears and flags, the backend skips or the helper's own per-step check skips, and whichever `started` or `permission` line still arrives is dropped by id.

### 3. The backend choice and the e2e stand-in

`src/main/voice/register.ts` chooses the backend once at startup: `MAWS_E2E=1` and `MAWS_E2E_VOICE` set → the scripted backend; `MAWS_E2E=1` and `MAWS_E2E_VOICE` unset → the helper backend in **probe-only** mode (`start` answers `{ accepted: false, reason: 'e2e_refused' }`; `probe` runs, since every read it makes is non-prompting), which is how the packaged e2e proves the bundled helper launches without ever opening the microphone; otherwise the helper backend. The gate is the same shape as `src/main/files/e2e-pick.ts` and `src/main/sessions/e2e-trash.ts`.

`src/main/voice/scripted-backend.ts` reads `process.env.MAWS_E2E_VOICE` at each `probe` and `start`, so a Playwright test changes the next answer through `electronApp.evaluate` on main's `process.env`, as the pick seam is driven. The value is JSON:

```json
{
  "permission": { "microphone": "authorized", "speech": "authorized" },
  "startedDelayMs": 0,
  "partials": ["hello", "hello world"],
  "partialDelayMs": 40,
  "final": "Hello world.",
  "lateAfterFinal": false,
  "error": null,
  "helper": "ready"
}
```

Every field is optional with the defaults shown (`final` defaults to the last partial). `permission.microphone` or `.speech` of `denied` emits `permission` then the matching `*_denied` error and no `started`. `error: { "code": "audio_failed", "message": "…" }` emits `permission`, then the error instead of `started`. `helper: "missing"` makes `probe` report the helper missing and `start` answer `{ accepted: false, reason: 'helper_missing' }`. `lateAfterFinal: true` emits one more `partial` 20 ms after the `final`, which the service must drop. `stop` clears the pending partial timers and emits `final` at once; `cancel` at any point (before `started` included) clears every pending timer and emits `cancelled`. The backend keeps a log on `globalThis.__mawsVoiceScript = { starts: [{ captureId, locale }], stops: [captureId], cancels: [captureId], privacyPanes: [pane] }` that tests read through `electronApp.evaluate`; `globalThis.__mawsVoiceBackend` is `'scripted'`, `'helper'` or `'helper-probe-only'` under `MAWS_E2E=1` only.

Because the backend is chosen once at startup, an e2e file that wants the scripted backend **launches** with the variable set (`launchMaws({ env: { MAWS_E2E_VOICE: '{}', … } })`) and then changes the script per case through `electronApp.evaluate`; the packaged probe-only case launches without it (the launcher copies the runner's environment, so the spec deletes `MAWS_E2E_VOICE` from the env it passes when the runner's shell happens to carry one).

### 4. Permission flow

Permission is requested by the helper on the first `start` (never by `probe`): the microphone dialog, then the speech-recognition dialog, each only while its state is `notDetermined`; the reason shown is the app's `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` ("MAWS uses the microphone to dictate into the composer while you hold the dictation key.") and `NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription` ("MAWS transcribes your dictation on this Mac with Apple's speech recognition."), written into `electron-builder.yml` under `mac.extendInfo` and into the helper's embedded plist. While the dialogs are up the toolbar shows "Asking for permission…"; a release during that time is remembered and the capture is cancelled as soon as the helper reports (nothing was heard, so there is no final to wait for). A denial renders in the toolbar as a notice with the pane named and a button that opens it: "Microphone access is off for MAWS · Open System Settings" or "Speech recognition is off for MAWS · Open System Settings"; the same two states appear in Settings › Voice. `restricted` (an MDM policy) uses the same copy with "and cannot be turned on here". A hardened-runtime signed build also needs the `com.apple.security.device.audio-input` entitlement: `build/entitlements.mac.plist` carries it and `electron-builder.yml` names it as `mac.entitlements` and `mac.entitlementsInherit`, which electron-builder applies only when it signs (today's default build is unsigned and `hardenedRuntime: false`, so nothing changes for `pnpm verify`).

### 5. The keymap action and hold semantics

`composer.dictate` ("Hold to dictate") is a renderer-sink, `menu: null`, **app-scoped** action with default chord `Alt+Space` and a new `hold: true` flag on `ActionSpec`. App scope, like `composer.focus` (⌘L): the owner holds the key wherever focus is and the words go to the focused pane's composer, resolved centrally through `focusedSession()` (`src/renderer/src/composer/ComposerAppKeys.tsx`) and that session's registered focus target, so a split never arbitrates by registration order and no composer-scoped binding has to be focused first. App scope also means the settings capture already refuses a bare letter or Space for it (`captureChord`: "an app-wide shortcut needs ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥", a function key excepted), which is exactly the restriction a hold action needs so it cannot steal typing. The alternative, composer scope, would require the editor to hold focus before dictating and would need a new capture rule; rejected.

Why `Alt+Space`: a thumb-and-finger hold used by macOS dictation apps (Superwhisper's default), free of role items (`MENU_ROLE_CHORDS`) and of every current default, and not `Ctrl+Space`, which switches input sources on a Mac with more than one (the owner types Korean). Chromium on macOS reports Option+Space's `key` as the character it would insert (a no-break space), not `' '`, so the chord vocabulary's `Space` is matched on `KeyboardEvent.code === 'Space'` from now on (`src/shared/keymap/chord.ts`, `matchesChord`) and the settings capture reads `code === 'Space'` as `Space` before its `key` lookup (`settings-tab/keymap.ts`, `tokenOf`); nothing bound today uses Space, so no existing binding changes meaning. The hold is rebindable like every action; a saved override of `composer.dictate` is honoured for both the down and the release.

Hold semantics live in the dispatcher (`src/renderer/src/keymap/dispatcher.ts`), which today installs only a capture-phase `keydown`:

- `ChordBinding` gains `release?(reason: 'keyup' | 'lost'): void`; `useKeyChord` gains `onRelease` in its options and calls `release('lost')` from its own cleanup when its binding is the latched one.
- On an accepted keydown for an action whose spec has `hold`, the dispatcher runs the binding and **latches** `{ id, binding, code: event.code, chord, released: false }`. While the latch exists, every further keydown whose `event.code` is the latched code is swallowed (`preventDefault`, nothing runs) whether or not the chord's modifiers are still down: key repeat is suppressed for the physical key until its own `keyup`, so releasing ⌥ first does not let the still-held Space start typing spaces into the composer. A keydown for a different code while latched dispatches normally.
- `connectKeys` also installs capture-phase `keyup` on the window, `blur` on the window and `visibilitychange` on the document. `keyup` **releases the capture** (`release('keyup')`, once; `released` is set) when `event.code === latch.code` or when the key is a modifier the chord requires (`event.key === 'Alt'` for `chord.alt`, `'Meta'`/`chord.meta`, `'Control'`/`chord.ctrl`, `'Shift'`/`chord.shift`): the hold ends when any part of the chord is let go, which also covers Cmd chords, whose letter keyup Chromium does not deliver while ⌘ is held. The latch itself is **cleared** only by the latched code's `keyup`, or by `blur` / a hidden document, which also release with `'lost'` when the capture was not yet released. So the capture's end (any part of the chord) and the key's suppression (the physical key) are two separate events.
- The latch holds the binding object, so a keymap change, a focus move or a re-render between down and up cannot lose the release; the release is never matched against the full chord (modifiers may already be up).

### 6. The dictation controller (renderer) and its state

`src/renderer/src/composer/dictation/` holds: `store.ts` (`useDictationStore`), `controller.ts` (`DictationController`), `DictationKeys.tsx` (mounted once from `App.tsx` beside `ComposerAppKeys`, and from the composer harness), `MicControl.tsx` (the toolbar control; `frame/Toolbar.tsx` renders it where the "no mic until dictation exists" comment stands, after the plugin mode slot and before the flexible spacer).

Store shape: `{ capture: { appSessionId, captureId, phase: 'requesting' | 'listening' | 'finishing', locale: string | null, interim: string } | null; notice: { appSessionId, kind: 'microphone_denied' | 'speech_denied' | 'unavailable' | 'failed' | 'busy' | 'no_editor', message: string } | null }`. A notice is per session, shown in that session's toolbar until the next successful capture starts there or its × is pressed.

The controller's events and transitions (one capture at a time; `press` while any capture exists is ignored, the toolbar already shows "Listening…" or "Finishing…"). Every row that leaves to idle also clears the store's capture; every `voice.*` answer and every event is identity-checked against the current capture id first, and one for another id is dropped:

| State | Event | Action | Next |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle | `press(appSessionId)` (the key's down resolves `focusedSession()`; the mic button passes its own session) | `target.dictation.begin()`; null → notice `no_editor` ("Dictation needs a composer that can take text") and stay idle; else mint `captureId`, `voice.start({ captureId, locale: settings.voice.locale })` | requesting |
| requesting | `voice.start` answers `{ accepted: false, reason }` | `sink.cancel()`; notice by reason (`busy` → "Another dictation is still finishing", `helper_missing` → "Dictation is unavailable · helper not found at <path>", `helper_failed` → "Dictation is unavailable · the helper did not start", `e2e_refused` → "Dictation is off under the test harness", `cancelled` → no notice) | idle |
| requesting | `release(any reason)` | `voice.cancel({ captureId })` at once (nothing has been heard; the capture must not open the microphone after the hold ended); `sink.cancel()` | idle |
| requesting | event `permission` with a denial | wait for the matching error | requesting |
| requesting | event `error` | `sink.cancel()`; notice (`microphone_denied` / `speech_denied` / `unavailable` for `recognizer_unavailable`, `locale_unsupported`, `on_device_unavailable` (with the Dictation-languages pointer), `helper_crashed`, `helper_failed` / `failed` otherwise) | idle |
| requesting | event `started` | record `locale` | listening |
| listening | event `partial` | `sink.partial(text)`; `interim = text` | listening |
| listening | event `final` (Apple ended the task itself: "no speech detected" after a long silence settles as a final before any `stop`) | `sink.final(text)`; the hold's later release finds no capture and does nothing | idle |
| listening | `release('keyup')` | `voice.stop`; arm a 5 s timer | finishing |
| listening | `release('lost')` | `voice.cancel`; `sink.cancel()` | idle |
| finishing | event `final` | `sink.final(text)` | idle |
| finishing | 5 s timer | `sink.final(interim)`; `voice.cancel` | idle |
| finishing | `release('lost')` | nothing (the stop is already on its way; the final still lands) | finishing |
| finishing | event `error` | `sink.final(interim)` (the words already on screen are kept, the recognizer's failure cost only the correction a final would have brought); notice `failed` with the message | idle |
| requesting, listening or finishing | the sink reports it ended (`onEnded`: the editor unmounted, was disabled, was covered by a card, its document was replaced, the human edited inside the interim text, or a submission settled it; Design §7) | `voice.cancel`; the sink is already settled or cleared by the editor, so no `sink.*` call | idle |

The controller binds a capture to the session and the sink it got at `press`; a late event never reaches whichever composer is focused when it arrives.

### 7. The composer's seam: begin, partial, final, cancel

`FocusTarget` (`src/renderer/src/stores/composer.ts`) gains `dictation?: { begin(onEnded: () => void): DictationSink | null }`. `Composer.tsx` supplies it from the editor handle and refuses (returns null) while a card covers the box (`docked`), while the editor is read-only (`editorDisabled !== null`) or while a submission is in flight; an unstarted session's composer **accepts** dictation (unlike the engine's `prompt.fill`, which refuses setup sessions for its own reasons), since dictating a first prompt is the common case.

`EditorHandle` (`controller.ts`) gains `beginDictation(onEnded): DictationSink | null`, which the controller implements over a ProseMirror plugin, `editor/dictation-plugin.ts`, that is the single owner of the interim range:

- Plugin state is `{ from, to, settledByEdit: false } | null`. A transaction carrying meta `dictation` sets it (`{from,to}` or null). Any other transaction that changes the document is classified against the range: if any of its steps **intersects** the range (a step map range `oldStart < to && oldEnd > from`, or an insertion with `oldStart === oldEnd` strictly inside `(from, to)`), the human is editing the provisional words themselves, and the plugin state becomes `{ …, settledByEdit: true }` with the range dropped to null on the next transaction (the interim text stays in the document as ordinary text, exactly as the human sees it, and the edit applies on top of it unchanged); otherwise the range is mapped through `tr.mapping` with `from` mapped with assoc `1` and `to` with assoc `-1`, so text the human types exactly at either edge lands outside the range (`to < from` collapses to `from`). Its `decorations` draw `Decoration.inline(from, to, { class: 'text-fg-muted' })` while `to > from`, which is the interim style (tokens only; no italics, Codex's and Claude Desktop's dictation both grey the provisional text).
- `beginDictation`: refused (null) when `busy`, `disabled`, or no editor; otherwise the editor takes focus (at its selection when focused, at the end otherwise, as `insertText` does), the range is set collapsed at the selection's head (`selection.to`), **normalised into a text block**: a selection that ends at a document or block boundary (ProseMirror's `AllSelection` after Select All, a node selection) resolves to the nearest inline position inside the last text block at or before it (`Selection.near(doc.resolve(pos), -1)` or an equivalent that lands in a textblock), so an insertion never creates a wrapper paragraph the recorded range would miss; and **nothing is deleted**: dictating with text selected inserts after the selection and never replaces it, so a refused or cancelled capture (missing helper, denial, early release, blur) leaves the document exactly as it was. A leading space is decided once: when the character before `from` exists in the same text block and is not whitespace (`doc.textBetween(from - 1, from)`, an atom counting as a non-space character), every inserted text is prefixed with one space. No trailing space is added (the next partial would have to strip it; the human types the next character).
- `partial(text)`: one transaction, `addToHistory: false`, `replaceWith(from, to, inlineContent(prefixed(text)))` using the controller's existing `inlineContent` (lines joined by hard breaks), meta `dictation: { from, to: from + size }`, selection at the range's end. Not in history, so undo never steps through provisional words; the history plugin maps its stored steps through these transactions. A partial that arrives after the range was settled by an edit is ignored (the sink has ended).
- `final(text)`: two synchronous transactions. The first, `addToHistory: false`, deletes `[from, to)` and clears the meta. The second, history-recorded and preceded by `closeHistory`, inserts `inlineContent(prefixed(text))` at `from` and sets the selection after it; an empty final skips the second. The intermediate state is never painted (same task). Result: ⌘Z after a dictation removes the whole dictated text in one step and never restores a partial. The alternative, replacing the last partial with the final in history, would make undo restore the last partial; rejected.
- `cancel()`: one transaction, `addToHistory: false`, deleting `[from, to)` and clearing the meta.
- `settle()` (the controller's own, not on the sink): the interim text becomes ordinary text where it stands (meta `dictation: null`, the range dropped) and the sink ends. Used by `submit()` **before** it serialises or snapshots the document, so what the human sees when pressing Enter (or Send) during `listening` or `finishing` is exactly what is sent, and a failed submission writes that same document to the draft; the dictation controller then cancels the capture on main through `onEnded`. The alternative, waiting for the final before sending, would make Enter asynchronous and hold the send for up to 5 s; cancelling would send words the human could see vanish. Text settled this way or by an edit inside the range is not undoable as one dictation step (the partials were never recorded); the human's own edit undoes as usual.
- The sink ends itself (calling `onEnded`) when the controller enters `submit()` (settle, above), `setDisabled(reason)`, `setDoc` (a prefill, a restored draft, a recall), a card covers the box (`EditorHandle.settleDictation()`, called by the Composer when it docks a card), the plugin reports `settledByEdit`, or `mount`'s cleanup. In every case but unmount the words on screen are **settled**, not removed (the human never loses words they could see, and what a card or a disabled editor hides is still there when it returns); unmount removes them before the draft writer's flush, so a stored draft never holds provisional words (M3 executor's refinement of the authoring draft, which cancelled in those cases; Decision Log). `onEnded` is how the dictation controller learns to cancel the capture on main. Once ended, the sink's `partial`, `final` and `cancel` are no-ops.
- Every dictation transaction goes through Tiptap's `onUpdate`, so `updated()` bumps `revision` and feeds the debounced draft writer: interim text that happens to be on screen when a draft write fires is written and corrected by the next; after `final` the draft holds the settled text. Acceptable, and simpler than suspending the writer.

## Residue decisions (for the owner to confirm at hand-back)

1. **Default chord `Alt+Space`, app-scoped**: hold it anywhere in the window; the words go to the focused pane's composer. Rebindable in Settings › Keyboard; a bare key is refused for it.
2. **Recognition language defaults to the system locale** (`ko-KR` on this Mac); Settings › Voice changes it. If English prompts are the norm, the owner sets `en-US` once.
3. **A language with no on-device model on this Mac is refused** (the notice points at System Settings › Keyboard › Dictation, where macOS downloads them); nothing is ever sent to Apple's servers. The alternative, Apple's server path for those languages, was rejected because it would break the "nothing leaves the Mac" promise of G2 and A-6.
4. **Interim text is grey and replaced by the final; one ⌘Z removes the whole dictation; a leading space is added after a word or a chip, no trailing space; dictating with text selected inserts after it, never over it; typing inside the grey words, or pressing Enter before the final lands, keeps the words as they stand and ends the capture.**

6. **Settings version 9 is used** for `voice.locale` (the ledger's provisional allocation stands).
7. **The helper stays warm for 10 minutes after the last capture**, then exits until the next hold.
8. **Under the e2e harness the real helper is probe-only**: it is launched to prove the bundle but never opens the microphone.
