## Purpose

P1 made MAWS the owner's whole workstation: the agent gained tools it can call (`maws`), places to put things (deliverables, artifacts), a channel the human answers in place (the ask sheet, comments and side threads), a browser the two share, an editor, source control, plugin management, voice, and sessions that outlive the app. Each child proved its own gates. What no child could prove is the whole: that the thirteen items of the parent's §2 hold together on the owner's Mac on one head, that the seams between children (the contracts X9 to X15, the host boundary after E10, the browser's trust boundary after E4, replay parity across every new row kind) carry no defect a per-child review structurally could not see (A-14; P0's lesson at charter §20), that the debt the children logged is either paid or deliberately carried, and that the three gaps P0 carried forward (the panel and reference taste sitting, the native terminal hands, the scale review) close rather than roll on again.

After E11 the owner can open the development-signed build on their own Mac and run one ordered checklist in which every §2 item is observed, with the agents having already run everything an agent can run under a scratch config home and recorded it; the P1 tree has had one independent review at the frontier with its findings fixed; the tracker has no row without a disposition; and the parent's §15 and the charter's P1 contracts are written from a record, not from memory.

To see it working: `docs/doperpowers/evidence/p1-e11/` holds `triage.txt` (every tracker row's bin), `gates/` (the final head's verify chain and every e2e group), `scale-review/` (five panel results and the routing of each finding), `prerun.txt` (§2 items 1 to 12, each with its evidence pointer), the live sittings' logs and the fixtures they recorded (admitted to the conformance suite), `owner-script.txt` and `owner-results.txt`, and `recomposition.txt` (the §2 checklist with verdicts). ## Constraints

Binding on the executor and every worker it dispatches.

1. `~/.claude` and `~/.claude.json` are read-only to every process E11 starts. Live runs only under a scratch `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, only through the relay, only on Haiku (every helper that hard-wires another model, `liveSpec`'s Sonnet default, `agent-tools-live.spec.ts`'s parent, `ptyEngine`'s and `printRun`'s `--model sonnet`, is overridden or given a model argument before use), only within the cap this document states per sitting (Design §5), with the A-15 check before and after each sitting and its two readings in the sitting's log. `MAWS_LIVE=1` is set only inside a sitting's command, with the file named, never for a whole `vitest run`. No GUI automation of any kind (no cua, no desktop input); Playwright drives only MAWS's own windows. Every process a sitting starts is stopped before the sitting ends, proved by a survivor check (the `ps` poll of `e2e/supervisor.spec.ts`'s `Scratch` cleanup; `host.stopAll` for a supervisor; `session.stop` for engines), and the scratch root is removed unless a finding needs it.
2. Heavy commands (the unit suite, `electron-vite build`, `build:app`, any `e2e:*`, a live run) only as `/tmp/maws-review/waitload20.sh && /tmp/maws-review/with-slot.sh <cmd>`, with `pnpm_config_verify_deps_before_run=false` exported; `pnpm -C <worktree>` and `git -C <worktree>`, never `cd`. At most four workers at once including reviewers (A-13).
3. Commits are fresh, never amended or reset; no attribution footer of any kind (no `Co-Authored-By`, no "Generated with", no session URL), not overridable, passed to every subagent. One commit per tracker row in the debt waves, the row's removal in the same commit. The executor never edits the parent spec, the ledger, a sibling's document or `docs/charter.md`; it edits `docs/tech-debt-tracker.md` (the triage is its act), this document (Progress, pins, the record) and the evidence directory. The only new `.md` file is none: evidence is `.txt`, `.json`, `.png`, `.jsonl`.
4. Reviews: every wave gets a `doperpowers:task-reviewer` on Opus (A-22), two rounds at most, tiny leftovers to the tracker; the scale review is the panel at `max`, handed up (Design §4); findings are fixed by workers in waves, never inline by the executor. The review-rung rule of `review-code`'s findings doctrine holds: real but tiny → tracker; contradicts design intent → dismissed with the reason in the Decision Log; deliberately out of scope → Deferred.
5. The engine is unmodified (D-1). A wire claim is verified against a fixture or a new probe, never the spec library alone. A fixture whose recording contradicts a child's reading of the wire is a `[parent-impact]` line in the hand-back, not a code change here.
6. Gate runs rewrite committed evidence unconditionally: `e2e/code-editor.spec.ts:318-327` writes `evidence/p1-e5/shots/` (`e2e:code`), `e2e/side.spec.ts:669-674` writes `evidence/p1-e3/shots/` (`e2e:side`, and also plain `e2e` and `e2e:packaged`, since `side.spec.ts` is not in `testIgnore`), `e2e/supervisor.spec.ts:325-341` and `supervisor-packaged.spec.ts:121-123` write `evidence/p1-e10/` (the other children gate theirs behind `MAWS_E<n>_SHOTS`). E1's live spec writes `evidence/p1-e1/live-sitting.txt` and its shots unconditionally too (`maws-tools-live.spec.ts:34-35, 232`). Until W-E puts these behind flags, every gate or sitting script ends with `git -C <worktree> checkout -- docs/doperpowers/evidence/p1-e1 docs/doperpowers/evidence/p1-e3 docs/doperpowers/evidence/p1-e5 docs/doperpowers/evidence/p1-e10` (and any other directory a run is found to rewrite), after copying what E11 needs into `p1-e11/`, and never restores `p1-e11`; the executor commits its own evidence before a gate runs and commits nothing from a sibling's evidence directory. A gate script is copied per run and never edited while it runs (zsh reads incrementally; E10's final driver lost a step to an edit mid-run).
7. Budget for engine usage across the whole child: US$5.00, per-sitting caps in Design §5; a sitting that would exceed its cap is not started and is recorded `not run (budget)`.

### 8. Failure semantics

- **Relay outage.** The gate (`GET /api/hello`) fails: wait two minutes and retry once (the children's rule; the vitest harness then skips, `live.ts:222-223`, and the probe's `relayReady` throws on anything but a 503, `p1-common.mjs:37-44`); either outcome is recorded `blocked: relay` in the sitting's log with the time, the executor moves to the next milestone work that needs no engine, and the sitting is retried at the next milestone boundary; never a direct route, never the owner's `~/.claude`. Three outages across M6 leave the remaining sittings `not run (relay)` in `prerun.txt` and the hand-back lists them for the architect to schedule.
- **Budget overrun.** A sitting's harness refuses to start a case whose cap is spent (`MAWS_P1_LIVE_MAX_USD` for the app spec; the per-session `maxBudgetUsd` and the sitting's own sum for the harness cases); a sitting that hits its cap stops after the running case, records the spend and what it proved, and the remaining cases are `not run (budget)`. The child's US$5.00 is never exceeded: the executor sums `prerun.txt` before each sitting and skips any whose cap would cross it, naming the skip.
- **A fixture that will not reproduce** (S1's notification never sent; S2(d)'s team never formed; S2(c)'s terminal never receiving): three attempts at most for S1 with the documented variation (the interrupt's moment: after `maws.ask.opened` with a longer wait, or during the model's streaming before the call; `session.interrupt` is the only client-side interrupt, `control_cancel_request` being an engine-to-host frame), one for S2(d) (the SPEC reading says it either forms or it does not); then the negative is recorded with the journal, the engine version and the attempts, the code path's unit proof is cited, and the item becomes a tracker row re-deferred as "no live reproduction on <engine version>", never a fabricated fixture and never a fixture edited by hand.
- **A pre-run item fails on the final head.** The defect is fixed in a follow-up wave (M6's wave F, `task-executor` plus `task-reviewer`), the affected gate groups re-run (Design §3's rule), the sitting re-run, and the head re-pinned; a defect that needs a child's design decision is a `[parent-impact]` line and the item stays `gap`.
- **A panel returns `interrupted` or a pin moves.** Design §4: re-pin the pair and re-run once; then a `reviewer-high` read of the same pair.
- **The owner's sitting finds a defect.** Small: M7's wave F2 fixes it, gates re-run by the rule, head re-pinned. Large or product-level: a tracker row or a P-n question; the item's verdict is `gap` or `met as restated` with the owner's words.
- **The load rule holds a gate.** `waitload20.sh` gives up after twenty minutes at a load of 40 or more: the executor records the load and retries at the next boundary; no gate runs outside the slot.
