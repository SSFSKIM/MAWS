## Purpose

After this child the right panel is a place to work, not only a column beside the session.

- **The floating card.** The owner drags the session/panel divider left past the panel's widest size, and the session becomes a card at the panel's bottom-left: the session's transcript, its docked request, its composer, a header with the session's title, Dock (expand) and Close. The panel takes the whole width beside the sidebar. ⌥⌘B or the header's Float button does the same and undoes it. Close leaves a small pill with the session's title at the bottom-left, with the unread dot when the turn ended while the card was closed; a click or ⌥⌘B brings the card back. Dock returns the panel to its column, and the composer has the keyboard. The card is resized by its top and right edges and remembers its size per panel tab. Over a Browser tab the card is a full-height column at the pane's left instead: the page stays live beside it (never frozen to a still), and the column is resized from its right edge only.
- **The header.** The empty bar above the panel's tool strip becomes Claude Desktop's pane header: the open tool's title (an editor's breadcrumb and title), an editor's saved state ("All changes saved", "Saving…", …), its open-comment count, its actions and its Export menu, Mark up where the surface allows it, and the region's own controls (Float, Maximize, Hide). Editors (P3-L, B, C, D, E) register these through one contract, X32, the same in the panel and in a main tab; today's tools register nothing and show their title, so nothing they do changes.
- **Mark-up.** On a browser page (and on any surface that registers, the deliverable viewer now, documents and slides when P3-B and P3-C land) Mark up freezes the surface to a still and lays a drawing overlay over it with Pen, Box, Arrow and Text in five inks; Add to chat composites the still and the marks into one PNG and attaches it to the session's composer with the surface's label; Escape discards. The page or artifact is never changed.
- **The Artifacts entry.** The sidebar gains an Artifacts row under New chat that opens a singleton Artifacts main tab, drawn by a slot P3-L fills; until then it shows a quiet placeholder.

To see it working once landed: `pnpm -C <worktree> dev` (never against the owner's running MAWS or user data; the e2e groups below launch scratch instances), open a session, open a Browser tab in the right panel and drag the divider left until the card appears.

What does not change: the engine and the wire (D-1); the browser's chrome (the cua session's); the comment card (X21) and its dock; every existing tool's body; the Overview; `main.tabs`'s version (the new tab kind is additive, §D5).

## Terms

- **Docked, maximized, collapsed, floating**: the panel's four states. Docked is the column beside the main area; maximized and collapsed are C5's; **floating** is this child's third non-docked state: the panel takes the main column's place as maximized does and the shown session is drawn by the card.
- **Shown session**: the focused main pane's active tab's session (`shownSession()` in `src/renderer/src/panel/host.ts`), the session the panel already follows.
- **Card**: the floating session card. **Pill**: what Close leaves. **Column**: the card's layout over a Browser tab (§D1.4).
- **Editor host registration** (X32): what a tool or main-tab body tells the header about itself (§D2).
- **Mark-up surface**: something a still can be taken of and drawn over, registered per panel or main tab (§D4).
- **Still**: the captured picture of a surface a mark-up session draws over.

## Design of the residue

The parent decides what the card is, where it lives over a Browser tab, the header contract's fields and Mark-up's behaviour. This section decides the rest: the state model and its transitions, layouts, sizes, copy, the chord, the contract's exact TypeScript, and the capture routes. Each decision carries its reason; the alternative that lost is named where one was weighed.

### D1. The floating state

**D1.1 The model (X30).** `regions` version 4 (`src/shared/ipc/schema/layout.ts`):

```ts
const PanelRegionSchema = z.strictObject({ width: z.number().int(), collapsed: z.boolean(), maximized: z.boolean(), floating: z.boolean() });
export const FloatingCardSizeSchema = z.strictObject({ w: z.number().int(), h: z.number().int() });
export const LayoutRegionsSchema = z.strictObject({ version: z.literal(4),
  sidebar: /* unchanged */, panel: PanelRegionSchema, overview: OverviewRectSchema.nullable(),
  floatingCard: z.record(z.string(), FloatingCardSizeSchema) });   // key: the panel tab the card sits over (§D1.5)
```

Version 3's value migrates by adding `floating: false` and `floatingCard: {}`; the existing v2 and v1 transforms produce v4 directly (their old panel shape has no `floating`, so they need their own v-old panel schema; the executor keeps one `PanelRegionV3Schema` for the old shapes). `LAYOUT_DEFAULTS.regions` becomes version 4. The ledger's slot `regions 4` (X15) is taken; `main.tabs` keeps version 2 (§D5).

Invariants: `floating` and `maximized` are never both true; `floating` implies `!collapsed`. Every writer goes through the store's `setPanel`, which enforces them (a patch setting one of `floating`/`maximized` true clears the other; a patch setting `collapsed: true` clears both).

Not persisted: whether the card is closed to its pill. A launch with `floating: true` shows the card open. Reason: the pill is a moment's choice ("out of my way for now"); restoring a window to a pill hides the session behind a chip the person may not remember choosing. It lives in a small renderer store (`src/renderer/src/shell/floating.ts`, `useFloatingCard`): `{closed: boolean, column: ColumnReservation | null}`; `closed` resets to false whenever `floating` changes.

**D1.2 Transitions.** One table; the action handlers live with C5's in `src/renderer/src/app/shell-actions.ts`.

| Event | docked | maximized | collapsed | floating, card open | floating, pill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `panel.float` (⌥⌘B, the header's Float) | floating | floating | floating | docked | card open |
| `panel.maximize` | maximized | docked | maximized | maximized | maximized |
| `panel.toggle` (⇧⌘B, Hide) | collapsed | collapsed | docked | collapsed | collapsed |
| divider dragged ≥ 40 px past the max | floating (width = the drag's start) | — | — | — | — |
| card's Dock | — | — | — | docked | — |
| card's Close / pill click | — | — | — | pill | card open |
| Overview opens; attention navigation reveals a session (`attention/navigation.ts`, `attention/anchor.ts`) | — | docked | — | docked | docked |

The last row: every call site that today writes `{maximized: false}` to bring the main area back must also clear `floating` (the Overview lives over main; navigation shows a session's main pane). The executor finds every such site (the grep for `maximized: false` in `src/renderer/src`) and routes them through one store helper, `revealMain()`.

Docking (by the action, the button or the card's Dock) restores `width` unchanged: floating never writes the panel's width except the drag's restore value, so the column comes back as it was. Before docking, the dock path calls `markFocusOnMount(shown session)` (`src/renderer/src/stores/composer.ts`) so the main pane's remounted composer takes the keyboard (acceptance 4). Floating from any state focuses the card's composer once it registers (the same mark, set before floating).

**D1.3 Region math and X5.** `regionWidths` and `gridColumns` (`src/renderer/src/app/regions.ts`) treat `floating` as `maximized` (the panel stands in the main column; `sidebar 0px 1fr`); `Shell.tsx`'s `mainHidden` likewise. **X5's host prop:** `PanelRegion` passes `maximized={maximized || floating}` to the `panelHost` slot, so tools that adapt to a maximized panel see the same. `session/visibility.ts`: main panes are not visible while floating (as maximized); the card's session is visible while the card is open and the panel is not collapsed (read from `useFloatingCard` and the shown session), not while it is a pill, so the unread rule marks a turn that ends behind the pill. `DeliverableTab`'s on-screen rule (`!(maximized && !collapsed)`) gains floating too.

**D1.4 The card.** A Shell-level layer, rendered by `PanelRegion` as a sibling of its clipping content div (so `region-main`'s inertness never reaches it and the region's overflow does not clip it), absolutely positioned inside the panel region below the header (the card never covers the header or the tool strip), at `z` above the panel's body and below toasts, dialogs and popovers. Never `role="dialog"` and never a portal into `body`: the browser's cover rule (`tools/browser/cover.ts`) classifies body children and dialogs as covering, and a card that froze the page would defeat the owner's ruling; inside `#root` it is invisible to the cover watch. `role="region"`, `aria-label` "Session card".

- **Contents:** a 36 px header (the session's title, truncated, `text-ui font-semibold`; then Dock, an arrows-out icon labelled "Dock session" with the `panel.float` chord in its tooltip; then Close, an × labelled "Close session card"), then `<SessionPane appSessionId={shown} paneIndex={focusedPane}>` unchanged (it already stacks the report bar, the transcript, the docked request, `SessionTabNotes` and the composer; C8a measured it at 280 to 640 px).
- **Surface:** the floating Overview's tokens (`rounded-lg border border-border-strong bg-bg shadow-float`), the header `bg-bg-raised border-b border-border`, so the card reads as the Overview's sibling.
- **Size:** default 420 × min(60 % of the panel body's height, 640); minimum 360 × 240; clamped inside the panel body less a 16 px margin; anchored 16 px from the panel's left and bottom edges (the seed's numbers, Claude Docs' chat popover). Resized by its top edge, its right edge and the top-right corner (pointer capture, as the Overview's resize); never moved. The size is written on pointer-up to `regions.floatingCard[<panel tab key>]`.
- **With no shown session** (the focused tab is Settings, Diagnostics, empty): no card and no pill; the panel stays full-width; ⌥⌘B docks.
- **One composer per session** (acceptance 5): the main area's `TabContent` (`src/renderer/src/main/Pane.tsx`) renders an empty element instead of `SessionPane` for the session tab whose session the card shows while `floating` is on (the main region is at 0 px and inert, so nothing is lost on screen). A split's other pane keeps its own session mounted (another session). The card is the only mount of the shown session.
- **The pill:** bottom-left of the panel body at the same 16 px margins; a 28 px-tall chip (`rounded-full border border-border-strong bg-bg-raised shadow-float px-3 text-ui-sm`) with the session's title truncated at 220 px and, when the session's record is `unread`, the sidebar's dot (`size-[5px] rounded-full bg-info`, `aria-label` "Unread", `src/renderer/src/sidebar/SessionRow.tsx`'s). `aria-label` "Show session card".

**D1.5 The size key.** `floatingCard` is keyed by the selected panel tab's id (`PanelTabRef.id`), or `'summary'` when the panel shows its resting summary. Reason: the card's geometry belongs to what it sits over (a column over a Browser tab holds a width only; over a viewer it is a w × h card), which is the panel tab; keying by the main tab would carry a browser column's width onto a viewer. The seed's alternative (on the main tab record) lost for the same reason and because the ledger fixed it in `regions`. Entries whose tab id is in no panel list are dropped at the next write; at most 64 entries (oldest first out).

**D1.6 The trigger on the divider.** `Resizer` (`src/renderer/src/app/Resizer.tsx`) gains the mirror of `collapseThreshold`: `expandThreshold?: number | null` and `onExpand?(restore: number)`, fired when a drag's raw value reaches `max + expandThreshold` (the drag ends there, as a collapse does). `PanelRegion` passes `expandThreshold={COLLAPSE_THRESHOLD_PX}` and `onExpand={(restore) => setPanel({ floating: true, width: restore })}`. The keyboard never floats (as it never collapses). The sidebar's resizer passes nothing.

**D1.7 The chord.** `panel.float`: title "Float session over panel", chord **`Cmd+Alt+B`**, `sink: 'menu'`, `menu: 'view'`, `scope: 'app'`. Reason: the B family already toggles the regions (⌘B the sidebar, ⇧⌘B the panel), so ⌥⌘B reads as "the third region state"; it is free in the action table, and the browser's in-page key handler (`src/main/browser/keys.ts`, `if (!meta || control || alt) return null`) passes every ⌥⌘ chord to the menu, so it works with a page focused, unlike ⌘L and ⇧⌘B which the page shadows. Alternatives weighed: ⌘. and ⌘\ are taken; an unbound default would contradict the owner's "a chord toggles it". The palette lists it through the action table; `composer/mentions/items.ts` gains its entry beside `panel.maximize`'s.

**D2.3 The contract** (`src/renderer/src/panel/editor-host.ts`, X32):

```ts
export type SavedState = 'saved' | 'saving' | 'unsaved' | 'reconnecting' | 'readonly';
export interface EditorHostAction { id: string; label: string; icon?: ComponentType<{ className?: string }>; onSelect(): void; disabled?: boolean; pressed?: boolean }
export interface EditorHostExport { id: string; label: string; onSelect(): void; disabled?: boolean }
export interface EditorHostRegistration {
  title: string;
  breadcrumb?: readonly string[];
  saved?: SavedState | null;            // absent or null: no indicator
  unresolved?: number;                  // 0 or absent: no count
  actions?: readonly EditorHostAction[];   // version history, Open in tab, comments, …
  exports?: readonly EditorHostExport[];
  closeGuard?: (() => Promise<boolean>) | null;   // false keeps the tab open (the editor shows its own confirmation)
}
export type EditorHostScope = { surface: 'panel'; tabId: string } | { surface: 'main'; tabId: string };
export interface EditorHostHandle { update(patch: Partial<EditorHostRegistration>): void; dispose(): void }
export function registerEditorHost(scope: EditorHostScope, host: EditorHostRegistration): EditorHostHandle;
export function useEditorHost(scope: EditorHostScope | null, host: EditorHostRegistration): void;  // registers on mount, updates on change, disposes on unmount
export function useEditorHostScope(): EditorHostScope | null;   // from context: ToolBody provides the panel tab's, the main pane's TabContent the main tab's
```

One registration per scope: a second `registerEditorHost` for a scope replaces the first (a dev-mode warning), and the first handle's `update`/`dispose` become no-ops, so a remounting editor never clears its successor. **A registration lives as long as its tab, not its mounted body** (Decision Log, M3's review): `useEditorHost` does not dispose on unmount (only the active body is mounted, and an inactive tab's `closeGuard` must still veto); the host clears the scope when the panel instance is disposed or the main tab is closed or replaced. A guard and an action therefore read the tab's own state (X5: outside React), never a body's component state. The ledger's shape (`registerEditorHost({…})` returning an update handle) gains the leading `scope` argument: a tool's render does not otherwise know which tab it is in (§H1 proposes the ledger line). `PanelToolContext` gains `editorHost: EditorHostScope` (`{surface: 'panel', tabId}`) so a tool can register outside React too.

**Today's tools register a title only, by doing nothing:** the header reads `registration?.title ?? stripLabel(tool, titles[tabId])`, where the strip's label is today's `tool.title` and `setTitle` suffix (`"Terminal · claude"`); `ctx.setTitle` is unchanged and still feeds the strip. Reason: no existing tool changes, which is the parent's no-regression rule made structural; the alternative (each tool registering through X32) touches ten tools for no behaviour.

**closeGuard.** Panel: `panelHost.closeTab` (`host.ts`) awaits the registration's `closeGuard` before the tool's `beforeClose`; either false vetoes (the same `closing` bookkeeping). Main tabs: the main close path consults the main-tab scope's registration's `closeGuard` before the kind's guard (`keymap/actions-runtime.ts`'s `closeGuards`, `registerCloseGuard`); reconciliation's dispose-without-guard path is unchanged.

**D2.4 Main tabs.** A main tab whose body registers draws the same header component (without the region's controls, and with no Float) as a 40 px row above its content inside the tab panel. A main tab with no registration draws nothing new, so today's main tabs are untouched. P3-L, B, C register from their main-tab bodies through `useEditorHostScope()`.

### D3. The column over a Browser tab

When the card is up (floating, card open, a shown session) and the panel's selected tab is a Browser tab, the browser pane reserves a left column instead of the card floating over it (the owner's ruling). Mechanism (X30's `reservedColumn`):

- `BrowserPane`'s placed pane (`src/renderer/src/tools/browser/Pane.tsx`, `PlacedPane`), when `surface === 'panel'` and the card is up, renders a left spacer `data-browser-reserved-column` of width `reservedColumn` beside its existing column, so the page element (what `Placement` measures, `paneRect`) excludes it. `reservedColumn` = the stored `floatingCard[<this panel tab>].w`, default 420, clamped so the page keeps at least 280 px.
- The pane registers the spacer element with `useFloatingCard` (`reserveColumn(element) → release`) while it owns the view (`mode === 'owner'`); the card reads the reservation and, while one exists, lays itself over the spacer's rect (full pane height, from its top), resizable from the right edge only (writing `w`). Without a reservation the card floats (§D1.4).
- Nothing new tells main anything: the page element shrinks or moves, and the existing reports (the ResizeObserver; the layout subscription on every `regions` change, which floating and a width write are) send the new rect. The card is DOM inside `#root`, so the cover watch never sees it and `covered` stays false (acceptance 6).
- A Browser **main** tab is under the main region, at 0 px while floating; it reserves nothing.

The alternative (one column reserved by the panel host for any tool that declares a native view) lost: the ledger names the browser pane's `reservedColumn`, and the browser is the only native-view surface; a second one can adopt `reserveColumn` the same way.

### D4. Mark-up mode

**D4.1 Shape.** One generic overlay over any registered surface, with two capture routes:

```ts
// src/renderer/src/markup/surface.ts
export interface CapturedImage { mediaType: 'image/png' | 'image/jpeg'; data: string /* base64 */; width: number; height: number }
export interface MarkupSurface {
  element(): HTMLElement | null;     // the region the overlay covers, in the window's CSS px
  label(): string;                   // the anchor's label, e.g. `"<page title>" — <url>`
  begin(): Promise<CapturedImage | null>;   // freeze: take the still (and, the browser, hold the cover); null = cannot mark up now
  end(): void;                       // unfreeze (release the cover)
}
export function registerMarkupSurface(scope: EditorHostScope, surface: MarkupSurface): () => void;
export function captureWindowRegion(rect: PaneRect): Promise<CapturedImage | null>;   // the DOM route, for surfaces drawn by the renderer
```

The still is taken **before** the overlay draws, so both routes capture the surface alone, and the marks are composited in the renderer afterwards. Reason: the window's own `capturePage` would otherwise capture the overlay's chrome, and a native view cannot be drawn over at all; a still for both keeps one compositing path.

- **The browser route.** The Browser tool registers its panel tab's surface: `begin` invokes a new command **`browser.view.capture {tabId}`** (main: `webContents.capturePage()` of the tab's view at device pixels, through `encodeCrop`'s caps in `src/main/browser/comment/crop.ts`: the longest edge at most 1568, PNG then JPEG, at most 3.5 MiB; answered `{image: CapturedImage | null}`; refused for a tab this window does not own), then `holdCover()` (`tools/browser/covering.ts`), which hides the view and shows its placeholder under the overlay; `end` releases the hold. `capturePage` works while the view is hidden (crop.ts's own note), so the order is safe either way.
- **The DOM route.** New command **`layout.captureRegion {rect}`** (main: the sender window's `webContents.capturePage(rect)`, same caps, `{image}`), for surfaces the renderer draws (iframes included, which the compositor paints). The deliverable viewer registers through it now, the proving consumer and the owner's HTML artifacts' Mark-up; P3-B and P3-C register their editors the same way. **P3-L carries the deliverable viewer's registration onto its viewers** (§H5).
- Both commands are in `src/shared/ipc/schema/` (browser and layout namespaces) with zod schemas and tests of the handler's caps and refusals.

**D4.2 The overlay** (`src/renderer/src/markup/MarkupLayer.tsx`, mounted once in `Shell`, inside `#root`): over the surface element's rect, the still as an `<img>` filling it, an SVG drawing layer above it, and a toolbar floating at the bottom centre 10 px above the surface's bottom edge (Claude's annotate toolbar, `c467aa787-C-G5f3et.js:2585-3003`, `cf550b864-BbtT5pW3.js:55`).

- **Tools** (a radio group, "Mark-up tool"): Pen (freehand polyline), Box (rectangle outline), Arrow (line with a head), Text (click to place a text field; Enter or a click elsewhere commits; Escape cancels the field only). Default: Pen.
- **Inks** (a radio group, "Ink colour", 14 px swatches): Red `#E03131` (default), Blue `#1971C2`, Green `#2F9E44`, Black `#1F1E1D`, White `#FFFFFF` (Claude's five). They mark content, not chrome, so they are the same in both themes; they live as tokens `--markup-ink-{red,blue,green,black,white}` in `src/renderer/src/styles/tokens.css` (the tokens-only rule). Stroke 4 px, text 16 px semibold with a 2 px halo of the opposite ink for legibility.
- **Controls:** Undo ("Nothing to undo yet" disabled), Clear ("Nothing to clear yet"), Cancel (= Escape), and the primary **Add to chat** (Claude's label; the parent's "Done"). Enabled without marks: an unmarked still is a screenshot, which is useful.
- **Escape** (outside a text field) discards everything and ends; so do Cancel, a tab switch, the surface unmounting, and the panel collapsing.
- The overlay's toolbar and the still are DOM inside `#root`, never a body child, so the browser's cover watch is untouched (the page is already held covered by `begin`).

**D4.3 Add to chat.** Composite the still at its pixel size with the marks scaled by `still.width / rect.width` on an `OffscreenCanvas`; encode PNG (falling back to JPEG 0.85 over 3.5 MiB); deliver to the **shown session**: when its composer is mounted (`focusTargets`), through a new optional `FocusTarget.attachFiles(files: File[])` that calls the controller's existing `addFiles` (`src/renderer/src/composer/editor/controller.ts`; it already downscales and caps images), with the file named `mark-up.png`, then `insertText("Mark-up of " + label + " ")` and `focusEditor()`; when none is mounted, `prefillComposer(shown, {text, images: [{name: 'mark-up.png', mediaType, data}]})`, which the composer takes when it mounts. The label is the surface's `label()`: for a browser page `"<title>" — <url>` (the title omitted when empty), for a deliverable its record's title. With no shown session, Mark up is disabled ("Open a session to mark up").

**D4.4 Entry.** The header's Mark up button (§D2.2) for the selected panel tab when it has a surface; a new action `markup.start` ("Mark up", chord null, `sink: 'renderer'`, `menu: null`, scope app) for the palette. Mark-up in a main tab is not wired in this child (no header there without a registration; §H6).

- Decision (controller, on M3's review): **a registration belongs to the tab, not to the mounted body.** `useEditorHost` keeps its registration when the body unmounts. Both hosts mount only the active body, so a tab switch had dropped the inactive editor's `closeGuard`, and closing that tab (Delete in the strip, ⌘W on a pinned list, `closeTab`) skipped the veto. The host clears a scope's registration only when the tab itself goes: in the panel when the instance is disposed, in main when the tab is closed or replaced. A remounting body's hook replaces the registration (one per scope). §D2.3 is revised to match. Consequence for siblings: a guard or action must not close over a body's component state. It reads the tab's own state, which by X5's rule already lives outside React (the tool's `S`, or the editor's document store), so a guard that outlives its body still answers truly. Rejected: re-mounting inactive bodies (memory and scale), and asking each editor to register imperatively at tab creation (two paths for one contract).
  Date/Author: 2026-10-12, P3-G controller.
- Decision (M3 fix wave, abf3c9b8):
  - **The withdrawal rule.** A mounted body withdraws its registration only by passing a null scope, or a different scope, to `useEditorHost`. Unmounting withdraws nothing.
  - **Remount.** A remount of the same tab's body replaces the registration quietly. The dev warning fires only when a registration replaces one whose body is still mounted, or one made directly.
  - **Clearing in the panel.** A panel registration is cleared only in `disposeInstance`.
  - **Clearing in main.** A main tab's registration is cleared when the tab leaves main by any path. This runs through one layout subscription in `editor-host.ts` that compares open tab ids across panes and project sets.
  - **Narrow threshold.** The threshold is measured from the header row's `offsetWidth`, and an unmeasured 0 px counts as wide. Under it, exports fold into "More actions" under an "Export" heading.
  Rationale: implements the tab-lifetime ruling with one clearing path per host.
