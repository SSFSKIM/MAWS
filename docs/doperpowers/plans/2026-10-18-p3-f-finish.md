### Wave contract

One fresh `doperpowers:task-executor` per wave in W; checkpoint 1 permits two waves at once only with disjoint file lists, each with its reviewer inside the four-worker cap. For disjoint product paths, each tracker edit plus staging only that wave’s exact paths and the tracker plus commit is one atomic `lockf /private/tmp/maws-p3f-commit.lock` operation; other code edits stay outside the lock. An occupied index or mismatched frozen row stops rather than absorbs another wave’s changes; one commit per row, including its removal or narrowing. A brief quotes the frozen full row and closing clause, gives the ruled bin, relevant contract and named gate. The executor owns implementation and tests; F owns scheduling and its document.
