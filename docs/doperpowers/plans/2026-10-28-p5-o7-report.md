## What was built

- **The link** (`src/main/machines/remote/link.ts`). One RL2 channel per machine. It runs the six-state machine of the document's tables.
  - It tries the direct carrier first: tailnet `ws://` addresses raced within 1 s, under a bucket that mirrors the listener's limit. Otherwise it takes the hub relay, one app socket per link.
  - It switches back to direct on the 60 s recheck or on a probe.
  - It keeps `up` through a 2 s grace, and backs off at 0/1/2/4/5 s, then 30 s past the resume window, with ±20% jitter.
  - Liveness matches the endpoint's `#arm`.
  - A trust generation closes a channel whose machine key the org retired.
  - `call`, `act` and `subscribe` work, and so do `caps`, `probe` and `rename`.
- **Acts** (`acts.ts`). RL4's whole opId lifecycle:
  - build, then optional step-up (`required` / `if-asked` / `never`);
  - ack, done, reseal (only after a lost channel: unsigned, unacked, within the window), query, tombstone;
  - the ambiguity rule: no refusal settles an act that may have run, and only the ledger's query answer does;
  - an `isAction` pre-check refuses a malformed act locally.
- **Subscriptions** (`subs.ts`):
  - stable ids across channels;
  - the cursor (a snapshot moves it only at its last page);
  - page assembly, and dropping a repeated event;
  - credit returned in batches, each split at `SUB_CREDIT_MAX`;
  - `busy` past 64 subscriptions;
  - `paused('offline')` once the grace has passed.
- **Carriers** (`carriers.ts`):
  - `dialDirect` and `raceDirect`; a tailnet address filter (IPv4 100.64/10, IPv6 fd7a:115c:a1e0::/48, `ws:` only);
  - `openRelaySocket`, the production relay multiplex;
  - every transport end reported as `carrier`.
- **Endpoint records** (`endpoint-records.ts`):
  - fetched from the hub at each attempt with a 2 s timeout, and verified;
  - the newest one kept, cached in memory and in `<userData>/endpoint-records.json` (0600, written through a temporary file and a rename), and verified again when loaded.
- **The router path** (`dispatch.ts`):
  - `list`/`view` commands go as calls, their output checked by the codec;
  - every other command is refused `act-required`;
  - errors map by §F.
- **The roster and the manager's second source** (`source.ts`, `manager.ts`):
  - the roster is the head's machines plus its live certificates, through `machineKeys`;
  - one eager link per listed machine;
  - the one-link-up-per-ref rule spans socket and RL2 links;
  - `machines.list` gains `carrier` and `refusal`;
  - a network watch probes every link when the address set changes.
- **The org session seam** (`src/main/org/session.ts`, `dev-session.ts`):
  - `signedOut()` in production;
  - `heldOrgSession` for tests;
  - `MAWS_DEV_ORG`, honoured in unpackaged builds only: it verifies the pinned chain and checks that the device is listed with this profile's key.
- **Composition** (`src/main/machines/index.ts`, `src/main/index.ts`):
  - `Machines.remote(ref)` and `onRemote`;
  - the wake, unlock and focus probes;
  - the session handed to `startMachines`;
  - the router's `remote` option left as the plain `machines.dispatch`, so that O9 M9 can wrap it.
- **Tests:**
  - unit suites under `src/main/machines/remote/`: link 26, acts 21, subs 12, carriers 5, dispatch 4, source 3, endpoint-records 3, timing 2, boundary 2;
  - `src/main/org/dev-session.test.ts` (6);
  - `src/main/machines/manager.test.ts` (16, 5 of them new);
  - `test/machines/remote-link.test.ts` (13): a real endpoint over the direct carrier, with the profile's real keys;
  - `test/machines/remote-link.local.test.ts` (4): desk-hub's Worker in Miniflare. It covers up over the relay; a relay socket cut with a subscription and a pending act open, where the act runs once and no event is lost or repeated; the probe's switch to direct; and the hub stopped plus a relaunch, after which the link dials direct from the cached file.

The red was a test race, not a link bug. `link.test.ts` holds the clock and lets real async work run for a fixed number of event-loop turns. An attempt computes key ids with WebCrypto's digest, which runs on libuv's thread pool. On a loaded runner that pool is shared, and the digest outlived those turns. I reproduced it locally by keeping the pool busy (`UV_THREADPOOL_SIZE=1` plus a pbkdf2 loop): the old suite failed 26 of 26 and the fixed one passed 26 of 26. The fix mocks `keyId` with the same digest computed synchronously. acts and subs passed under the same contention unchanged.
