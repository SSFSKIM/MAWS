**Residue for P5's Decision Log.** Each item names the alternative it rejected; the document's Decision Log has the full entries.
1. One org session per run. Under `MAWS_DEV_ORG` no production session exists. Rejected: both sessions alive, which means two feeds.
2. The fresh-token grace: up to 120 s of backoff on Access's refusal of a new token, and never on the Worker's `not-a-member`. Rejected: refusing at the first 401.

6. The chain is re-read from a recovery's `fromVersion`, or from genesis when the served head is below the held one. Rejected: `?since=<head>` alone, which refuses a genuine recovery as a rollback.
7. The held view's shape as built: files rather than a path, `certificatesChanged`, `highWater()`, and rollback/fork judged only when nothing was adopted. Rejected: a base the delivery carries.
8. The transfer's box key comes from WebCrypto, and the metadata `iat` check is not done. Rejected: tweetnacl's PRNG, and adding `iat` before staging shows Access sets it.
9. One serialized commit queue for every durable write and trust change. Rejected: per-write fences.
10. Hub answers are sanitized at the boundary (error codes, email, `expires`), and malformed certificate items are dropped. Rejected: forwarding response text, and failing a whole read for one bad certificate.
