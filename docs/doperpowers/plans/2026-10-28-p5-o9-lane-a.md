## Purpose

Most of the owner's sessions start in a terminal. Today MAWS shows such a session read-only while it is live: the transcript as the engine writes it to disk, a notice naming the terminal, and "Take over here", which ends the terminal process and resumes the session in MAWS. Every terminal `claude` the owner runs loads sminos's bridge mod, which can already send, interrupt, answer, approve and change the model of its session from outside. O9 puts that reach inside MAWS.

After O9:

- **A terminal session on this Mac is driven from MAWS, signed out, with no hub.** Its pane has the composer: send a message whether or not a turn runs, interrupt, set the model and effort, run a slash command, ask a side question, compact, toggle an MCP server, stop or message a subagent, stop a background shell, attach an image. Its permission prompts and questions are cards in the pane and rows in Needs you, answered in MAWS or in the terminal, whichever comes first. Its reply streams as it is written.
- **What the bridge can't reach is disabled in place, with its reason.** Permission mode, rewind, file rollback, an MCP server's form, a slash command whose effect is a terminal panel: each is a greyed control that says why on hover, never a control that fails after it is used.
- **A terminal session without the bridge stays as it is today**: read-only, with "Take over here".
- **A terminal session on another machine is driven the same way** from MAWS on the MacBook, through that machine's endpoint, once O7's link to other machines exists and O8's sign-in has put the app in the org.

**How to see it working.** Locally: in a terminal, export a scratch `SMINOS_HOME` and start `claude` in a scratch directory; start a build of this branch on a scratch profile with the same `SMINOS_HOME`; open that session. The composer is live. Type a message: the terminal shows it arrive as your words and the reply streams in both places. Ask for something that needs a permission: the card shows in MAWS and the dialog in the terminal; answer in either and both settle. Remotely, after the remote milestones: the same from the MacBook against a terminal session on the mini. Concrete Steps has the commands.

## Acceptance

Each item is checked by the command Concrete Steps names. "The fake bridge" is the DX5 test double of Interfaces §F. "The sitting" is M8's or M10's live check with the owner present.

1. **Local controls, signed out.** On a MAWS profile with no sign-in and no endpoint enrolled, and with no sminos daemon involved (`e2e:lane-a` runs none), a terminal session whose bridge answers can, from its MAWS pane:
   - be sent a message while idle (a turn starts with those words as the human's) and while its turn runs (the words are read at the turn's next model request); two messages sent back to back arrive in order; a message the session refuses to take shows as not entered, with its reason and its text kept for sending again;
   - be interrupted;
   - have its model and effort set, each on its own without resetting the other (the next `step start` names both);
   - run a slash command whose route is runnable (its `run` end shows its outcome);
   - take a side question (its answer shows), be compacted with instructions, have an MCP server enabled or reconnected;
   - have a running subagent stopped or told, and a background shell stopped.
   In `e2e:lane-a`, each control reaches the fake bridge with the exact body Interfaces §B gives; in the sitting, each works on a real terminal `claude`.
2. **Honest gaps.** Each control the bridge does not reach is disabled in place with its reason, none hidden: permission mode; rewind, forget and file rollback; deny-and-interrupt; a plan's "clear and implement"; an edited tool input on an approval; an MCP server's form (elicitation); MCP disable where the route table drops it; a slash command whose route is not runnable; the queue. `e2e:lane-a` asserts each one's disabled state and reason against the capability fixture.
3. **The approval race.** A permission prompt, a native question and a plan approval raised by a terminal session show as cards in its pane. Answered in MAWS, the card settles and the terminal's dialog closes. Answered in the terminal first, the card settles as answered there. A MAWS answer that arrives after the terminal's shows that settlement, never an error. A background subagent's request past its window reads as the terminal's to answer. `e2e:lane-a`; the sitting.
4. **No bridge, no change.** With no socket, a stale socket, or a socket path the bridge could not bind (over 103 bytes), the session is read-only exactly as today: the holder notice and "Take over here". With a bridge that answers but can't be driven (a lane B observer's verdict, a sminos whose `/status` carries no `capabilities.controls`, a bridge still starting), it is the same, and the notice adds one line with the reason. The foreign-live scenes of `e2e/sessions.spec.ts`, `e2e/shell.spec.ts` and `e2e/terminal.spec.ts` pass unchanged.
5. **Attention.** A local terminal session's pending permission or question appears in Needs you, the sidebar glyph, the Overview card and the hover preview within 1 s of its `raised`, and leaves within 1 s of its `settled`. `e2e:lane-a`.
6. **Streaming.** While a local terminal session's turn runs, its assistant text appears in the MAWS transcript as it streams, and gives way to the transcript row once the engine writes it, with no duplicate and nothing left behind; a pane opened, or a second window opened on it, in the middle of a reply shows the text already streamed; an aborted turn leaves no streaming text behind. `e2e:lane-a`, the fake bridge streaming chunks and then writing the row into the transcript fixture.
7. **Attachments.** An image dropped on a local terminal session's composer reaches the session through the bridge's `media` op, then the text; the attachment's states show. `e2e:lane-a`.
8. **The endpoint offers `sminos.send`.** Plain text is graded `converse` and a text starting with `/` by its slash command's grade; a member below the grade is refused `grade`; the action is drawn with its lane A context and launch, and goes down RL10 as `rpc: 'send'`. `test/endpoint/grades.test.ts`, `lane-a.test.ts`, the conversation fixture.

### Orientation

- **Today's foreign-live session.**
  - The engine host's `HolderRegistry` (`src/engine-host/fs/holders.ts`) reads the engine's registry, `<config>/sessions/<pid>.json`, every 2 s while any holder is known and on watcher changes. A live record that isn't MAWS's own is a foreign holder: `{kind: 'terminal', pid}` for an interactive one, `background_job` for a `--bg` job or the engine's daemon (`holderFor`). It reaches main as `fs.holderChanged`, and the index keeps it as `SessionRecord.holder` (`src/shared/graph/session.ts`), keyed by the engine session id it maps to an `AppSessionId`.
  - Main seeds such a session's digest with `phase: 'foreign'`, `pendingRequests: 0`, `activity: null` (`src/main/index/source.ts`), and `availabilityOf` answers `foreign_held` (`src/main/sessions/availability.ts`). Its transcript is a disk tail (`src/engine-host/fs/follow.ts`, `chain-follower.ts`): completed JSONL records only, no streaming text and no request events.
  - No capability object exists. About fifteen sites gate on `holder.kind` or `availability`: `SessionPane.tsx` (no docked request, `HolderNotice` in place of the composer), `HolderNotice.tsx`, `app/session-actions.ts`, `sidebar/HoverCard.tsx`, `sidebar/RequestPreview.tsx`, `overview/SessionCard.tsx` and `derive.ts`, `attention/glyph.ts` (the holder glyph wins over every attention state), `composer/submit/submit-state.ts`, `composer/frame/bindings.ts`, `widgets/DormantGesture.tsx`, `side/btw.ts`, `tools/session-info/model.ts`, `resume/ResumeChooser.tsx`, `owner/report/answering.ts`, `transcript/source/ipc.ts`.
  - "Take over here" (`src/renderer/src/sessions/HolderActions.tsx`, `record-ops.ts`; main's `session.takeOverPane`, `session.takeOver`, `src/main/sessions/take-over.ts`, `pause-take-over.ts`) pauses a CLI in a MAWS pane with Ctrl+Z, else sends `SIGTERM` or `claude stop`, then resumes the session in MAWS.
  - Lane B's controls funnel through three places: `sendHumanPrompt` and `dispatchHumanPrompt` (`composer/submit/send-human-prompt.ts`: `SendContent`, `HumanPromptOptions`, `HumanPromptResult`), the chips' `FrameBindings` (`composer/frame/bindings.ts`: `model`, `mode`, `directories`, each a `ChipBinding {value, options, disabled, set}`), and `RequestCard`'s delivery (`request-card/RequestCard.tsx`: `session.answer` with a `RequestAnswer` from `src/shared/engine/answers.ts`, whose union carries permission scopes, `deny_and_stop`, an edited input, plan outcomes with feedback, dialog results and elicitation actions).
- **The bridge (DX5).** Every Claude Code process that loads sminos runs the bridge mod, which spawns a child serving the session over HTTP on a Unix socket, `$SMINOS_HOME/bridge/<engine session id>.sock` (`SMINOS_HOME` from the environment, else `DAEMON_HOME`, else `~/.claude/sminos`; the child inherits the `claude` process's environment). The directory is 0700 and the socket 0600; a client presents nothing. One request per connection.
  - `GET /status`: `{session, pid, protocol: 1, socket, started, ready, subscribers, mod, lane?, launch?, capabilities?}`, `capabilities` being `{sminos, protocol, engine, ops, reads, routes, controls}`. A socket that answers with the right `session` is a live session process: the child exits within 0.5 s of its parent.
  - `GET /events`: one `snapshot` line (current streaming blocks, turns, pending requests, recent settlements, the model override, the catalog), then every event numbered `n` from 1 per connection, no resume. A module reload or a gap ends the stream; a subscriber 10,000 events behind is cut off. The client resubscribes for a fresh snapshot. Any number of subscribers may hold streams at once.
  - `POST /command[?wait=ms]` with `{op, …}`; `POST /requests/<id>/decision`; `GET /requests`, `GET /requests/<id>`. Interfaces §B has each body.
  - The stream is not a transcript: rows come from the transcript on disk. The stream adds live chunk text, row notices, requests, op outcomes, the catalog, the model override, usage and turn state, for the main loop and every subagent loop.
  - **`deliver`** is the one op that submits a person's words: idle, as a prompt (a turn of its own); while the main turn runs, as a user row read at the next request. It answers `{submitted: true}` once it has taken the key, before the words have entered; `delivered {key, outcome: 'entered', uuid}` or `delivered {key, outcome: 'refused', reason}` follows on the stream. Its de-duplication is a high-water mark per writer: any key at or below the last one taken is dropped as a duplicate, so keys must reach the child in order.
  - **What the local path does not get.** sminos's daemon checks a remote RPC's freshness, policy, shape, digest and scope, keeps it in an at-most-once ledger, and keeps its answer under its nonce. A direct client gets none of that: the child accepts any rule list in a decision, and a 504 means the op may have run. Only `deliver` (by its key) and `media` (by its upload id) de-duplicate.
  - **A launch fence.** A body naming `launch` is refused `409 launch` when the child's process is another launch; a body naming none is a local caller's. `/status` names the launch from sminos 1.25.0 on.
  - **The model override.** The `model` op replaces model and effort together; sending one resets the other unless both are sent. It rewrites the main loop's requests only.
  - **The lane B observer.** A MAWS lane B session's bridge refuses every op `lane-b` ("MAWS controls this session"). Such a session's holder is never foreign.
  - **sminos's daemon relays what MAWS does.** On a machine not cut over, for a session in a hub project, the daemon's uplink relays the bridge's `raised`, `settled` and op outcomes to the desk hub, so desk-web sees MAWS's settlements as answered elsewhere. Harmless, and gone at the machine's cutover.
- **The DX4 session plane, and the endpoint's lane A channel.** sminos's daemon serves each session as DX4 messages (`apps/desk-hub/src/protocol/session.ts`: `SessionMessage`, `SUBSCRIPTION_KINDS`, `SESSION_WIDE_KINDS`): the bridge's `chunk` and `block-row` unchanged, `step-end` for every step a `step end` or a `turn complete` closes, `raised` and `settled` with `session` added, `op` and `compact` for op outcomes, plus what only the plane has: `row` (a disk row verbatim, with chain marks), `rows` pages, history in the snapshot, `presence` (the engine record's status, `bridge`, `capabilities`, and on RL10 `launch`), `subagents`, `diff`, `output`, `read` answers and `host` messages. It carries no model override.
  - The endpoint's lane A subscription (O10 M8, `src/endpoint/plane-a.ts`): `sub {session: {lane: 'A', id: <engine session id>}, view: 'full'}` answers `subbed`, then `snap` pages (one DX4 snapshot as JSON text, cut at 256 KiB), the pending `raised` set as `ev`s, then one DX4 envelope per `ev`. The cursor is `{epoch, seq}`, the endpoint's own; a resume inside its ring is a replay, anything else a fresh snapshot. Every sminos `end` reaches the device as `ended unavailable`. There is no agent-scoped subscription.
  - Lane A calls (`src/endpoint/calls.ts`): `sminos.rows`, `diff`, `output` and `read` each name the device's own subscription id in `input.sub` and answer `{requested: true}`; the answer arrives on that subscription as a message of its kind, and only `read`'s answer carries an id of its own.
  - Lane A acts (`src/endpoint/rl10/link.ts` `draw`): `action.session`, `context.session` and `args.session` must agree, `requestDigest` must equal `args.digest`, a member with no `launch` is `unavailable`, and a stale launch is `context`. A `done` carries only a failure's code; the full outcome comes from `query`. `launch` reaches a device only in the plane's `presence`.
  - There is **no lane A send act.** O10 offers `sminos.steer`, which the bridge refuses while no turn runs, and desk-web sent a person's words as a log `human` event through the hub. M1 adds the act.
- **desk-web's lane A view.** `apps/desk-web/src/depth/**` turns DX4 messages into MAWS's own transcript store: `DeskTranscript` (`depth/source/desk-transcript.ts`) implements the renderer's `TranscriptSource` from DX4 rows through MAWS's backfill mapping, keeps streaming text in `StreamingLayer` (`depth/stream/layer.ts`) outside the store, and reads the subagent tree into MAWS's task graph. ### M2 — The local driver: main reaches each terminal session's bridge

**At its end.** For every session on this machine held by a terminal or a `--bg` job, main knows whether its bridge answers and, when it does, keeps that session's lane A state (Interfaces §A, `LaneAState`) and drives its controls through the bridge. The `laneA.*` namespace (commands and events, Interfaces §A) answers the renderer. Unit tests against the fake bridge show every control's body, every refusal, every state transition and every delivery's fate. Nothing in the renderer uses it yet.

**Touches.** `src/main/lane-a/` (new: `driver.ts`, `bridge-client.ts`, `discovery.ts`, `reshape.ts`, `deliveries.ts`, `decisions.ts`, `state.ts` and tests); `src/shared/ipc/schema/lane-a.ts`, `names/lane-a.ts`, `grades/lane-a.ts` (new), and their entries in `names.ts`, `grades.ts`, `schema.ts` and the registry; the `routes.json` and `address-paths.json` snapshots; `src/main/machines/link-surface.ts` (`laneA.changed` and `laneA.plane` are link events: they name a session); `src/main/index.ts` (composition); `eslint.config.js` (`lane-a` joins `MACHINE_BOUND`); `test/support/fake-bridge.ts` (new, Interfaces §F); `apps/desk-hub/src/protocol/session.ts` (`SessionPresence` gains `launch?: string`, additive).

**Decisions.**
- **Local lane A goes straight to the bridge's socket, from main's machine services.** Rejected: through the local endpoint over RL10, as P5 §4's sentence reads. An endpoint serves RL10 only while its enrollment is org-listed (P5 Decision Log 55), sminos cuts a machine over for good at its first RL10 welcome, and the MacBook cuts over last; a signed-out Mac never does. That path would contradict "local lane A needs no sign-in", and would wait for O2, O10's M10 and the MacBook's cutover. Rejected too: shelling out to the `sminos` CLI verbs, which start Python per action (hundreds of milliseconds) and give no stream. The driver is Electron-free under `src/main/lane-a/`, a `MACHINE_BOUND` directory, so O3 moves it into `mawsd` with main's other machine services; after O3 the app reaches it through its local daemon, which is what P5 §4 asked for, by a different route.
- **Discovery.** The sminos home is `SMINOS_HOME` from main's own environment (a dev run, a test or the sitting sets it), else from the login-shell environment MAWS captures for launches (X6's `captureShellEnvironment`, `src/shared/host/shell-env.ts`; a Dock-launched app has no shell exports of its own), else `DAEMON_HOME` from either, else `~/.claude/sminos` (sminos's rule). A session whose `claude` ran under another home is not found and stays read-only, which is the honest result. The driver probes `<home>/bridge/<engineSessionId>.sock` with `GET /status` when the session's holder becomes foreign, when that file appears in the bridge directory (watched while any foreign holder is live), and after a stream ends. A probe that times out (2 s), is refused, or answers another `session` means no bridge. Names starting with `.` and the `<id>.json` kept files are never probed.
- **State.** `bridge` is `live` once `/status` answers `ready: true` with `capabilities.controls`; `starting` while it answers `ready: false`; `observer` for `lane: 'match'` (a MAWS lane B session, which a foreign holder never is: the state guards it anyway); `none`, with a reason, otherwise, including a sminos whose capabilities lack `controls` (older than 1.11.0, MW1), for which the reason names updating sminos and `/reload-plugins`. Only `live` drives controls.
- **A starting bridge becomes live by its stream.** A bridge found `starting` gets its state stream at once: `/events` holds the request until the mod's first resync (up to its own wait, then `503`, which the stream's backoff retries while the holder is live), and the stream's first snapshot re-reads `/status` and makes the state `live`. Nothing else re-probes the socket, since a bridge becoming ready changes neither the holder nor the directory.
- **The state stream: one per bridged session, for as long as it is held and bridged.** The driver holds one `GET /events` subscription per live or starting bridged session whether or not a page shows it, because its pending requests feed attention (M6) and its deliveries' fates come on it. On an end, a 503 or a reset it resubscribes with backoff from 0.5 s doubling to 8 s, reset by a snapshot that holds for a minute, and drops the session when its holder leaves. This stream feeds `LaneAState` only.
- **Each watch is its own stream.** `laneA.watch` opens a further `GET /events` subscription for that page and that session, and answers a watch id; the child's own snapshot line seeds that watcher atomically, and every later event on that subscription follows it, so a pane opened mid-reply, or a second window, starts from the text already streamed without disturbing any other watcher. Its messages go out as `laneA.plane` with the watch id, and each page reads only its own ids. A watch ends at `laneA.unwatch`, at the page's end (O1's `clientGone`), or when the session leaves lane A; a stream that ends under a live watch is resubscribed with the state stream's backoff and announced as a reset before its new snapshot. Rejected: one shared stream fanned out with a snapshot folded in main, which re-implements the streaming layer's fold in main and still races a watcher's start.
- **One lane A vocabulary in the renderer: DX4's session-plane kinds.** The driver reshapes DX5 events into the DX4 messages sminos's own plane makes of them (Interfaces §B), so the renderer reads one vocabulary whether the session is here or on another machine. Rejected: a MAWS-specific vocabulary, which needs two translators and a third shape; and a full local DX4 plane (history pages, `row`s with chain marks, the jobs ledger), which re-implements sminos's plane beside MAWS's own disk reader. Locally, rows keep coming from MAWS's disk tail; the plane carries only what the disk lacks.
- **Turns and steps** (Interfaces §B). The reshaper tracks each loop's open steps by `(turnId, agentId)`, per subscription, seeded from every snapshot's `streaming` entries (each `{turnId, agentId, step}` an open step), as sminos's plane seeds its own (`sminos_plane.py`), so a watch opened mid-reply knows the step it never saw start; `step start` opens one; `step end` closes its step; `turn complete` closes every step of that turn still open, one `step-end` each, so an aborted turn leaves no streaming block behind. `turn.running` follows the main loop alone (`agentId` null): a subagent's turn completing never marks the session idle.
- **Send is `deliver`, through one ordered lane per session.** `laneA.send` gives the text to the session's delivery lane, which posts the bridge's `deliver` op with key `{writer: 'maws/<local MachineRef>', seq}`, in either turn state. Rejected: the bridge's `steer` op, which is refused while no turn runs (so the composer would race the turn state), is not idempotent on this path, and labels the person's words `[sminos steer from human]`. A text starting with `/` is refused `bad-args` here; the renderer sends commands as `laneA.command`. The lane's rules (Interfaces §B, "the delivery lane"):
  - One `deliver` in flight per session, in the order the sends arrived, from every page: the bridge's mark drops any key below one it has taken, so a later key must never reach it first.
  - `seq` is the last allocated plus one, written to `<userData>/lane-a/deliveries.json` (Interfaces §H) before its post, so a crash leaves a gap rather than a reuse.
  - A post that times out or loses its socket is retried under the same key, after 1 s and 3 s; `{duplicate: true}` to a retry means the first copy was taken. If all three fail, that send is `unconfirmed` and the lane moves on; nothing ever reposts a key below the mark.
  - The lane holds at most 8 sends; a ninth is refused "too many messages waiting for this session".
  - Each send's fate is tracked by its key in `LaneAState.deliveries`: `submitted` at the child's answer, then `entered` (with the row's uuid) or `refused` (with the bridge's reason) from the state stream's `delivered`. A send still `submitted` when the state stream ends is `unconfirmed` once its fresh snapshot arrives, since the stream that would have named its fate is gone. `{duplicate: true}` to a first copy is `refused` ("the bridge had already taken this key"), never a success. Settled entries stay 60 s.
  - `laneA.send` answers once its send is `entered`, `refused` or `unconfirmed`, or at 5 s still `submitted` (a busy session's row enters at once; an idle one's prompt enters as its turn starts). Its answer names the send's key, and the send's later fate, if it had none yet, arrives in `LaneAState.deliveries` under that key (M5 shows it).
- **Decisions are built from typed fields** (Interfaces §D). The renderer passes the `RequestAnswer` its card made; the driver maps it to a decision body or refuses the variant. A scope is resolved against the raised request's own `suggestions`: accepted only when the request carries exactly one suggestion, an `addRules` or `addDirectories` one, and the destination is `session` or `localSettings`, and checked by that suggestion's SHA-256 over its canonical JSON against the raised record the driver holds, as sminos's daemon does for a remote RPC. Never `setMode`, never another rule list. Immediately before posting, the driver re-reads `GET /requests/<id>`: a `410` or `404` answers with the settlement it names, and nothing is posted. A request id is the call's tool-use id, so a different request never takes it, and a digest check adds nothing here. `by` is `maws`.
- **Every command and decision carries the launch** `/status` named, when it named one, so an action meant for a session's earlier process is refused `launch` at the child.
- **Timeouts and retries.** Commands post with `?wait=10000`. A 504 or a closed socket answers `{state: 'unknown'}`: the op may have run. Nothing is retried on its own but `deliver` (by the lane's rule) and `media` (same upload). The state stream resolves an unknown op where it can (`run start` for a command, `compact start`, `btw start`), and the state says so.
- **`model` keeps what it isn't changing.** `laneA.model` takes `model`, `effort` or both. The driver serializes model changes per session, reads the current override fresh (`read status`) under that lock, and posts both fields, the unchanged one as read, so a change made in the terminal meanwhile is not undone.
- **`from` is omitted**, so the note the mod writes for a stop reads "from outside the terminal", not "from the desk".
- **Session id changes.** `/clear`, `/branch` or `/resume` in the terminal gives the session a new engine id, a new transcript and so a new `AppSessionId` in MAWS; the old record's holder clears, its streams end, and the new record's holder starts discovery. Nothing follows one into the other.

**Does not touch.** The renderer (M3 to M5). Attention (M6). Media (M7). The endpoint. The engine host.

**Proves.** Unit tests with the fake bridge pin: discovery by holder change and by the socket's appearance; `starting`, `live`, `observer` and each `none` reason; a bridge that answers `ready: false` and later becomes ready on the same socket, with no holder or directory change, turning `live` through its stream; the state stream's backoff and its drop on holder end; a watch's own snapshot, a second watch opened mid-stream starting from the streamed text, and a reset under a watch; each control's exact body (Interfaces §B) with the launch; the delivery lane: two sends posted in order with the first's post delayed, a timeout retried under the same key and answered `duplicate`, a timeout exhausted then a new send under the next seq, `entered`, a refused idle submission and a refused busy append each ending `refused` with the reason, a state stream lost while `submitted` ending `unconfirmed`, and the seq written before its post; a `/` text refused; each `RequestAnswer` variant mapped or refused as Interfaces §D says, a scope checked by its SHA-256 and a request with two suggestions refusing scope; a decision on a settled request answered without a post; `unknown` on a 504; a model change alone keeping the effort read fresh; a turn completed with a step still open closing it, a watch opened mid-reply whose turn then aborts with no `step end` closing the step its snapshot named, and a subagent's turn completing leaving `turn.running` true; the guard on the real sminos home under test.

### M7 — Attachments (local)

**At its end.** An image or PDF dropped on a local lane A composer goes to the session through the bridge's `media` op, the message's text after it, and the attachment's states (`read`, `forwarded`, `failed`) show on it.

**Decisions.**
- **The bytes cross as the composer already serializes them.** The composer turns a dropped or pasted image or PDF into a base64 image or document block of `SendContent` (`composer/editor/serialize.ts`), and a pasted image has no file to name. Rejected: paths from the drop, which a paste doesn't have; and a handle taken before serialization, which reaches into the composer's editor for one lane.
- **The save follows sminos's own rules for a desk upload** (`sminos_media.py`, read for its rules; nothing of it is run): files go under `<cwd>/.claude/desk-uploads/<engineSessionId>/`, reached by a walk from `/` that follows no link; each is created exclusively as `<op>.<n>.<ext>`, `<op>` MAWS's upload id (16 random bytes, base64url) and the extension the one its bytes' signature names; the repository's common `info/exclude` gains `**/.claude/desk-uploads/` through the same walk, and a linked or irregular exclude file refuses the upload; each root is recorded in `<userData>/lane-a/uploads.json` before anything is written, and swept after a day, again following no link. `cwd` is the session's live directory from the bridge's `read status`. The bridge reads the files in by a Read of its own. Rejected: a root under MAWS's user data, outside the workspace, whose Read raises a permission prompt in the terminal for every image; and sending bytes, which the bridge has no op for.
- **Bounds** are the bridge's: one to four files, of `png`, `jpeg`, `gif`, `webp` and `pdf`; a refused or oversize file is refused before anything is written, with its reason.
- **Order.** The `media` op first, then the text through the delivery lane, so the files are queued for the turn the text starts.

**Does not touch.** Remote attachments: a session on another machine takes uploads through O7's upload surface (P5 §5), and its lane A media waits for it (M9 greys it).

### M9 — Remote lane A over O7's `RemoteLink` **[needs O7 M1 and sminos's `send`]**

**At its end.** From MAWS on one Mac, a terminal session on another machine is shown and driven like a local one: its transcript streams and pages back, its controls work, and its requests are cards in its pane. A `laneA.*` command addressed to a machine that `Machines.remote(ref)` answers is carried by O9's lane A adapter over that machine's `RemoteLink` (O7 Interfaces §A; this document's §G). It is proved under `MAWS_DEV_ORG`; the owner sees it once O8's first milestone signs the app in and lists the other machines' sessions.

**Touches.**
- Main: `src/main/lane-a/remote.ts` (new: the adapter), `remote-state.ts` (a remote session's `LaneAState` from its subscription and its status reads), `src/main/index.ts` (the composition: the router's `remote` option wrapped, below), and an integration suite in `test/lane-a/` driving the adapter through a real `RemoteLink` (`createRemoteLink`, O7) against the real endpoint with the fake sminos (`test/endpoint/sub-harness.ts`'s pattern, as O7 M1's own suites do).
- Renderer: `src/renderer/src/lane-a/remote-source.ts` (new: a `SessionPlane` over `laneA.watch`, `laneA.plane` and `laneA.read`, under M3's `plane-transcript.ts`), `controls-for.ts` (a lane A row of another machine takes the lane A port; its transcript takes the remote source).

**Decisions.**
- **How a `laneA.*` command reaches the adapter.** The router hands a command for another machine to `RouterOptions.remote` before any main handler runs, and O7's router path refuses `laneA.*` (`act-required`, or `not-offered` from the endpoint for a `view` call). So the composition root wraps the `remote` it passes the router: a command whose name starts `laneA.` and whose target `Machines.remote(target)` answers goes to O9's adapter, with the link; every other command, and `laneA.*` for a socket-linked stand-in, goes to the manager as now. The adapter's events reach the windows through `emitFrom(machine, …)`. It answers `laneA.list` with the states of the sessions it watches on that machine (none until a pane opens one), since a remote session's state comes only from its subscription. Rejected: a hook inside O7's `dispatch.ts`, which changes O7 M1 while it is being built; and a second, `client`-graded namespace naming its machine, which puts a branch by link kind in the renderer and a second set of schemas beside `laneA.*`.
- **Operations from typed fields** (Interfaces §D): each `laneA.*` control becomes `link.act({action: 'sminos.<rpc>', session, context: {lane: 'A', session, launch}, args, requestDigest?})`, `session` the engine session id (the address's bare id), `args` in DX6's shape and, for `answer` and `decide`, `requestDigest` equal to `args.digest`, which is DX6's `{session, request, tool, input_sha256}` over the raised input (`desk-hub/protocol`'s `inputSha256`, as `apps/desk-web/src/cards/answers.ts` builds it). `executor` takes O7's default for `sminos.*`. The renderer's `laneA.*` inputs are the same as local; only the adapter knows the wire.
- **Step-up is O7's `'if-asked'`** (its default): the act goes unsigned, and an `ack refused {reason: 'step-up'}` makes the link sign it through the session's step-up and send it again under the same `opId`. The endpoint asks only where RL §5.6 does (a decision on a session the member doesn't own), so O9 needs no owners to decide it. A cancelled confirmation is `refused {reason: 'step-up-cancelled'}`.
- **Sends, in order.** `send` is `sminos.send` in either turn state. One per session is in flight at a time: the next act is created only once the one before it has its outcome. Rejected: releasing it at `accepted`, which means only that the endpoint handed the RPC down RL10 (`src/endpoint/actions.ts`); sminos runs each RPC on its own worker (`sminos_rpc.py` `dispatch`), so two accepted sends can reach the bridge in either order. sminos's `send` answers within its 10 s bound (Interfaces §E), so the wait is bounded. A send the link holds past O7's 60 s window ends `refused {reason: 'offline'}` and never runs (O7's open question A4 may change that to an outbox); the composer keeps its text.
- **Outcomes.** The `ActHandle`'s `outcome` (never rejecting) maps to the port's types (Interfaces §C): `done` to done; `failed` and `refused {reason}` to refused, worded from their code (M3's `words.ts`); `not-sent` to refused "not sent", the person free to send again (a new `opId`); `unknown {reason}` to unknown. Its `progress` (`waiting`, `sent`, `accepted`, `unknown`) shows on a pending control. Since a `done` carries only a code, a card's race outcome comes from the plane's `settled {winner, by}`, never from the act's result. A send's fate is its `done` result (Interfaces §E: `entered`, a `refused-delivery` failure, or `submitted` past sminos's bound, read as `unconfirmed`); a remote `unconfirmed` stays in the pending-send strip (M5) as "may not have arrived", since nothing later reports it, and the transcript shows its row if it entered.
- **`model` sends only what changed.** `sminos.model` carries `model`, `effort` or both, and sminos's RPC keeps the other from a live read under its own per-session lock (`sminos_rpc.py` `model`). The remote state's `override` is hydrated by `link.call('sminos.read', {session, sub, id, what: 'status'})` at each watch, after each model act's outcome, and at a main-loop `step-end` at most once per 10 s, since the plane carries no override.
- **`launch` comes from the plane's `presence`**, as the phone's cards take it from their snapshot. A session whose presence names no launch, or no bridge, has every control unavailable, reason "no bridge".
- **A sminos without `send`** refuses `sminos.send` as an unknown RPC. The plane carries no list of sminos's RPCs, so the capability can't know beforehand; the composer shows that refusal's reason, worded as the machine's sminos predating sends from MAWS, and keeps the text.
- **The view.** Each `laneA.watch` opens its own `link.subscribe({session: {lane: 'A', id}, view: 'full', cursor: null}, sink)`. The sink's `snapshot` arrives joined and parsed and its `event`s are DX4 envelopes; the adapter forwards them as `laneA.plane` with the watch id. The link resumes by cursor across drops, sleep and a carrier switch (O7 M1; parent acceptance 7's lane A half rides it), and a transport pause is not a transcript discontinuity:
  - `paused` and `resumed` go to the renderer as `{watch, paused: reason}` and `{watch, resumed: true}`, a state the pane may show and the source keeps serving through: paging, diff and reads stay live, and nothing waits for a snapshot;
  - `subbed {mode: 'replay'}` changes nothing, and the events it replays are forwarded like any others, each once (the link drops any at or before its cursor);
  - only a `subbed {mode: 'snapshot' | 'missing'}` after the first, an `omitted` event, or a fresh subscription after `ended` is a reset: the adapter sends `{watch, reset: true}`, then that snapshot, which `plane-transcript.ts` reads as desk-web's `interrupted()` then snapshot.
  `ended(reason)` is final at the link; the adapter opens a fresh subscription with backoff from 1 s doubling to 30 s (the endpoint collapses every sminos end to `unavailable`), announcing a reset.
- **Reads name the watch's subscription.** `rows`, `diff`, `output` and `read` go as `link.call('sminos.<kind>', {session, sub: subscription.id, …})`, the `Subscription`'s id, which O7 keeps stable across channels. `rows`, `diff` and `output` answer `{requested: true}` at once, and their answers arrive on that subscription as messages of their kind, which `plane-transcript.ts` and the moved jobs and diff modules consume by kind, as desk-web does; one request of each kind is outstanding per watch, a second waiting for the first's answer or its watch's reset. A `read` (`usage`, `config`, `settings`, `mcp`, `status`) carries an id the adapter mints, its answer matched by that id, timing out at 10 s. A watch's end rejects its outstanding reads.
- **Rows come from O8.** The sidebar's rows for another machine's sessions are O8's first milestone's, from RL6 listings (P5 Decision Log 92); O9 builds no source of its own and reads a lane A row as §G's contract request to O8 describes it. Until that milestone, the integration suite addresses a remote lane A session directly by `<ref>/<engine session id>`.
- **Remote gaps, each disabled with its reason** (copy per Q2): a subagent's own rows (the channel has no agent-scoped subscription; the tree and its statuses still show); stopping a background shell and "Take over here" (`stop`, `resume` and `retire` are not offered remotely, P5 Decision Log 51); reopening an ended remote session as lane B (O7's remote resume); attachments (O7's uploads); widgets beyond the model, `/compact` and `/mcp`.
- **Attention across machines** stays O7's (its M6): a remote lane A request shows as a card in the session's pane while it is open, and in the sidebar through the row's state.
- **Capabilities** are the plane's `presence.capabilities` (`controls`, `ops`) with `presence.bridge`, the turn state from `presence.record`, the link's `caps()` (the device's grades), and the link's state (`state()`, `refusal()`); a link that is down or refused disables every control with the reason O6's and O7's machine rows already show.

**Does not touch.** O7's link, its router path (`dispatch.ts`), its carriers or its org session. O8's rows. The endpoint beyond M1. sminos.

**Proves.** Acceptance 9 in the integration suite: each control as an act reaching the fake sminos with Interfaces §D's frame; an answer's digest equal to the fake's pending request; a decision on a session the device's member doesn't own signed after the endpoint's `step-up` refusal; two sends in order, the second not created until the first's outcome, with the first's outcome held back by the fake sminos; a model change carrying only the changed field; a lost `ack` resolved with one effect; a stale launch `context`; older rows paged and an MCP read answered through the watch's subscription id across a reconnect; the view through a forced channel drop and a carrier switch with each DX4 message delivered once (counted against the fake's numbering); a drop whose cursor stays in the endpoint's ring resuming as a replay with no reset, after which older rows still page and a read still answers; a fresh snapshot resetting the transcript cleanly; each remote gap's reason; a non-`laneA.*` command for the same machine still taking O7's router path.

**§E. `sminos.send`.**
- The endpoint's row (M1): `'sminos.send': {kind: 'act', executor: 'sminos', target: 'session', grade: {floor: 'converse', byEffect: true}}`; its by-effect rule is `session.send`'s text rule. RL10's draw refuses a send `malformed`, before anything goes down, when `args.text` is no string, is empty or blank, or is longer than 65,536 UTF-16 code units; a text starting with `/` is graded by its slash command there, never refused.
- Down RL10: `{t: 'rpc', rpc: {rpc: 'send', project, machine, member, device, created, nonce, opId, opCreated, args: {session, text}, context: {lane: 'A', session, launch}, fingerprint}}`; up: `rpc-result {project, opId, nonce, outcome}`.
- **Asked of the desk track, for sminos** (built in doperpowers after M1 lands; M9 needs its release):
  - `send` joins the RPCs on RL10 only; DX2 does not offer it.
  - Shape: `args.text` a non-empty string of at most 65,536 characters whose first non-whitespace character is not `/` (so `' /clear'` is refused too); else `bad-shape`. The figure is `session.history.append`'s limit on a prompt as typed (`src/shared/ipc/schema/session.ts`), counted there in UTF-16 code units, so a text MAWS takes is within sminos's count of code points. An `args.session` that is not a session of the project keeps the `no-session` refusal every RPC shares, not `bad-shape`.
  - A remote context is required and fenced as O12 M7 fences every bridge command: `plan` passes `launch` to the bridge, which refuses `launch` at the effect; a session with no bridge is refused `context`.
  - Effect: the bridge's `deliver` op, its key a writer that is no log writer (not `<machine>/human`, `hub/<member>` or a session writer). Sends to one session are serialized in the order their `rpc` frames arrived: the seq is reserved, durably, in the receive path before the RPC goes to a worker, and posted under one per-session lock that takes them in seq order, one `deliver` in flight, since the bridge drops any key below its mark; the seq is chosen before the op's claim, so a resealed copy answered from the ledger never delivers twice. (MAWS also holds each session's next send behind the previous one's outcome, M9; the rule here keeps order between two devices.) A conformance case stalls the first send's worker before its lock and shows the second waits.
  - Result: the RPC waits for the bridge's `delivered` outcome for its key, up to 10 s: `{entered: true, uuid}`, or failed `refused-delivery` with the bridge's reason, or `{submitted: true}` past the bound. A `{duplicate: true}` to a fresh key is a failure (`duplicate`), never a success.
  - Ledgered by opId like every RPC; the conformance replay plays M1's lines.
