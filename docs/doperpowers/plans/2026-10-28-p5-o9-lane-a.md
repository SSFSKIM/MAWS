## Purpose

Most of the owner's sessions start in a terminal. Today MAWS shows such a session read-only while it is live: the transcript as the engine writes it to disk, a notice naming the terminal, and "Take over here", which ends the terminal process and resumes the session in MAWS. Every terminal `claude` the owner runs loads sminos's bridge mod, which can already send, interrupt, answer, approve and change the model of its session from outside. O9 puts that reach inside MAWS.

After O9:

- **A terminal session on this Mac is driven from MAWS, signed out, with no hub.** Its pane has the composer: send a message whether or not a turn runs, interrupt, set the model and effort, run a slash command, ask a side question, compact, toggle an MCP server, stop or message a subagent, stop a background shell, attach an image. Its permission prompts and questions are cards in the pane and rows in Needs you, answered in MAWS or in the terminal, whichever comes first. Its reply streams as it is written.
- **What the bridge can't reach is disabled in place, with its reason.** Permission mode, rewind, file rollback, an MCP server's form, a slash command whose effect is a terminal panel: each is a greyed control that says why on hover, never a control that fails after it is used.
- **A terminal session without the bridge stays as it is today**: read-only, with "Take over here".
- **A terminal session on another machine is driven the same way** from MAWS on the MacBook, through that machine's endpoint, once O7's link to other machines exists.

**How to see it working.** Locally: start `claude` in a terminal in a scratch directory, open MAWS on the same Mac, open that session. The composer is live. Type a message: the terminal shows it arrive as your words and the reply streams in both places. Ask for something that needs a permission: the card shows in MAWS and the dialog in the terminal; answer in either and both settle. Remotely, after the remote milestones: the same from the MacBook against a terminal session on the mini. Concrete Steps has the commands.

## Acceptance

Each item is checked by the command Concrete Steps names. "The fake bridge" is the DX5 test double of Interfaces §F. "The sitting" is M7's or M9's live check with the owner present.

1. **Local controls, signed out.** On a MAWS profile with no sign-in and no endpoint enrolled, and with no sminos daemon involved (`e2e:lane-a` runs none), a terminal session whose bridge answers can, from its MAWS pane:
   - be sent a message while idle (a turn starts with those words as the human's) and while its turn runs (the words are read at the turn's next model request);
   - be interrupted;
   - have its model and effort set (the next `step start` names both);
   - run a slash command whose route is runnable (its `run` end shows its outcome);
   - take a side question (its answer shows), be compacted with instructions, have an MCP server enabled or reconnected;
   - have a running subagent stopped or told, and a background shell stopped.
   In `e2e:lane-a`, each control reaches the fake bridge with the exact body Interfaces §B gives; in the sitting, each works on a real terminal `claude`.
2. **Honest gaps.** Each control the bridge does not reach is disabled in place with its reason, none hidden: permission mode; rewind, forget and file rollback; deny-and-interrupt; an MCP server's form (elicitation); MCP disable where the route table drops it; a slash command whose route is not runnable; the queue. `e2e:lane-a` asserts each one's disabled state and reason against the capability fixture.
3. **The approval race.** A permission prompt, a native question and a plan approval raised by a terminal session show as cards in its pane. Answered in MAWS, the card settles and the terminal's dialog closes. Answered in the terminal first, the card settles as answered there. A MAWS answer that arrives after the terminal's shows that settlement, never an error. A background subagent's request past its window reads as the terminal's to answer. `e2e:lane-a`; the sitting.
4. **No bridge, no change.** With no socket, a stale socket, or a socket path the bridge could not bind (over 103 bytes), the session is read-only exactly as today: the holder notice and "Take over here". With a bridge that answers but can't be driven (a lane B observer's verdict, a sminos whose `/status` carries no `capabilities.controls`, a bridge still starting), it is the same, and the notice adds one line with the reason. The foreign-live scenes of `e2e/sessions.spec.ts`, `e2e/shell.spec.ts` and `e2e/terminal.spec.ts` pass unchanged.
5. **Attention.** A local terminal session's pending permission or question appears in Needs you, the sidebar glyph, the Overview card and the hover preview within 1 s of its `raised`, and leaves within 1 s of its `settled`. `e2e:lane-a`.
6. **Streaming.** While a local terminal session's turn runs, its assistant text appears in the MAWS transcript as it streams, and gives way to the transcript row once the engine writes it, with no duplicate and nothing left behind. `e2e:lane-a`, the fake bridge streaming chunks and then writing the row into the transcript fixture.
7. **Attachments.** An image dropped on a local terminal session's composer reaches the session through the bridge's `media` op, then the text; the attachment's states show. `e2e:lane-a`.
8. **The endpoint offers `sminos.send`.** Plain text is graded `converse` and a text starting with `/` by its slash command's grade; a member below the grade is refused `grade`; the action is drawn with its lane A context and launch, and goes down RL10 as `rpc: 'send'`. `test/endpoint/grades.test.ts`, `lane-a.test.ts`, the conversation fixture.
9. **Remote lane A.** From MAWS on the MacBook, a terminal session on the mini: its transcript with streaming; send while idle and while running; interrupt; answer; approve, with the race; model and effort; a runnable slash command; a side question; compact; MCP enable. A dropped channel and a carrier switch resume the view with no row lost or doubled. Integration suite in main (M8); the sitting (M9).
10. **Nothing else moves.** Lane B's e2e groups (`default`, `composer`, `c7`, `widgets`, `side`, `agents`, `transcript`, `panel`, `layout`) stay green. ### Orientation

- **Today's foreign-live session.**
  - The engine host's `HolderRegistry` (`src/engine-host/fs/holders.ts`) reads the engine's registry, `<config>/sessions/<pid>.json`, every 2 s while any holder is known and on watcher changes. A live record that isn't MAWS's own is a foreign holder: `{kind: 'terminal', pid}` for an interactive one, `background_job` for a `--bg` job or the engine's daemon (`holderFor`). It reaches main as `fs.holderChanged`, and the index keeps it as `SessionRecord.holder` (`src/shared/graph/session.ts`), keyed by the engine session id it maps to an `AppSessionId`.

Its transcript is a disk tail (`src/engine-host/fs/follow.ts`, `chain-follower.ts`): completed JSONL records only, no streaming text and no request events.

About fifteen sites gate on `holder.kind` or `availability`: `SessionPane.tsx` (no docked request, `HolderNotice` in place of the composer), `HolderNotice.tsx`, `app/session-actions.ts`, `sidebar/HoverCard.tsx`, `sidebar/RequestPreview.tsx`, `overview/SessionCard.tsx` and `derive.ts`, `attention/glyph.ts` (the holder glyph wins over every attention state), `composer/submit/submit-state.ts`, `composer/frame/bindings.ts`, `widgets/DormantGesture.tsx`, `side/btw.ts`, `tools/session-info/model.ts`, `resume/ResumeChooser.tsx`, `owner/report/answering.ts`, `transcript/source/ipc.ts`.

  - The lane B controls all funnel through three places: `dispatchHumanPrompt` (`composer/submit/send-human-prompt.ts`), `FrameBindings` (`composer/frame/bindings.ts`, whose `ChipBinding {value, options, disabled, set}` is the nearest "available or why not" shape), and `RequestCard`'s delivery (`request-card/RequestCard.tsx`).
- **The bridge (DX5).** Every Claude Code process that loads sminos runs the bridge mod, which spawns a child serving the session over HTTP on a Unix socket, `$SMINOS_HOME/bridge/<engine session id>.sock` (`SMINOS_HOME` from the environment, else `DAEMON_HOME`, else `~/.claude/sminos`; the child inherits the `claude` process's environment). The directory is 0700 and the socket 0600; a client presents nothing. One request per connection.
  - `GET /status`: `{session, pid, protocol: 1, socket, started, ready, subscribers, mod, lane?, launch?, capabilities?}`, `capabilities` being `{sminos, protocol, engine, ops, reads, routes, controls}`. A socket that answers with the right `session` is a live session process: the child exits within 0.5 s of its parent.
  - `GET /events`: one `snapshot` line, then every event numbered `n` from 1 per connection, no resume. A module reload or a gap ends the stream; a subscriber 10,000 events behind is cut off. The client resubscribes for a fresh snapshot.
  - `POST /command[?wait=ms]` with `{op, …}`; `POST /requests/<id>/decision`; `GET /requests`, `GET /requests/<id>`. Interfaces §B has each body.
  - The stream is not a transcript: rows come from the transcript on disk. The stream adds live chunk text, row notices, requests, op outcomes, the catalog, the model override, usage and turn state.
  - **What the local path does not get.** sminos's daemon checks a remote RPC's freshness, policy, shape, digest and scope, keeps it in an at-most-once ledger, and keeps its answer under its nonce. A direct client gets none of that: the child accepts any rule list in a decision, and a 504 means the op may have run. Only `deliver` (by its key) and `media` (by its upload id) de-duplicate.
  - **A launch fence.** A body naming `launch` is refused `409 launch` when the child's process is another launch; a body naming none is a local caller's. `/status` names the launch from sminos 1.25.0 on.
  - **The lane B observer.** A MAWS lane B session's bridge refuses every op `lane-b` ("MAWS controls this session"). Such a session's holder is never foreign.
  - **sminos's daemon relays what MAWS does.** On a machine not cut over, for a session in a hub project, the daemon's uplink relays the bridge's `raised`, `settled` and op outcomes to the desk hub, so desk-web sees MAWS's settlements as answered elsewhere. Harmless, and gone at the machine's cutover.
- **The DX4 session plane, and the endpoint's lane A channel.** sminos's daemon serves each session as DX4 messages (`apps/desk-hub/src/protocol/session.ts`: `SessionMessage`, `SUBSCRIPTION_KINDS`, `SESSION_WIDE_KINDS`): the bridge's `chunk` and `block-row` unchanged, `step-end` from `step end` and `turn complete`, `raised` and `settled` with `session` added, `op` and `compact` for op outcomes, plus what only the plane has: `row` (a disk row verbatim, with chain marks), `rows` pages, history in the snapshot, `presence` (the engine record's status, `bridge`, `capabilities`, and on RL10 `launch`), `subagents`, `diff`, `output`, `read` answers and `host` messages.
  - The endpoint's lane A subscription (O10 M8, `src/endpoint/plane-a.ts`): `sub {session: {lane: 'A', id: <engine session id>}, view: 'full'}` answers `subbed`, then `snap` pages (one DX4 snapshot as JSON text, cut at 256 KiB), the pending `raised` set as `ev`s, then one DX4 envelope per `ev`. The cursor is `{epoch, seq}`, the endpoint's own; a resume inside its ring is a replay, anything else a fresh snapshot. Every sminos `end` reaches the device as `ended unavailable`. There is no agent-scoped subscription.
  - Lane A acts (`src/endpoint/rl10/link.ts` `draw`): `action.session`, `context.session` and `args.session` must agree, `requestDigest` must equal `args.digest`, a member with no `launch` is `unavailable`, and a stale launch is `context`. A `done` carries only a failure's code; the full outcome comes from `query`. `launch` reaches a device only in the plane's `presence`.
  - There is **no lane A send act.** O10 offers `sminos.steer`, which the bridge refuses while no turn runs, and desk-web sent a person's words as a log `human` event through the hub. M1 adds the act.
- **desk-web's lane A view.** `apps/desk-web/src/depth/**` turns DX4 messages into MAWS's own transcript store: `DeskTranscript` (`depth/source/desk-transcript.ts`) implements the renderer's `TranscriptSource` from DX4 rows through MAWS's backfill mapping, keeps streaming text in `StreamingLayer` (`depth/stream/layer.ts`) outside the store, and reads the subagent tree into MAWS's task graph. - **Machines.** O6's connection manager (`src/main/machines/manager.ts`) holds one `MachineLink` per machine: the local one in process, and a socket link for another profile's serve directory (the stand-in, deleted at O3). The router dispatches a command addressed to another machine through the manager. O7's first milestone adds the RL2 link to other machines; until then no remote lane A session exists in the app.

### M1 — The endpoint offers `sminos.send` (the remote send's MAWS half)

**At its end.** A device holding `converse` on a terminal session can send it a message through the endpoint as an RL4 act, `sminos.send {session, text}`, which the endpoint authorizes, draws with its lane A context and launch, and hands to sminos down RL10 as DX2's `rpc` frame with `rpc: 'send'`. sminos answers it once it ships the RPC (Interfaces §E); until then sminos refuses the unknown name, and the device reads the refusal.

**Touches.** `src/endpoint/offers.ts` (the row), `src/endpoint/by-effect.ts` (its rule); `test/endpoint/grades.test.ts`, `by-effect.test.ts`, `lane-a.test.ts`, `actions.test.ts`; `test/endpoint/rl10/conversation.json` and `fake-sminos.ts` (the `send` lines).

**Decisions.**
- **Why an act, not a person's words in the log.** The person's message into a remote terminal session travels as an RL4 act, as lane B's `session.send` does. Rejected: a device-signed `human` event posted to the project log through the hub, which the endpoint then decides with `words?` (RL §5.7). The hub reads log contents (RL §5.7's phase-1 residual), so every message would be readable at the hub; it needs the app's hub log connection and the owner's v2 words, which P5 Decision Log 75 holds until O14's last machine; and it reaches only sessions in projects the hub relays. RL §5.5 grades `send` as an act already. Rejected too: making `sminos.steer` deliver when idle, which changes an RPC desk-web and sminos's own CLI read.
- **The grade is by effect, as `session.send`'s text.** The row is `{kind: 'act', executor: 'sminos', target: 'session', grade: {floor: 'converse', byEffect: true}}`, and `by-effect.ts`'s rule for `sminos.send` is the rule `session.send` uses for its text: plain text `converse`, a text starting with `/` its slash command's grade. sminos refuses a `/` text anyway (Interfaces §E); grading it by effect keeps the endpoint right on its own, and the client sends commands as `sminos.command` (M4).
- **No new frame.** RL10 carries it as DX2's `rpc` frame, as every other lane A act; `draw` already checks session, context and launch. RL2 gains nothing.
- **The fixture.** `conversation.json` gains a `send` act's `rpc` frame down and its `rpc-result` up, and a `/` text refused at grade. sminos's conformance replay plays the fixture only when the desk track refreshes its copy, which is when it builds `send`.

**Does not touch.** sminos. O10's document (P5's writer records the row there). The app.

**Proves.** Acceptance 8. Tests pin: `sminos.send` refused `grade` for a member with `view` only; plain text accepted at `converse`; `/clear` graded `manage`; a send whose `args.session` differs from `context.session` `malformed`; a member with no launch `unavailable`; a stale launch `context`; the frame down carries `rpc: 'send'`, `args {session, text}`, the context and the fingerprint.

### M2 — The local driver: main reaches each terminal session's bridge

**At its end.** For every session on this machine held by a terminal or a `--bg` job, main knows whether its bridge answers and, when it does, keeps that session's lane A state (Interfaces §A, `LaneAState`) and drives its controls through the bridge. **Decisions.**
- **Local lane A goes straight to the bridge's socket, from main's machine services.** Rejected: through the local endpoint over RL10, as P5 §4's sentence reads. An endpoint serves RL10 only while its enrollment is org-listed (P5 Decision Log 55), sminos cuts a machine over for good at its first RL10 welcome, and the MacBook cuts over last; a signed-out Mac never does. That path would contradict "local lane A needs no sign-in", and would wait for O2, O10's M10 and the MacBook's cutover. Rejected too: shelling out to the `sminos` CLI verbs, which start Python per action (hundreds of milliseconds) and give no stream. The driver is Electron-free under `src/main/lane-a/`, a `MACHINE_BOUND` directory, so O3 moves it into `mawsd` with main's other machine services; after O3 the app reaches it through its local daemon, which is what P5 §4 asked for, by a different route.
- **Discovery.** The sminos home is `SMINOS_HOME` from the login-shell environment MAWS captures for launches (X6's `captureShellEnvironment`, `src/shared/host/shell-env.ts`), else from main's own environment, else `DAEMON_HOME`, else `~/.claude/sminos` (sminos's rule). A session whose `claude` ran under another home is not found and stays read-only, which is the honest result. The driver probes `<home>/bridge/<engineSessionId>.sock` with `GET /status` when the session's holder becomes foreign, when that file appears in the bridge directory (watched while any foreign holder is live), and after a stream ends. A probe that times out (2 s), is refused, or answers another `session` means no bridge. Names starting with `.` and the `<id>.json` kept files are never probed.
- **State.** `bridge` is `live` once `/status` answers `ready: true` with `capabilities.controls`; `starting` while it answers `ready: false`; `observer` for `lane: 'match'` (a MAWS lane B session, which a foreign holder never is: the state guards it anyway); `none`, with a reason, otherwise, including a sminos whose capabilities lack `controls` (older than 1.11.0, MW1), for which the reason names updating sminos and `/reload-plugins`. Only `live` drives controls.
- **One stream per bridged session, for as long as it is held and bridged.** The driver holds one `GET /events` subscription per live bridged session whether or not a tab has it open, because its pending requests feed attention (M5). On an end, a 503 or a reset it resubscribes with backoff from 0.5 s doubling to 8 s, reset by a snapshot that holds for a minute, and drops the session when its holder leaves. Chunk lines are small; at the owner's scale (tens of terminal sessions, a few streaming at once) parsing them costs main little, and the OX7 bench at M7 is the check.
- **What reaches the renderer.** `laneA.changed` carries the whole small state (Interfaces §A) on every change, to every page. `laneA.plane` carries DX4-shaped messages (Interfaces §B) only for sessions a page watches (`laneA.watch`, released by `laneA.unwatch` or the page's end through O1's `clientGone`), so chunk traffic never reaches pages that don't show the session.
- **One lane A vocabulary in the renderer: DX4's session-plane kinds.** The driver reshapes DX5 events into the DX4 messages sminos's own plane makes of them (Interfaces §B), so the renderer reads one vocabulary whether the session is here or on another machine. Rejected: a MAWS-specific vocabulary, which needs two translators and a third shape; and a full local DX4 plane (history pages, `row`s with chain marks, the jobs ledger), which re-implements sminos's plane beside MAWS's own disk reader. Locally, rows keep coming from MAWS's disk tail; the plane carries only what the disk lacks.
- **Send is `deliver`.** `laneA.send` posts the bridge's `deliver` op with key `{writer: 'maws/<local MachineRef>', seq}` and the text, in either turn state: idle, the bridge submits it as a prompt (a turn of its own); while a turn runs, it goes in as a user row read at the next request, as a steer does. `seq` is the session's last plus one, written to `<userData>/lane-a/deliveries.json` (Interfaces §H) before the post, so a crash leaves a gap rather than a reuse; the bridge drops any key at or below its mark, so a resend under the same key never delivers twice, and an answer of `{duplicate: true}` to a fresh key is reported as a failure. Rejected: the bridge's `steer` op, which is refused while no turn runs (so the composer would race the turn state), is not idempotent on this path, and labels the person's words `[sminos steer from human]`. A text starting with `/` is refused `bad-args` here; the renderer sends commands as `laneA.command`.
- **Decisions are built from typed fields** (Interfaces §D). Allow or deny, a deny's message, a question's answers keyed by question text; a scope only as `{suggestion, destination}`, resolved against the raised request's own `suggestions`, accepted only for an `addRules` or `addDirectories` suggestion and a `session` or `localSettings` destination, and checked by the suggestion's SHA-256 over its canonical JSON, as sminos's daemon does for a remote RPC. Never `setMode`, never another rule list. Immediately before posting, the driver re-reads `GET /requests/<id>`: a `410` or `404` answers with the settlement it names, and nothing is posted. A request id is the call's tool-use id, so a different request never takes it, and a digest check adds nothing here. `by` is `maws`.
- **Every command and decision carries the launch** `/status` named, when it named one, so an action meant for a session's earlier process is refused `launch` at the child.
- **Timeouts and retries.** Commands post with `?wait=10000`. A 504 or a closed socket answers `{state: 'unknown'}`: the op may have run. Nothing is retried on its own except `deliver` (same key) and `media` (same upload). The stream resolves an unknown op where it can (a `row-seen` for a delivery, `run start` for a command, `compact start`), and the state says so.
- **`model` carries both fields.** The bridge replaces model and effort together, so the driver always sends both, taking the one not being changed from the state's `override`.
- **`from` is omitted**, so the note the mod writes for a stop reads "from outside the terminal", not "from the desk".
- **Session id changes.** `/clear`, `/branch` or `/resume` in the terminal gives the session a new engine id, a new transcript and so a new `AppSessionId` in MAWS; the old record's holder clears, its stream ends, and the new record's holder starts discovery. Nothing follows one into the other.

**Does not touch.** The renderer (M3, M4). Attention (M5). Media (M6). The endpoint. The engine host.

**Proves.** Unit tests with the fake bridge pin: discovery by holder change and by the socket's appearance; `starting`, `live`, `observer` and each `none` reason; the resubscribe backoff and the drop on holder end; each control's exact body (Interfaces §B) with the launch; `deliver`'s key and the seq written first; a `/` text refused; a decision's scope resolved from the suggestion and one naming another rule refused; a decision on a settled request answered without a post; `unknown` on a 504; the reshape of every DX5 event kind (Interfaces §B); `laneA.plane` only for watched sessions and dropped on `clientGone`; the guard on the real sminos home under test.
