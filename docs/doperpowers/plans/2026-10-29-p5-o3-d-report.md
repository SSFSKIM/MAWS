## What landed

On the daemon shape `mawsd` composes the spine (O3-C's library: the index and read model, attention's core, the owner channel and mods, the session service, settings' machine half, the library's derived index, lane A's local driver, the design-system defaults relay) and serves it on `machine.sock` to every client; the app reads it through a synchronous mirror in main and forwards the moved namespaces. The engine host's one client, `mawsd`, is permanent, and the engine host learns of the app from `host.attachment`. Quitting MAWS stops nothing; a kept lane B session keeps folding into the index and attention keeps deriving; reopening shows it with no gap. `mawsd` starts cold, loads the spine at the first command that needs it, releases it at the idle time, and can re-execute into its cold core (built, off by default, D38). The embedded shape still runs the spine in main, and the profile flips both ways. `WIRE_MINOR` is 7 (master 6).
