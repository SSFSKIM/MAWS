## Purpose

Today an agent's browser exists only while a MAWS app is open on the session's machine. cua, the agent's browser tool, reaches the app's in-app browser through `mawsd`'s per-session socket; with no app the socket does not listen and the browser reads unavailable. A session on the mini, or one on the MacBook after the app quit, cannot open a page, not even the dev server it just started on that machine's `localhost`.

After O17's local stage, on a Mac with no app attached, the same session gets a browser the moment its agent asks for one. `mawsd` answers cua at once from a small cold broker that starts nothing; the agent's first real browser call starts the render host, which now also serves cua from offscreen Chromium tabs, so the agent navigates to that machine's `localhost`, clicks, screenshots and closes the tab, and the render host exits again after its quiet period while cua stays connected. A session holding such a tab is not released under it, and a tab whose cua connection died can still be closed. After the remote stage, a person on another machine opens that session and sees the agent's tab live in the Browser tool and, given the right, clicks and types into it; the tab keeps running on the session's machine, so it still reaches that machine's `localhost`. On Linux the browser reads unavailable, with its reason, until cua's owner ships a Linux peer check (R6).

**How to see it working.** The local stage (M8's scene), on a Mac on the daemon shape with no app: `mawsd status` shows no render host; a lane B session's `session.capabilities` reads `browser: {available: true, viewed: 'screencast'}`; the agent's `tabs.create` of `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/` answers within 4 s and `mawsd status` shows the render host with one browser session and one tab; the agent closes it, and after the quiet period the render host is gone while cua is still connected and `render.starts` has not moved again. The remote stage (M10's scene), two profiles on one Mac with B running no app: A's Browser tool on B's session shows B's tab, and after "Take control" A's click changes B's page in the next frame.

**Terms.**
- *Tab engine*: a Chromium process that holds an agent's tabs and serves cua's primitives on a per-session socket: the app's in-app browser (today's), or the render host (this child's).
- *Route*: which tab engine `mawsd` sends one session's cua connections to: `relay` (the app's), `cold` (the broker's), `starting`, `headless` (the render host's) or `none` (Interfaces §B).
- *Cold broker*: code in `mawsd` that, with no app and no render host serving the session, greets cua and answers its inventory with "no tabs" (`src/mawsd/cua-broker.ts`, a deferred chunk).
- *Promotion*: a cold connection sends a real browser primitive; `mawsd` starts the render host, connects the waiting cua connection to it and splices the bytes through (Interfaces §C).
- *Epoch*: an opaque id `mawsd` mints for a session's route each time its tab engine changes; anything bound to an older epoch is refused (Interfaces §B).
- *Headless tab*: a tab the render host holds for a session. *Agent tab*: an app tab reserved to or leased by a live cua connection.
- *Browser work*, *the fence*: a tab engine holding tabs for a session reports browser work; `mawsd` tells the engine host, which then releases that session under no rule until the work clears. The engine waits for the engine host's acknowledgement before its first tab: that wait is the fence (Interfaces §E).
- *Producer*, *viewer*: the producer turns a tab's paints into JPEG frames for viewers and takes their input; a viewer is a consumer of `DaemonServices.browser`, a test's stand-in in the local stage and the endpoint serving a remote app in the remote stage (Interfaces §G).

### M1 — Cold admission and the broker

**At its end.** With a session's route `cold` (no app, the headless engine usable, the session's engine live; until M5 the route's inputs are a stub), its cua socket listens. A connecting peer passes O5 M5's check unread; only then does the broker chunk load, greet with MAWS's hello and answer `tabs.query`, `windows.query` and `debugger.getTargets` with `[]`, and `hostRefused` by closing, with no timer and no spawn. The first other primitive pauses the connection, takes a place in the pending budget and asks `ensureBrowser`; when that answers for the current epoch, the broker connects to the target socket, reads and suppresses its hello, writes the held trigger and every byte after it exactly once, and pipes both ways. Everything is bounded (§C).

**Touches.** `src/mawsd/cua-socket.ts` (the cold path beside O5's relay: listen in `cold`, hand an admitted peer to the broker, the budgets); `src/mawsd/cua-broker.ts` (new deferred chunk: framing through `src/main/browser/cua/framing.ts`'s decoder and encoder, the cold answers, promotion, the splice, the bounds); `src/engine-host/bundle-guard.test.ts` (the chunk's marker: never on `out/main/mawsd.js`'s run path); tests beside them.

**Interfaces.** Produces `CuaBroker`, `BrowserDemand`, `BrowserTarget` and §C's constants. Consumes `ensureBrowser` by its §D signature (a fake here, M5's real one) and the route's states by §B's names (a stub here).

**Decisions.**
- The broker answers from the protocol alone. It is no browser: no tab state, no CDP, nothing but the four methods named. Any other syntactically valid request, an unknown method included, is demand: the render host answers it, a refusal included.
- The splice's order is §C's; the suppressed hello is checked for equality of `extensionInstanceId` (`maws:<appSessionId>`) and `protocolVersion`, and a mismatch closes the connection with a log line, never forwards.
- A promotion whose cua connection closes while it waits keeps its share in the start (another connection may need the render host) but never splices.
- Linux never reaches the broker (its route is `none`, M5), but the broker's tests are plain Node and run on Linux too.

**Does not touch.** The relay path's behaviour (O5 M5's relay to the app, byte for byte); the render seam; the engine host; the app.

**Proves.** Item 2 whole; item 1's cold half. Tests pin, over a real Unix socket with the real framing: a hello and then quiet; repeated inventory; several connections of one session sharing one start; a fragmented trigger and a coalesced trigger with trailing bytes, each delivered exactly once after exactly one hello; a start timeout answering the triggering RPC with an error, then closing; an `ensureBrowser` that resolves after the epoch moved never splicing; an inventory flood with the reader stopped holding the daemon at its buffer budget and closing the offender; fragmented input across many connections closing the offender at the aggregate budget; the pending budget's eight and sixty-four counting root waits and promotions together; `hostRefused` closing with no spawn; a rejected peer getting no hello and the chunk not imported; a capability read importing nothing.

### M2 — Peer and event policies (conditions 5 and 6)

**At its end.** MAWS's cua primitive server admits peers on Linux by a stated uid boundary in a private directory, never through a fake darwin addon; on darwin a missing addon still refuses every peer. Each cua lease receives only the CDP events its own commands asked for (`CuaEventScope`), in the app and the render host alike.

**Touches.** `src/main/browser/cua/peer.ts` and `index.ts` (the policy selected by platform, mirroring `mawsd`'s `uidBoundary`); `src/main/browser/cua/event-scope.ts` (new) and `connection.ts` (outgoing notifications pass the scope); tests and pinned recordings of cua's command sequences.

**Interfaces.** Produces `CuaEventScope` (§J) and the Linux policy (§H's rows for the app's and the render host's backend).

**Decisions.**
- The scope gates the outgoing notification, never internal bookkeeping: target tracking and `debugger.detached` stay correct for suppressed events.
- A domain enable admits that domain's ordinary events for that lease and child session; `Page.screencastFrame` also needs that lease's own `Page.startScreencast`; `Page.enable` alone never subscribes frames. `Fetch` interception, `Target` auto-attach and explicit attach, the file chooser, lifecycle events and tracing follow their own request state. An enable that can emit synchronously is scoped before it is sent and rolled back if it fails; a disable or stop takes effect before later events are forwarded; a child's detach or a lease's end discards their scopes.
- "Asked for" is read from the lease's own successful commands, never from the tab being leased, and is kept per child session, never one root set for all.
- On Linux the boundary is the private 0700 directory and 0600 socket with the owner and symlink checks of `@shared/host/private-path`, and the peer's uid where the platform reports it. It is stated as the uid boundary (O2's rule), not as session isolation.

**Does not touch.** cua's repository; the broker; `mawsd`'s own socket policy (O5 M5's).

**Proves.** Items 7.5 and 7.6. Tests pin: two consumers on one `TabDebugger`, one enabling `Network` and `Runtime` or starting a screencast, and the cua host receiving only what its lease asked for; a failed enable, a disable, a detach and re-attach, child-session isolation, auto-attach, `Fetch` continuation; cua's screenshot sequence (recorded from cua's host) getting its frame without `Page.enable`; on Linux a same-uid peer admitted, a foreign-owned directory and a symlinked socket refused; on darwin with the addon withheld, every peer refused.

### M3 — Browser work in `mawsd`: the fence's daemon side and the engines' reports

**At its end.** `mawsd` keeps every tab engine's browser-work reports per session, sends the engine host the full set of held sessions as `host.browserWork` whenever it changes and first on every fresh engine-host link, and answers an engine's `browserEngine.work {held: true}` only after the engine host acknowledged a snapshot naming that session. The app reports its agent tabs and waits for the fence before a zero-to-one transition (severable, D17). Until M7 no real engine host lists `host.browserWork`, so in real runs the app proceeds unfenced and the headless route is never selected (the old-host cell); the tests drive a stand-in engine host that lists it.

**Touches.** `src/mawsd/browser-work.ts` (new, eager); `src/mawsd/daemon.ts` (the snapshot first on each fresh engine-host link, beside `sendInterest(true)`); `src/mawsd/front.ts` (`browserEngine.work` accepted from the session's selected engine connection only); `src/shared/ipc/schema/browser-engine.ts` (new: the namespace with `browserEngine.work`; M6 adds the rest), `src/shared/ipc/grades/browser-engine.ts` (uniform `internal`), the registry and `wire.ts`; `src/shared/ipc/schema/host.ts` and `src/shared/ipc/grades/host.ts` (the `host.browserWork` entry, `internal`, so `mawsd` can send it; its handler is M7's), `src/shared/host/socket-protocol.ts` (the `HOST_BROWSER_WORK_CAPABILITY` constant; M7 lists it) and `e2e/support/cross-build-samples.ts` (a sample for each new command). Severable (D17): `src/main/browser/cua/{index,connection}.ts` (the work hook) and `src/main/app.ts` (wired to `browserEngine.work` when the welcome lists `machine.browserEngine`), and `machine.browserEngine` in `PEER_CAPABILITIES.daemon`. Tests.

**Interfaces.** Produces §E's `BrowserWork` and `browserEngine.work`, and the `host.browserWork` contract M7 implements. Consumes the route's notion of the selected engine connection by §B's name (a stub until M5).

**Decisions.**
- What counts, per engine (§E): the render host counts every tab of the session plus a creation in flight; the app counts every tab reserved to or leased by a live cua connection of the session plus a creation or attach in flight. The holding session is the connection's.
- The fence's linearization point is the engine host's acknowledgement of a snapshot that names the session. Only then does `mawsd` answer `{fenced: true}`, and only then does the engine create the tab or take the lease. A failure (no engine-host link, or the link lost before the acknowledgement) answers an error the engine passes to cua as the primitive's error, and no tab is created.
- `mawsd`'s holds come only from live connections through it: a render host's tabs die with its `machine.sock` connection (the render host exits at once when it loses `mawsd`, O5 M3), and an app's agent tabs stop being held when its relayed cua connections end, which O5 M5 makes happen whenever its machine link is not ready. So a fresh `mawsd`'s first snapshot is its own truth, ordinarily empty, and rightly clears the engine host's `unknown` entries; nothing is persisted (D12).
- A race between an acquire and a release the engine host has already begun is not refused by the release ladder, which would reach into O16's release paths beyond its seam. The engine ends; M5's engine-exit drop closes the session's headless tabs, and an app tab becomes the person's, as today (D12).
- Old host: with no `host.browserWork` in the engine host's welcome, `mawsd` answers the app's acquire `{fenced: false}` at once (today's behaviour), and M5 never selects the headless route.
- The app checks `machine.browserEngine` in `mawsd`'s welcome before it reports anything; with it absent (a pre-O17 `mawsd`) it creates as today.

#### §B. The route, per session

Inputs: *app* (an app is attached and its cua directory serves the session: O5 M5's `target(dir)`), *headless* (usable: the render runtime available, the seam not `unavailable`, the engine host lists `host.browserWork`, on darwin the addon loaded; never on Linux), *live* (the session's engine process is live, from raw `session.status`).

| State | Listening | cua connections go to | `browser` capability |
|---|---|---|---|
| `none` | no | — | `{available: false, reason}` (§I) |
| `relay` | yes | the app's socket for the session (O5 M5) | `{available: true, viewed: 'local'}` |
| `cold` | while *live* | the broker | `{available: true, viewed: 'screencast'}` |
| `starting` | yes | the broker; demand waits for the start | as `cold` |
| `headless` | while *live* | the render host's socket for the session, relayed directly; a still-cold connection promotes at once | as `cold` |

| From | Event | To | Actions, in order |
|---|---|---|---|
| any but `relay` | *app* becomes true | `relay`, epoch+1 | mint; end the session's cold, pending and promoting connections; from `starting` or `headless`: close subscriptions `engine_changed`, `dropBrowser`, keep the render host's hold until it answers or the host exits; listen as O5 M5 |
| `relay` | *app* becomes false | `cold` (if *headless*) or `none`, epoch+1 | mint; end the relayed pairs (O5 M5); the app's holds removed (§E); subscriptions `engine_changed` |
| `none` | *headless* becomes true, no app | `cold`, epoch+1 | mint; listen if *live* |
| `cold`, `starting`, `headless` | *headless* becomes false | `none`, epoch+1 | as the app's arrival, without listening |
| `cold` | demand on a connection | `starting` | a pending place; `ensureBrowser({appSessionId, epoch, deadline, signal})` |
| `starting` | `ensureBrowser` resolves for the current epoch | `headless` | splice each waiting trigger; later connections relayed to the render host |
| `starting` | `ensureBrowser` fails or a deadline passes | `cold` | answer each trigger's RPC with the error and close it; free its place |
| `starting`, `headless` | the render host ends | `cold` (if *headless*) or `none`, epoch+1 | mint; end the relayed connections; subscriptions `engine_changed`; that host's holds removed |
| `headless` | the engine exits or its pid changes | `headless` | `dropBrowser`; stop listening while not *live* |
| `cold`, `headless` | *live* changes | unchanged | listen or stop (the path is kept) |
| any | `session.removed` | gone | `dropBrowser` if `headless`; the socket removed (O5 M5) |

Linearization points: the epoch is minted before any teardown, so every waiter and reply bound to the old epoch is refused from that instant; a splice happens only when `ensureBrowser` resolves for the current epoch while the route is `starting`; the render host's hold on a session ends at `drop`'s answer or the host's exit, never at the request.

#### §C. Cold admission and the broker (`src/mawsd/cua-socket.ts`; the deferred `src/mawsd/cua-broker.ts`)

```ts
export const CUA_BROWSER_START_MS = 4_000;           // the first real primitive: render host start, prepare and splice
export const CUA_PROMOTION_MAX_BYTES = 1_048_576;    // one connection's buffered input while cold or promoting (one cua frame)
export const CUA_COLD_BUDGET = { session: 8, total: 64 }; // admitted connections served cold
export const CUA_COLD_BUFFER_BYTES = 4_194_304;      // every cold or promoting connection's buffered input together
// cua-socket.ts's PENDING_BUDGET ({session: 8, total: 64}) now counts promotions waiting for the render host beside
// peer-root waits: the section's CUA_PROMOTION_BUDGET (D2)
export interface BrowserDemand { appSessionId: AppSessionId; epoch: string; deadline: number; signal: AbortSignal } // deadline: monotonic ms
export interface BrowserTarget { epoch: string; socketPath: string } // the path computed by mawsd (§D), never an engine's
```

- **Framing.** cua's extension protocol: a little-endian u32 length, then JSON-RPC (`src/main/browser/cua/framing.ts`).
- **Cold answers.** On admission, one hello with `connection.ts`'s `greet` fields (`{extensionId: 'maws', extensionInstanceId: 'maws:<appSessionId>', version, protocolVersion: 1, profileName: 'MAWS'}`). `tabs.query`, `windows.query` and `debugger.getTargets` answer `[]`: a session with no live tab engine, never tabs hidden. `hostRefused` closes locally, with one log line, and starts nothing.
- **Demand.** Any other syntactically valid request. The connection stops reading; the trigger frame and every byte received after it are kept.
- **The splice.** `assertPrivateSocket(target.socketPath)`, connect; read until one complete message, which must be a `hello` with an equal `extensionInstanceId` and `protocolVersion`, and suppress it; keep any bytes after it; write the trigger and its trailing bytes to the target exactly once; write the kept post-hello bytes to cua; then `pipe` both ways, ended together.
- **Deadline.** `now + min(CUA_BROWSER_START_MS, the trigger's own remaining timeoutMs when it carries one)`. On expiry or failure: an error answer to the trigger's id (`the browser on <machine> did not start within <ms> ms`, or the start's reason) when it has one, then destroy. A slow or broken runtime gets a named failure, never an invented success. The peer-root wait (`PEER_ROOT_WAIT_MS`, 3 s) and the broker's immediate hello keep cua inside its `HELLO_WAIT_MS` (5 s); Chromium's start is never on that path.
- **Bounds.** Past `CUA_COLD_BUDGET` the new connection is destroyed, one log line per burst. A connection's buffered input above `CUA_PROMOTION_MAX_BYTES`, or the read that takes the aggregate above `CUA_COLD_BUFFER_BYTES`, destroys that connection. When a write returns `false`, the broker stops reading that connection until `drain`. Invalid framing, an oversized frame or a request after `hostRefused` closes. While cold, nothing runs on a timer; a promotion's deadline is the only timer.
- **Loading.** The chunk loads after the first peer admission, never at start, at an app's attachment or at a capability or inventory read. Its resident code after use is measured in the warm-to-idle case, not claimed away.

Alternatives sized at the draft: the literal "start Chromium on an accepted connection" saves about 260 lines and restarts Chromium at every quiet period, since cua reconnects every 5 s for its life; a cua demand mode costs about 440 lines across both repositories and waits on cua's owner, and is better only if zero no-app listeners were binding, which P5 Decision Log 187 rules out.

#### §E. Browser work and the fence

Engine host (M7):

```ts
export const HOST_BROWSER_WORK_CAPABILITY = 'host.browserWork';
// src/shared/ipc/schema/host.ts; grade internal; accepted on mawsd's permanent link only, refused from forwarded callers
'host.browserWork': { input: { held: AppSessionId[] /* the held sessions, at most the host set's 10,000 */ }; output: void }
// src/shared/engine/status.ts, inside `work` (optional, additive):
holds: z.strictObject({ browser: z.enum(['held', 'unknown']).optional() }).optional()
```

- On a snapshot: every live row named becomes `held`, every other row's entry is cleared, a named id with no row is ignored; answered after it is applied, so the next release evaluation reads it.
- When `mawsd`'s permanent link ends: every `held` becomes `unknown`. `createAdopter` sets `unknown` on every adopted session, beside M3a's `host_gap`. The next snapshot settles both.
- `holdsWork(s)` is true while `s.work.holds?.browser` is set; `hardReasons` folds it into `background_tasks`.

`mawsd` (M3, `src/mawsd/browser-work.ts`, eager):

```ts
export interface BrowserWork {
  acquire(engine: EngineConnection, appSessionId: AppSessionId): Promise<{ fenced: boolean }>; // rejects with a reason on failure
  release(engine: EngineConnection, appSessionId: AppSessionId): void; // never waits
  engineGone(engine: EngineConnection): void;                         // every hold of that connection removed
  linkUp(send: (held: AppSessionId[]) => Promise<void>, capable: boolean): Promise<void>; // the snapshot first on a fresh link
  linkDown(): void;                                                   // pending acquires reject
  held(): AppSessionId[];
}
```

- Holds are a set of engine connections per session; a session is held while the set is non-empty. One snapshot is in flight at a time and changes coalesce. An acquire resolves `{fenced: true}` at once when the last acknowledged snapshot already names its session, and otherwise at the acknowledgement of the next snapshot naming it; with no capable engine host it resolves `{fenced: false}` at once; with no link it rejects `the engine host on <machine> is reconnecting`.
- Engines report through `browserEngine.work {appSessionId, held: boolean} → {fenced: boolean}`. `held: true` is accepted from the session's selected engine connection only, and resolves after the fence or rejects, so the engine creates nothing; `held: false` is accepted from any connection holding the session (an engine handed over reports its zero after `drop`) and answers at once. The render host treats `{fenced: false}` as a refusal: it is never selected without the capability, and a headless tab without a fence is never made.
- What counts: the render host, every tab of the session plus a creation in flight; the app (severable, D17), every tab reserved to or leased by a live cua connection of the session plus a creation or attach in flight. The engine acquires at the cua creation wrapper (`tabs.create`, and in the app `debugger.attach`) when the session's count is zero, never inside the synchronous `TabStore.open`; a popup adopted from a counted tab needs no acquire, and a parent's close never clears the hold before its adopted child is counted.

#### §F. The machine-local engine wire (`src/shared/ipc/schema/browser-engine.ts`)

```ts
type BrowserEngineOperation =
  | { op: 'prepare' } | { op: 'drop' }                 // the render host only
  | { op: 'inventory' }
  | { op: 'open'; subscriptionId: string; tabId: string; control: boolean }
  | { op: 'ack'; subscriptionId: string; seq: number }
  | { op: 'input'; subscriptionId: string; seq: number; event: BrowserInput }
  | { op: 'close'; subscriptionId: string };
// event, mawsd → the selected engine's connection alone (never the bus's fan-out):
'browserEngine.request': { id: string; appSessionId: AppSessionId; operation: BrowserEngineOperation }
// commands, engine → mawsd (namespace browserEngine, grade internal), each → void unless noted:
'browserEngine.answer': { id: string; result: { ok: true; value: null | EngineInventory } | { ok: false; error: string } }
'browserEngine.work':   { appSessionId: AppSessionId; held: boolean } /* → { fenced: boolean } */
'browserEngine.state':  { appSessionId: AppSessionId; inventory: EngineInventory }
'browserEngine.frame':  { subscriptionId: string; frame: Omit<BrowserFrame, 'jpeg' | 'epoch'> & { jpegBase64: string } }
'browserEngine.ended':  { subscriptionId: string; reason: string } // 'tab_closed' | 'view_gone' | 'frame_too_large' | 'producer_failed'; a string, so a new reason stays additive
type EngineInventory = Omit<BrowserInventory, 'epoch'>; // mawsd stamps the epoch
```

Rules. Every engine message is bound to the connection it arrives on and the session it names, and accepted only while that connection is the session's selected engine; the socket's order is the order of events, so engines carry no epoch (D7). Request and subscription ids are `mawsd`'s; an answer is accepted only for a pending id sent to that connection; a frame only for that connection's outstanding subscription, one in flight. `prepare` answers `null` after §D's conditions, `drop` after the session's views, connections and subscriptions are gone, `inventory` with the bounded snapshot, the rest `null`. `browserEngine.state` follows each tab's creation or removal, a metadata or control change, and a ready or unavailable change, at most 256 tabs a push; no polling. A connection's end closes its subscriptions and pending requests. A frame line is JSON with base64 (a binary frame on `machine.sock` would be a new message kind, a wire major).

#### §G. The producer and the feed seam (`src/shared/browser/screencast.ts`, `DaemonServices.browser`)


#### §I. The session capability

`browser` stays a loose `SessionCapability` with one optional field: `{available: boolean; reason?: string; viewed?: 'local' | 'screencast'}`; `viewed` says how a person on that machine sees the tabs (in the app's pane, or only by screencast), not whether anyone is watching, and never chooses a remote app's branch (§K). In order:

1. An app attached: `{available: true, viewed: 'local'}`, or O5 M5's path-too-long reason.
2. Linux: `cua's host cannot check its peers on Linux yet, so the agent's browser is unavailable on <machine>`.
3. darwin with the addon not loaded: `the peer identity check did not load on <machine>, so the agent's browser is refused`.
4. An engine host without `host.browserWork`: `the engine host on <machine> predates browser work holds; it is replaced when idle`.
5. The runtime absent or the seam unavailable: O5's `the render service is unavailable on <machine>: <reason>`.
6. Otherwise `{available: true, viewed: 'screencast'}`, before Chromium starts and whether or not the engine is live.

One `session.capabilitiesChanged` per change of reading; runtime install and uninstall refresh through O5 M6's bounded watch, with no polling.

## Surprises & Discoveries

- Observation: an app's agent tab is not lost when its session is released: the engine's exit closes cua's connection, and its reservations end, making the tab the person's, still in the panel and persisted. Fencing app tabs protects nothing the release would destroy, and holds laptop sessions (R5).
  Evidence: `src/main/browser/cua/claims.ts` `dropReservations` ("its tabs become the person's"); `connection.ts` `close()` ("its tabs are the person's now"); `src/main/browser/tabs.ts` persists tabs.
- Observation: cua's hello is deterministic per session, so the broker's hello and the render host's are equal and the second can be suppressed by checking two fields.
  Evidence: `connection.ts` `greet()`: `extensionInstanceId: \`maws:${this.appSessionId}\``, `protocolVersion: PROTOCOL_VERSION`.
- Observation: every hold `mawsd` reports comes from a live connection through it (the render host exits at once when it loses `machine.sock`; the app ends relayed connections when its machine link is not ready), so a fresh `mawsd` needs no persisted browser-work state (D12).
  Evidence: `src/render-host/host.ts` header ("the connection's end exits 0 at once"); O5 Decision Log, M5's review rounds (5).
- Observation: O7's §N admits only streams opened under an act, so a `view`-graded read stream (this kind, and O7 M5's own artifact pages) needs a new admission rule, which R1 proposes once for both.
  Evidence: O7's plan, §N Rules ("The endpoint admits an open when the act `op` names answers `done` …").
- Observation: `browser.*` is graded uniformly with 54 commands; mixing grades in it would need a complete per-command map, so the engine commands take a namespace of their own (D8).
  Evidence: `src/shared/ipc/grades/browser.ts` (`'client'`); `src/shared/ipc/grades.ts` `CommandGrades` ("a mixed namespace's complete map").
