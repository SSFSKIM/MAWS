## How it ran

The controller ran the spec's two milestones through doperpowers:subagent-driven-execution: one `doperpowers:task-executor` per milestone, a `doperpowers:task-reviewer` at each boundary with fixes resumed on the executor, and the whole branch reviewed at `doperpowers:reviewer-high` (Decision Log 1). Every decision and discovery is folded into the spec: Decision Logs 4, 5, 7 and 12 to 15, and Surprises. Local runs on the MacBook were one-file eslint or vitest only, with no typecheck (constraint 6, as corrected in Decision Log 5); everything else ran through `scripts/ci-gate.sh`, and every Mac run was cleared by the architect first.
