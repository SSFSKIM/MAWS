After this child, a session MAWS runs whose question tool is "Default" asks through the same sheet a terminal session does, and is answered the same way. The agent's call returns at once and draws its row; nothing opens. The person answers in the side sheet, on the local page or with `sminos sheet answer`. The tool inside the session delivers the answer, as it does in a terminal: on the agent's next tool result while a turn runs, on the next prompt otherwise, each question's words and the picked labels with it. MAWS adds one thing a terminal does not need: when the session is idle and an answer is owed, it writes one constant sentence, and the tool fills it with the answer; when the session has no process, it starts it first. MAWS holds nothing of a delivery, so nothing that happens to its frame, its host or its journal can lose an answer, deliver it twice or carry it into another conversation. A question holds no engine process. A session set to "MAWS" keeps `ask` exactly as it is, and no default changes.

## Words this document adds

The composite's Terms define terminal session, MAWS-run session, the tool, sheet, question, asker, surface, the page, the bridge and envelope. C2's plan adds the record, the door, the step, the view, a route, a flight (the tool's: one envelope on one route until it lands or fails) and the published set; C3's adds the set's pin, the model, a sheet call, the spine and the side sheet. Added here:

- **The host** is the engine host: the detached process `mawsd` starts, which owns each MAWS-run session's engine process, its queue and its journal (`src/engine-host/`). The spine reaches it through `SpineHosts.dispatch('engine-host', …)` and hears it through `hosts.onEvent` (`src/services/spine-root.ts`). A host is **parked** when it has exited at `mawsd`'s asking, with no session left to run and no app attached for the idle time; `mawsd` starts one again when something wants it.
- **A sheet launch** is a launch that carries `MAWS_QUESTION_SHEET`: its served set of MAWS's tools has `question: 'engine'` and its spec names the sminos home, on a host that sets the variable (F2, F3). Whether a running process is one is read from its spawn row (F31).
- **The form** is the number in the variable's value, `2:<session id>`: the delivery's form a plugin must know to take the sheet under MAWS (the composite's §5.4, "The proof names the form").
- **The ring** is the one frame MAWS writes for a delivery: a user-role message whose whole text is the constant sentence "The human answered questions on your sheet." followed by **the mark**, one invisible character that no trimming removes (U+2060; the composite's §5.4), written as a steer of the host's own kind. **To ring** is to write it. **The ringer** is the engine host's part that decides when, one for each process (`src/engine-host/sheet-ring.ts`, M4). **The teller** is the spine's part: it tells the host **the table**, each session's keys owed, and starts a session with no process (`src/services/sheet/owed.ts`, M4).
- **Filled** and **dropped** are what the tool's prompt hook does with a ring: it adds the ready envelope as context, or, when the envelope it would attach names no answer, drops the frame, and no turn starts. A frame is a ring to the tool on three tests together (the runner is `maws`, the prompt's origin is the composer's, its whole text is the sentence with its mark), and to MAWS on its own evidence only (the uuid it wrote, or, in a file, a sheet envelope attached to a record its own launch wrote): the two are apart on purpose (F33, F23).
- **Owed** is said of an answer the record shows and not yet delivered. The table carries it as two sets for a session: `main`, the answers pending with recipient `main`, which are what a ring is for; and `rest`, the other answers a start would hand the main conversation (pending for another recipient, or queued in the inbox and not cancelled by a queued retract). A session with no process is owed both.
- **The hold** is the Stop's: the keys owed, `main` and `rest`, as the host knew them where it accepted a Stop, or where a turn ended aborted that no Stop of its own and no message accounts for. No ring is written and no session is started for answers that are all in it, until a person's message has been written to the engine (F36). The engine host keeps it, in memory and in the session's state file.
- **MAWS ran it last** is said of a session with no process: MAWS launched it at some time, and the newest record of its transcript that names an entrypoint names MAWS's (F37).
- **The transcript** is the engine's own file of a session's conversation.
- **The carriers round** is the probe of 2026-11-28 named in Progress. **A recording** is one of a probe's fixtures, an engine journal; the gate's suites play one back in place of an engine, in process (`src/engine-host/replay-transport.ts`) or as a real child process where a session host must hold one (**the stub engine**, `test/support/stub-engine.ts`).
- **The Mac runner** is the project's self-hosted Mac, the one the gate's `mini` lane runs on.

- **MAWS's constant frame over the wire** (B1, B4). Written to an idle session with an answer pending, the frame reaches the tool's prompt hook with origin `composer` and no turn id; the hook's context and the frame reach the model as one user message; the stored row is an attachment of kind `hook_additional_context` (hook `prompt.submit`), which the tool's `session.append` hook sees and lands, about a third of a second after. On the wire only the frame's own echo shows. The engine holds its reply to the host's `initialize` until the plugins' `session.start` has settled, and a frame written earlier waits with it, so no frame arrives before the tool's start; at a resumed launch with a queued answer the hook took the envelope (marked as answered while the session was stopped) and the start's context together.
- **What the hook is given of a prompt** (M2's P9, on the pinned engine). The hook's event holds `text`, `wait` and `origin`, and nothing of the frame's form. For a frame written as a string, `e.text` is the string as written, a trailing line break included. For one written as blocks, the engine joins the text blocks and trims the white space around the result before the hook reads it, and gives the hook nothing of an image block; the saved row and the model's request keep the blocks as written. A U+200B at a block's end survives the trim. A hook that drops such a prompt drops its image with it (`prompt_kept: false`).
- **What tells the host that a process has sminos's tool** (M2's P8, P7). The control request `mcp_status`, asked after `initialize` has resolved and before any prompt, names a server `sminos` with `source: 'plugin'` and `AskUserQuestion` among its tools where M1's plugin took the sheet, and no such server under the plugin of `40b79a7d1`; `get_context_usage` names the tool too. The reply to `initialize` and the frames the engine sends unasked name nothing. `system/init`'s list of tools holds the built-in's name `AskUserQuestion` even where the sheet's tool is offered and the built-in is not, so that list is evidence of nothing. The host already asks `mcp_status` once when a session becomes ready and keeps each later answer whole (`noteMcpStatus`, `src/engine-host/session.ts`).
- **A settings variable reaches sminos** (M2's P6). `SMINOS_HOME` given only in `--settings`' `env` is seen by the mod at its first `session.start` and by the step, the bridge and the command hooks; nothing is written under the default place.
- **A frame during a turn carries nothing** (B1). A `next` frame is given to the prompt hook when the engine folds it in, at the running call's result, by which time the result route has delivered on that result; a `now` frame moves the call to the background and the same happens. Any prompt that enters with an envelope ready carries it, the person's own included.
- **A dropped frame** (B2). A function hook can drop a host's frame (`return { drop: reason }`). The engine then echoes nothing and calls no model, and emits: `command_lifecycle` `queued` and `started`; `system/init`; a `system` frame of subtype `informational`, level `warning`, `prevent_continuation: true`, `prompt_submit_outcome: { kind: 'dropped_by_hook', prompt_kept: false, reason }`; a `result` (success, `num_turns` 0, the drop's sentence as its text, origin `human`); `command_lifecycle` `completed`. Read without the outcome field, that is a turn that ran. The warning is also filed in the transcript, where the backfill maps a record of that subtype to a notice, and the frames include a `running` and an `idle` state change, which the digest follows.
- **The prompt hook has ten seconds** (B3). Past them the prompt enters without the hook's context, and the hook's later work is lost with it; the limit holds for every prompt.
- **Nothing of a delivery is on the wire** (B5). A context on a tool result is in the model's request and in the transcript, as an attachment of kind `hook_additional_context` (hook `tool.call`), and in no frame the engine sends its host; the same holds for a context on a prompt.
- **A process killed as an envelope landed** (B6). The resumed start's search of the transcript takes the key out of `pending` before any prompt; the common case is smaller still, since the `landed` step is a child process the kill of the engine does not reach.
- **Where the tool's start context goes over the wire.** On a resumed launch the classic SessionStart fires before the tool has made its choice, so the start's context rides the next prompt as that hook's context; only a compaction's SessionStart carries it itself.
- **What holds a process.** `SessionMaws.pending` is the tool server's calls in flight plus the asynchronous asks; `hardReasons` (`budget.ts`) refuses a release while it is above zero. Sheet questions are in neither count, since they are in no table of the host.
- **A steer in the queue** (`src/engine-host/queue.ts`). `MessageQueue.steer(content, { priority: 'next', preface })` refuses a session that is not ready, journals the `preface` rows with the frame's `out` row once the write resolves, and keeps a `next` steer among the pending steers it publishes (`QueueState.steers`) until the engine starts or drops it; a Stop's withdrawal phase (`holdAll`) holds such a steer into the app queue, to be sent later as a message. A person's send while a steer is unstarted waits in the app queue, and what releases the queue's head is a turn's end (`onTurnEnded`) or a submission's own drop; a steer's `completed` alone releases nothing.
- **Bringing a session up.** `SessionService.acquire(appSessionId, reason, options)` (`src/services/sessions/service.ts`) resumes a session and resolves an `AcquireOutcome` (`src/shared/sessions/setup.ts`): `ready`, `needs_trust`, `relay_unreachable`, `held`, `archived`, `missing_directory`, `invalid`, `refused`, or `failed`, which is every error of the setup, "still stopping" and a readiness that timed out among them. `reason` is logged and nothing branches on it. It is not by itself a start that sends nothing: a message a person left behind an unanswered trust or relay card is retained (`resumeMessages`, `src/services/sessions/start-guards.ts`) and a later resume sends it; the option `requireEmptyMessage` refuses such a start (`refused`, reason `busy`) and leaves the message.
- **A Stop in the host** (`MessageQueue.stop`, `src/engine-host/queue.ts`): it counts itself in (`stopping`), pauses the queue for `interrupt`, withdraws the waiting steers (`holdAll`) and then interrupts. `EngineSession.interrupt` reaches it only for a process that is ready (`readyClient()` throws otherwise), and `holdAll` runs only for a Stop that asks for it (`holdEngineQueued`, which every Stop of the app's passes). The count falls when the interrupt is answered, which can be before the interrupted turn has ended; the queue holds `stoppedTurn` from then until that end. A steer is refused only for readiness, so one can be written between the snapshot of what waits and the interrupt; a Stop that cancels a steer before the engine started it ends no turn. A turn a `now` message preempted ends aborted too, and the normaliser marks its end `endedForMessage`. The writer drains the engine's frames it held, a steer's own lifecycle among them, before the write's promise resolves, so a frame's end can be known before its uuid is.
- **A spine that may not wake the host.** `mawsd` loads the spine for a command, for the endpoint, or for the engine host's events (`ensure`, `src/mawsd/spine-seam.ts`). A load that only events caused is not `wanted`: its adapter refuses every dispatch to an engine host that is not ready (`host_unavailable`, `SpineHostsAdapter.dispatch`, `src/mawsd/spine-hosts.ts`), so it never starts a parked one. Two callbacks are apart there: the one `SpineEvents` calls for an event in `NEED_EVENTS` marks the load wanted, and the adapter's `need` asks `mawsd` for an engine host, which it does only for a load already wanted. The session service's `hostLists` dispatches `engine.sessions` when the link holds no welcome, so on such a load it is refused, and on a wanted one it wakes the host merely to ask.
- **Who wrote a transcript's record.** Each record of the engine's transcript names the entrypoint that wrote it: `cli` for a terminal, `sdk-ts` for a launch of MAWS's (`OWN_ENTRYPOINT`, `src/engine-host/fs/holders.ts`; MAWS sets `CLAUDE_CODE_ENTRYPOINT` to it). The index's row holds the transcript's path (`transcript_path`, `src/services/index/sessions.ts`) and whether MAWS ever launched the session (`readRecordedRoute(record.gateway)`, `src/shared/engine/route.ts`, null for one MAWS only discovered); it does not hold who wrote last.
- **Events reach the spine at most once.** `mawsd/spine-hosts.ts` drops what arrives while the link is not ready, and an adopter's fold emits nothing again. A reader that must not miss a fact asks for it.
- **What the host keeps, and for how long.** An `EngineSession` is made for each launch and for each adoption (`src/engine-host/launcher.ts`), so what it holds is one process's. The session state store (`src/shared/engine/session-state-store.ts`; the file store in `src/engine-host/store/`) keeps one state file for an app session, read and written whether or not the session has a row, with optional fields added over time (`keep`, `owedInterrupt`, `observerSeenLaunch`) and a schema that strips what a build does not know. The engine host outlives its sessions' processes: `mawsd` asks it to exit when idle only after no app has been attached and no session has been of interest for the idle time, ten minutes by default (`src/mawsd/clocks.ts`), and an update replaces it by a handover, in which the new host adopts the running engines.
- **A command of the host that needs no session.** The `engine.*` family (`src/shared/ipc/names/engine.ts`, `schema/engine.ts`, `grades/engine.ts`; handled in `src/engine-host/ipc.ts`) is answered without a session's row, and the spine dispatches one today (`engine.sessions`, `src/services/spine-root.ts`). `host.interest` is a level, told whole and told again at the next link. A dispatch from a load that is not wanted reaches a host that is ready and is refused for one that is not; the adapter's own comment says what follows: the part that sent it sends again at the next welcome. The adapter's `need` returns at once when the load has already streamed, and causes no new welcome.
- **How the engine ends a frame it was given** (`onLifecycle`, `src/engine-host/queue.ts`). Every user frame gets `command_lifecycle` rows: `queued`, then `started` and, at its turn's end, `completed`; or, with no `started`, one of `cancelled`, `discarded` and `refused`, after which no result names it. The queue retires a submission at those three and considers its head; a steer it only stops listing.
- **How the step packs an envelope** (`envelope`, `batches`, `sminos_sheet_step.py`). What is pending for one recipient goes in as batches, those that hold an answer not applied across a stop first (`wakes`), each group in the order accepted, up to the budget and 200 keys; the text ends in one block line whose `answered` and `dismissed` name what it carries, which the mod already reads (`blocksOf`, `sheet-model.tsx`). At a start every outcome pending is marked `acrossStop`, so after a resume no batch wakes and the order is the order accepted. The view lists the first 200 pending outcomes of all recipients together (`VIEW_PENDING`). `readyFor` gives the mod no envelope while one of its keys is on a route.
- **How a carrier is read for the transcript** (`question-sheet-c3`). `src/shared/engine/sheet-carrier.ts` reads a sheet's envelope on a plugin's prompt and on a hook's context, and `SheetRow` draws receipts from the deliveries a history holds and, while the record lives, from the record. The live wire carries no hook's context, so on the live route only the file's rows ever held one; a MAWS-run session's live rows have the record alone.
- **The channel journal.** `LaneBSource` (`src/services/channel/lane-b.ts`) records `question` and `answered` entries from a MAWS process's live events only, keyed by `src = <project>/lane-b/<appSessionId>/<anchor>`; the journal's `src` is unique, and an answer waits for its question's entry (`requires`). The renderer joins an answer to its question by the anchor's form (`questionOf`, `src/renderer/src/channel/project.ts`). sminos writes every sheet question, a MAWS-run session's too, to its own project log from the record, the question and its outcome under one `src` of its own, `sheet:<session>:<id>` (`src`, `sminos_sheet_step.py`; the projection in `sminos_sheet.py`); where the machine's sminos daemon is enrolled, its host events reach the same journal as lane A entries under `<project>/<writer>/<seq>` (`#journal`, `src/endpoint/rl10/link.ts`), each event still carrying sminos's own `src` (the uplink passes the event and `host_body`, `sminos_plane.py`, keeps the field).
- **The notifier** on `question-sheet-c3` (`src/main/attention/notifier.ts`) announces a sheet item once per call and returns before any system notification for it, as for a terminal session's item; its comment leaves a MAWS-run session's to this child.

## Decisions settled at authoring

Each is a choice the composite left to this child. They are numbered in the F series (the charter's are D-, C2's K- and U-, C3's S-, C4's T-); the numbers not in this list (F9, F11 to F21, F28 to F30) belonged to the design in which MAWS delivered, are withdrawn, and are not used again. Where a decision makes a sentence of the composite false or incomplete, the sentence is in the last section. The interfaces they fix are §R, §S, §V, §W, §Y and §Z.

**The launch.**

- **F1 · A sheet launch is told by one fact the spec already carries: the served set's `question` is `engine`.** The launch line, the supplement, the spawn row, the prestart's comparison and an adopter all read `mawsTools` today, so no second fact can disagree with it. From this child on, "Default" on a host that lists F3's capability means the sheet where a sminos that knows the form runs, and the engine's built-in where it does not, as the composite's §9 says. Lost: a `questionSheet` flag on the spec, which is a second fact, a wire change, and one more thing an older host ignores silently.
- **F2 · The host sets `MAWS_QUESTION_SHEET`, in `buildEnvironment`, to `2:` and the session id `buildLaunch` resolved**, for a launch whose set's question is `engine` and whose spec names the sminos home (F4). Only `buildLaunch` knows the id of a prestarted engine, and an adopted prestart keeps it, so the variable a prestart was started with is the right one after Send. Lost: the spine writing it into `extraSettings.env`, the way `MAWS_NATIVE_TO_HUMAN` travels: the spine does not know a prestart's id, and a value in the spec is compared by `sameLaunchSpec`, so Send's id would never equal the prestart's and no Default chat would be adopted. **How it joins the prestart's key:** it does not need to. The comparison that decides an adoption is `sameLaunchSpec` over the whole spec; the variable is derived from two things the spec holds (the set and the home, both compared), a constant and the id the prestart itself minted (kept). `prestartKey` gains nothing.
- **F32 · The form is 2, and it is the host's constant.** The composite's §5.4 fixes the value's shape: the number names the delivery's form, so a plugin that would apply answers and never deliver them under a MAWS that rings (sminos as published, and `75d21fc93`) does not take the sheet. Form 1 was never set by any host: the value is `2:<id>` from the first host that sets the variable at all.
- **F3 · A host that sets the variable says so: the capability `engine.questionSheet`.** The spine reads it where it reads `engine.mawsTools` (`s.hostLists`), to choose the supplement (F6), to serve the override (F5), and before the teller tells a host its table or starts a session for the sheet (F36, F37). Without it a Default launch is what it is today. It says what a host will do for a launch it is asked for now; what a running process was launched with is read from that launch's own record (F31).
- **F4 · The spine names the sminos home, as `SMINOS_HOME` in the spec's `extraSettings.env`, for a launch whose set's question is `engine`.** The value is the one the sheet's reader was created with (`sminosHome(deps.env, shellEnv)` in `spine-root.ts`), so what a MAWS-run session's sminos writes and what MAWS reads are the same directory by construction, not by two resolutions agreeing. It is stable between a prestart and its Send, so the comparison holds. `DAEMON_HOME` is not passed: sminos reads `SMINOS_HOME` first. Every other launch's line is unchanged. A home that cannot be resolved (the test runner's refusal of the real one) names nothing, and then the host sets no variable either: a sheet whose record MAWS cannot find is one nobody could answer. That same rule closes the skew of an older `mawsd` over a newer host at a launch: a spine without this child never names the home, so that host gives its Default launches the engine's built-in, as today. Lost: admitting the two names through the host's allowlist, which passes the login shell's value and not the reader's (they differ wherever `mawsd`'s own environment names a home), and changes the environment of every launch. That a settings variable reaches sminos's own processes from their first start is measured (M2's P6).
- **F5 · A session that already has a sheet is launched as a sheet launch for that launch, and its record keeps its set.** Before `servedSet`, a resume asks the sheet service for the record of the engine session it resumes; when the record holds an open question, a pending outcome or a queued line (the model's `open`, `hidden`, `pending`, and `sent` entries of state `queued`, any of them), and the host lists F3's capability, the set served is the record's with `question: 'engine'`. Nothing is written back: D1 stores a set once, and the stored `maws` goes on meaning `ask` for every launch that finds no sheet, until C7 retires the value. A record this build cannot read, or one read as `busy`, leaves the stored tool, logged. A fork resumes under a new session id and an empty sheet, so it is never overridden. Known residue: a session whose conversation was sent `ask` keeps it in its requests after a launch that does not list it, and a call of it is answered by the engine's own "No such tool available" (the tool choice's P2); it is logged, not built around.
- **F6 · A sheet launch's supplement is a new text, supplement 5**, chosen where 3 and 4 are (§R has it word for word). It names sminos's tool "where it is listed" and the built-in otherwise, because MAWS cannot know whether the session's sminos registered the tool (not installed, hooks off, or one that does not know the form), and supplement 4's sentence that sminos's tool "is not available here" would be false in every session this child serves. A session recorded under supplement 4 and resumed as a sheet launch has its prompt rendered anew by `decideFlags`' existing rule.
- **F7 · Two sentences of the app say what "Default" is, and they change** (§R). The owner read them and let them stand as written (2026-11-27).
- **F31 · Whether a running process may be rung is read from its own launch, and bounded by what its rings do.** A host of before this child already launches `question: 'engine'` without the variable, and a host of this child that adopts such a process lists the capability though that engine never took the sheet: so the ringer's first test of a process is the launch's spawn row, whose environment's names must hold `MAWS_QUESTION_SHEET`, and it writes nothing for any other launch. The variable is what MAWS asked for, not proof that a plugin took it: a session resumed with an answer already pending, under a sminos that does not know the form or under none, would be rung with a bare sentence. M2's P8 found positive evidence, had before any prompt: the engine's `mcp_status` names a server `sminos` of source `plugin` whose tools include `AskUserQuestion` where the plugin took the sheet, and under MAWS's launch sminos gives `2:<id>` the sheet or no question tool at all. So the ringer requires it too, and a process that does not show it is never rung. F36's budget of rings that ran the model and landed nothing stays as the second bound, for a hook that ran past its ten seconds, and ends the ringing for that process after two.

**sminos (M1), a second pull request in doperpowers; the published set does not move.**

- **F8 · Under runner `maws` the tool delivers as it does in a terminal, by every route but its own idle prompt.** The result route and the prompt route carry the ready envelope, the notices and the contexts; a stored row of a route's kind lands its keys; a start searches the transcript for envelopes that landed before the process died; the step composes the main conversation's envelope. Four of the five tests of the runner that held this back go (in `take`, at the landing, in `afterStart`, in the step's `ready`); the fifth stays: the tool submits no prompt of its own there, because a plugin can attach no context to its own submit, does not know why its process was started, and its queued prompt is outside what a Stop in MAWS withdraws (the composite's §5.1). With the routes open, an ended subagent's open questions and a call's late outcome reach the main conversation in a MAWS-run session as in a terminal one (the composite's Acceptance 18, and the item C2's Status left for this child); doperpowers' TECH-DEBT row 54 keeps its other half, in both kinds of session. Of `75d21fc93`, the half that put the notices and contexts on a MAWS-run session's routes stays and is completed by this; the half that added a line per answered subagent question to a prompt carrying a block (`askedBy`, `steerContext`, `shownOutcomes`, the harness's `show`) is removed: the envelope is now the tool's own context and carries the question's words and the asker itself.
- **F33 · The mark is on the ring, and the tool drops one frame only: under runner `maws`, of origin `composer`, whose whole text is the sentence with its mark, when the envelope it would attach carries no answer.** The ring's text is the composite's sentence followed by one invisible character that no trimming removes, U+2060, written as an escape in both repositories; the tool's test is exact equality of the hook's `e.text` with that constant, and it normalises nothing. Every other prompt enters as any prompt does and carries what is ready: one of any other origin (a scheduled prompt, a task's notification, a peer's message), every prompt under runner `terminal`, and a person's message of the same words in any form and beside any block, since a person types no mark. MAWS's three paths that write a person's words take the mark out of a person's text that holds it (§V), so a pasted ring is a person's message too. The mark is on the ring and not on the person's message because of what M2's P9 measured: the engine joins and trims a message's text blocks before the hook reads them, so a line break appended to a person's message of those words was gone whenever the message went as blocks; the hook took it for a ring and dropped it, an attached image with it. An escape on the person's side is safe only if MAWS normalises text exactly as the engine does, and the cells show that it does not do so simply. With the mark on the ring, a typed sentence cannot match whatever the engine does, and an engine that one day strips the mark gives a ring that enters as any prompt does, with the envelope if one is ready and bare if not (bounded, F36): never a person's message dropped. Lost: the line break on the person's message, this document's text until P9. A ring is filled when the block of the envelope ready for the main conversation names an answer, one applied at a start (`acrossStop`) included. The hook reads that envelope and nothing else: the view lists only the first two hundred pending outcomes, of every recipient, and cannot say what one envelope holds. And so that an owed answer is always in the envelope a ring would get, the step packs the main conversation's envelope under runner `maws` with the batches that hold an answer first; a terminal session's order stays C2's, the batches that wake first. An envelope of dismissals alone is not a reason for a turn (the composite's §5.2), so the ring is dropped and the dismissals wait for a prompt that enters or a tool result. A dropped ring takes nothing, so a notice or a start's context that was waiting still waits. The hook orders no step and waits for none: it has ten seconds for everything (B3), and a hook that passed them would let the frame in bare. An answer the step has applied and the mod has not yet adopted is therefore not ready at that instant: the ring is dropped, the answer stays pending, and MAWS rings again (F36). The drop's reason is a constant both repositories hold, since MAWS reads it to keep the drop out of the transcript (F39).
- **F34 · A turn the person stopped is marked in the tool, in both runners, at the Stop and for what the Stop overtook.** At the end of a main turn that was aborted, every key then pending for the main conversation joins `gen.noIdle`, the set the idle route already will not start a turn for; and the outcomes of a step that was ordered before that end and answers after it join the set when its view is adopted, so an answer committed just before the Stop does not reopen the idle route by arriving late. That set's existing rules are then the composite's Stop rule: nothing in it opens the idle route, a fresh answer does and takes the rest along, and a prompt that enters carries them all. A turn aborted because a message preempted it needs no case of its own: the entering prompt carries everything. It closes a gap of C2's as built (a later dismissal, a reroute or a `landed` retry opened the idle route after a Stop).
- **F10 · Nothing of the set changes.** Its steer example and the inbox's `delivered` line were written for the design in which MAWS composed and acknowledged; nothing composes or appends them now, and they stay in version 1 until the set's next version removes them (the composite's X3).

**The ring, and a session with no process.**

- **F35 · The ring is one constant frame, written by the host as a steer of its own kind, when the host itself finds every condition true at the instant of writing.** Its whole text is the composite's sentence with its mark (F33). It is never written while a main turn is open or a frame of anyone's is unstarted: a frame folded into a running turn carries nothing (B1). It is the host's own kind of steer in the queue: not published among the pending steers a window shows, not holdable, withdrawn and dropped by a Stop that finds it unstarted, known again to a host that adopts the session, and settled by a transition of the queue's own whenever the engine ends it without a turn (the tool's drop; the engine's cancelling, discarding or refusing it before start), which lets a person's message queued behind it go out (§V). The host follows each ring to its end from the engine's own rows, in the process that wrote it: unstarted, started, and then dropped, gone before start, stopped, or ran the model. MAWS keeps nothing else of a ring: whatever becomes of the frame, the outcome is landed by the tool or still pending.
- **F36 · The spine says what is owed; the host decides when to write.** Each fact is decided where it is known at the instant it changes. The record is the spine's to read, so the spine's part (the teller) is a level and not an event: for each session, the keys owed, told to the engine host whole each time the table changes, at each welcome and at the teller's own start (`engine.sheetOwed`, §V). Whether a session is ready, idle, in a Stop's withdrawal, has a ring of MAWS's unended, how its last ring ended, which launch it is: each of those is the host's, so the whole predicate is read in the host, in the process that owns the queue, in one synchronous stretch that ends in the write (the ringer). Nothing is asked across the boundary in order to ring: no reply is waited for, and no fact can be stale but the table, which is harmless stale, since a ring for a set that has since emptied is dropped by the tool and one for a set that grew carries everything. **The predicate**: an answer is owed to the main conversation; the launch is a sheet launch (F31); not every owed answer is in the Stop's hold; fewer than four rings were written since the owed set last changed; the time is not before the deadline; the process's budget is not spent; and the session is ready, with no main turn open, no frame of anyone's unstarted, no Stop in its withdrawal and no ring of its own unended. A dismissal alone never satisfies it. **The Stop's hold** is taken where the Stop is accepted: the keys the host had been told are owed at that instant, to the main conversation or to any other recipient or still queued, whether or not any turn then ends; and the same at the end of a turn that ends aborted when no Stop of the host's accounts for it and no message preempted it. A turn a message preempted holds nothing. An unstarted ring the Stop withdraws had its answers in the table, so they are in the hold. The hold ends where the host writes a person's message to the engine, and nowhere else. It is kept for the session, in the host's memory and in the session's state file in the host's store, so it outlasts the engine process, the host's own exit or replacement, and `mawsd`; a session with no process whose owed answers are all held is not started (F37). An answer the host had not been told of when the Stop was accepted is not held: it was accepted within the time the record takes to reach the host, it rings, and nothing is lost either way. **The budgets** are the ringer's own, for one process, and every fact that resets one is applied when it arrives, before any test reads it. A table in which the session's owed set has lost or gained a key is progress: the count of rings goes to zero and the deadline to now (an envelope carries what fits, and a long sheet leaves in instalments). A ring's end sets the deadline 2, then 15, then 120 seconds on, and a fourth ring without progress is the last until the set changes. Separately, a ring whose turn ran the model, with no key gone since, counts against the process, and is counted in the same pass that would write the next ring, so no ring is written past the bound: after two, the host rings that process no more, and its answers leave by the next prompt or tool result (F31). A table in which a key has gone resets that count; a dropped ring is not counted; a new launch starts with an empty ringer by construction. Lost: the reconciler on the spine (this document until its re-check), which had to learn the Stop, the launch and each ring's end from the reply to a write, and so learned each too late: a Stop with nothing owed was first seen at the next answer's ring and marked that answer; a release before a deadline lost the mark; two rings could end between passes; and a ring the engine discarded had no end to report. The other sound shape, the host publishing each of those facts as state and the spine writing conditionally against the last fact it had evaluated, keeps a process boundary in the middle of one decision, and was not taken.
- **F37 · An answer owed to a session with no process starts it when MAWS ran it last, with nothing sent.** This part is the teller's, since it turns on files. Owed, for a start, is every answer the next start will inherit: queued in the inbox and not cancelled, or pending in the record whatever its recipient, since the start hands a dead subagent's answer to the main conversation (the composite's §6). A dismissal starts none. "MAWS ran it last" is read without the host, which may be parked: the index's record says whether MAWS ever launched the session (`readRecordedRoute(record.gateway)` is not null) and where its transcript is, and the newest record of that transcript that names an entrypoint names MAWS's (`sdk-ts`); a terminal that has written since leaves `cli` there. An archived session and one whose transcript cannot be read or names no entrypoint are not started. Then, and only then, the host: the table is told afresh, and its reply names the sessions whose owed answers are all in the Stop's hold; such a session is not started, and its keys are spent. The start is an acquisition that requires no message be waiting (`requireEmptyMessage`): a message the person left behind an unanswered card is theirs to send, and an acquisition refused on that ground spends nothing and leaves the message where it was. A key is spent, for the run of the spine, only by a start that ended ready, by a refusal that is known to be final, or by the hold; everything that may pass or that the outcome does not tell apart is tried again after 5, 30 and 120 seconds, and after those waits for the next change of the record or the index (§W classifies each outcome). No time is compared and nothing is written.
- **F38 · The teller may wake a parked host, in a fixed order, and nothing else of the sheet may.** A spine loaded only by the engine host's events refuses every dispatch to a host that is not ready, the capability's own read among them, so the order is: first everything that needs no host (the record, the index, the transcript's last writer); only for a session that passes all of it, the statement of need (`SpineHosts.want('sheet')`), which marks the load wanted and resolves at once from a welcome already current (a host running for another session is the common case), and otherwise subscribes to the link before it asks `mawsd` for the engine host; then the capability, read from that welcome; then the table, and the hold's answer with it; then the acquisition. The ring is then the host's own. A host that comes up without the capability is an older build: nothing is launched for the sheet, the answer waits for the session's next start, and the teller does not wake that host again for the sheet until its link changes. Reading a record, drawing a sheet, and deciding from the files not to start a session make no host call at all; a table is told only to a host that is ready, and wakes none. One wake starts nothing: a held answer of a session with no process, when the host is parked, since the hold is the host's to read; the host is woken, answers that it is held, and the keys are spent for the run of the spine. What this does not reach is `mawsd` with its services not loaded: nobody there is watching the sheet's files, and an answer given then is seen when the services next load. It is logged as a limit.
- **F22 · Nothing of the sheet is in what a process exit would lose.** `SessionMaws.pending` is not touched; a test pins that a session with open sheet questions, an answer pending and a ring unstarted reads zero from it.
- **F40 · The skews, stated.** A sminos that does not know form 2 (as published at doperpowers `40b79a7d1`, and `75d21fc93`) takes no sheet under `2:<id>`: a new session has the engine's built-in and supplement 5's second sentence; a session resumed with an answer already pending is not rung, since its process does not show the tool (F31). A host without the capability launches Default as today, the spine appends supplement 4 and tells it no table, and it is never woken twice for the sheet. A `mawsd` replaced by an older build while a sheet launch runs tells no table: the host goes on from the last one it was told, which costs at most three rings the tool drops for a set that has emptied, and an answer given then stays pending and leaves with the person's next message or the agent's next tool result, since the tool's routes need no ring. A host replaced by an older build strips the hold from a state file it rewrites: the hold is lost, and a held answer is owed like any other.

**What draws, and what else reads.**

- **F23 · A MAWS-run session's rows draw from the record while it lives and from the transcript's attachments after, and a ring is the sheet's line only on MAWS's own evidence.** Nothing of a delivery crosses the wire (B5), so the live route has only MAWS's own frame. C3's `SheetRow` already lays the record over a row's lines, and its reader already finds an envelope in a hook's context among a file's rows; neither changes. What is added is the ring, and what makes a row a ring is never its words alone: on the live route and from MAWS's journal, the uuid MAWS wrote as a ring; among a file's rows, the sheet's envelope attached to that prompt on a record MAWS's own entrypoint wrote, since in a terminal session a person can type the sentence while an answer is ready and the tool attaches the envelope to it. A row that has only the sentence, or the sentence and an envelope under another entrypoint, is a person's message: in a transcript from before this child, in a terminal session, wherever someone typed it.
- **F39 · A ring that was filled heads its turn with one line; a ring that was dropped leaves nothing a person can see.** The line is C3's notice row for a sheet's delivery: where the history being drawn holds the tool's context for that turn (a file's rows), it names the ids as C3 words it; where it holds only the ring (the live route), it reads "Answers from your sheet", which stands until the owner reads it. A dropped ring leaves no row, live or rebuilt from the file, no notice of the drop (the engine files its warning in the transcript and sends it on the wire: both projections pass over a warning whose drop carries the tool's own reason; what the file's record carries of it is W2's to record), no unread mark, no notification and no attention item. The session's phase should not show it either; whether the normaliser can hold a ring's tentative start until the engine has admitted or dropped it, without a change to how a turn is opened, is read in M5, and if it cannot, a phase that flickers for under a second on a ring that found nothing is logged as a limit.
- **F24 · A MAWS-run session's sheet item is announced as an asynchronous `ask`'s is**: once per call (C3's S11), in the app while it is focused and by a system notification while it is not. A terminal session's stays in-app only. The test is the record's holder (`self`), read where the notifier reads the record.

## When MAWS rings

The whole of MAWS's part in a delivery, fixed before it is built. It is small because the tool owns the rest: there is no state of an outcome here, only when to write one sentence. One rule shapes it (F36): a fact is decided where it is known at the instant it changes, so nothing here is learned from the reply to a write.

### Who acts, and on what

| actor | where | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| the tool | sminos's mod and step, inside the session | applies inbox lines; composes the envelope; puts it on a route; lands it by the stored row; searches the transcript at a start |
| a surface | the app, the page, `sminos sheet` | appends answer, dismiss and retract lines through the door |
| the teller | the spine | reads the record and the index; tells the host the table of what is owed; for a session with no process, reads who ran it last and asks the session service to start it |
| the ringer | the engine host, one for each process | reads the predicate and writes the ring; follows each ring to its end; keeps the two budgets and the deadline |
| the host | the engine host, for each session id | keeps the table it was told and the Stop's hold; takes the hold where a Stop is accepted and ends it where a person's message is written; withdraws an unstarted ring at a Stop |
| the engine | the pinned binary | gives the ring to the tool's prompt hook; runs the turn, or reports the drop |

### What is kept, and where

| name | where | what it is | how it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `main`, `rest` | the host's memory, for each session id | the session's keys in the table, as the teller last told them | replaced whole by each `engine.sheetOwed`; a session the table does not name owes nothing |
| `hold` | the host's memory and the session's state file | the Stop's hold | grows by `main` and `rest` where a Stop is accepted, and at an aborted end that no Stop of the host's and no message accounts for; emptied where a person's message is written to the engine |
| `ring` | the ringer | the ring not yet ended: its uuid, and whether the engine has started it | set before the write; cleared at its end |
| `still` | the ringer | rings written since `main` last lost or gained a key | +1 at a write; 0 when a table changes `main` |
| `deadline` | the ringer | no ring before this time | at a ring's end: now plus the wait for `still`; now when a table changes `main` |
| `ran` | the ringer | the last ring's turn ran the model, and no key has left `main` since | set at such an end; cleared when a table shows a key gone, and when it is counted |
| `bare` | the ringer | rings of this process that ran the model and landed nothing | +1 when `ran` is counted, in the step that would write the next ring; 0 when a table shows a key gone |
| `tried` | the teller's memory | keys a start was spent for | F37 |

A new launch has a new ringer. An adopter's is new too, but for the unstarted ring it reads from the journal. The table and the hold belong to the session, not to a process.

### The predicate

Read by the ringer in this order, in one synchronous stretch that ends in the write. `main` and `hold` are the session's.

| # | condition | read from | when it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `main` is not empty | the table | nothing; a dismissal is in no table |
| 2 | the launch is a sheet launch, and its process shows sminos's tool (`mcp_status`, P8) | the launch's spawn row; the session's noted MCP servers | nothing for this process; while no answer of `mcp_status` is in, nothing yet, and its noting considers again |
| 3 | not every key of `main` is in `hold` | the host | nothing, until a person's message is written or the table gains a key |
| 4 | `still` is below `RING_STILL_MAX` (4) | the ringer | nothing, until a table changes `main` |
| 5 | the time is not before `deadline` | the ringer | one timer at `deadline` |
| 6 | `ran`, if set, is counted into `bare` and cleared; then `bare` is below `RING_BARE_MAX` (2) | the ringer | nothing more for this process |
| 7 | the process is ready; no main turn is open; the queue holds no frame the engine has neither started nor dropped, and no submission whose turn has not ended; no Stop is in its withdrawal; `ring` is empty | the session and its queue | one timer, 2 seconds on, doubling to 60 while this row keeps failing |
| 8 | the write | the queue | `still` rises and `ring` is set before the write; a write that fails takes both back and arms row 7's timer |

Rows 1 to 6 read what was applied when it arrived ("What moves the ringer", below); no row learns anything during the pass, and nothing is between row 7 and the write.

### What moves the ringer

Each is a call inside the host's own process to the one `consider()`. None crosses a process boundary, so none can be lost on the way; the timers are there so that a call nobody thought of costs a wait and not a ring.

| what happens | what is applied first | then |
|---|---|---|
| a table arrives (`engine.sheetOwed`) | the session's `main` and `rest` are replaced. If `main` lost or gained a key: `still` 0, `deadline` now. If it lost one: `bare` 0, `ran` cleared | `consider()` |
| the process becomes ready | nothing | `consider()` |
| a main turn ends | the ring's end, if the turn names the ring (below) | `consider()` |
| the queue settles a frame (a submission dropped; the ring settled) | nothing | `consider()` |
| a Stop is accepted | `hold` grows by `main` and `rest`: in memory at once, then saved | nothing: the withdrawal is running |
| a Stop's withdrawal ends | nothing | `consider()` |
| a person's message is written to the engine | `hold` is emptied: in memory at once, then saved | nothing: its turn follows |
| an answer of `mcp_status` is noted | the evidence of row 2 | `consider()` |
| the ringer's timer | nothing | `consider()` |

A ring's end is applied where the engine's row for it is read:

| the engine's rows for the ring | its end | what is applied |
|---|---|---|
| `command_lifecycle` `started` | none yet | `ring` is marked started |
| the hook's drop: the warning whose `prompt_submit_outcome.kind` is `dropped_by_hook` while the ring is the prompt last started, or the ring's `result` with `num_turns` 0 | dropped | `ring` cleared; `deadline` set; the queue's `ringSettled` |
| `command_lifecycle` `cancelled`, `discarded` or `refused` with no `started` before it, whatever caused it | gone | `ring` cleared; `deadline` set; the queue forgets the frame and considers its head (it releases nothing while a Stop is in its withdrawal) |
| a `result` that names it, `num_turns` above 0, not aborted | ran | `ring` cleared; `ran` set; `deadline` set |
| a `result` that names it, aborted | stopped | `ring` cleared; `deadline` set; `ran` not set |
| the process exits | lost | the ringer ends with the process |

`deadline` after an end is now plus `RING_STILL_MS[still - 1]` for `still` 1 to 3. For `still` 4 it is now: row 4 already holds, and no timer is set.

**The table's command.** `src/shared/ipc/` (`names/engine.ts`, `schema/engine.ts`, `grades/engine.ts`), owner `engine-host`, deadline shape `immediate` (`src/engine-host/ipc.ts`), declared and handled beside `engine.sessions`, the pattern for a command that needs no session's row:

It is a level, as `host.interest` is: the whole table each time, replacing the last one; a session it does not name owes nothing. `held` names each session of this table whose keys, `main` and `rest` together, are not empty and are all in its hold. **In `src/engine-host/queue.ts`:**

`stopOwns` is why one Stop takes one hold: the count `stopping` falls when the interrupt is answered, which can be before the interrupted turn ends, and `stoppedTurn` covers the time between. A steer with `host`:

- tells the ringer its uuid (`registered`, a dependency of the queue) when it is registered, before the write, since the writer drains the engine's rows for that uuid before the write's promise resolves;
- is in no `QueueState.steers`; `hold` and `cancelSteer` refuse its uuid as they refuse one that already started;
- is refused while a Stop is in progress (`stopping` above zero), under the predicate's own test of the same fact;
- is withdrawn by every Stop, whatever the Stop's options: `stop` asks the engine to cancel an unstarted host steer between its pause and its interrupt, in `holdAll` where the Stop holds the person's steers and by the same request where it does not (`hold` false); when the engine cancelled it, the queue journals `cancel` and forgets it, where it would hold a person's steer; `settleAfterExit` abandons it with the rest;
- at a `command.lifecycle` of `cancelled`, `discarded` or `refused` for it, whoever caused that, is forgotten, and the queue considers its head as it does after a submission the engine dropped before start (under its ordinary gates: not paused, idle, ready; so nothing is released inside a Stop's pause). No notice is posted for it;
- when the hook drops it, is settled by `ringSettled`: the same forgetting and the same consideration of the head. No `turn.ended` is needed for it and none is published (§Y);
- is known to an adopter: the fold reads a `sheet_ring` row and the `out` row of its uuid, and a steer of that uuid that the journal shows neither started, dropped nor named by a result is restored as the host's own kind and given to the new ringer (`restore`), so a Stop after the adoption withdraws it and no second ring is written beside it.

Its memory is the queue's; the queue's persisted and published shapes do not change. `ask`'s steer is untouched.

**A person's frame is never a ring, and ends the hold.** The ring carries the mark and a person's words do not, so nothing is added to anyone's message. In the three paths that write the person's own words (`submit`, the `session.steer` command's steer, `promote`; a person's message from another machine arrives by the same paths), a content whose text, its text blocks taken together, holds both `SHEET_SENTENCE` and `SHEET_MARK` has every `SHEET_MARK` taken out of its text before it is written, as a string or in each block; the queue entry keeps what the person typed. That covers a pasted ring however the engine joins and trims the blocks, since `e.text` cannot equal the ring's text without a mark in it. In the same three paths, when the write has resolved, `SheetOwed.personWrote`. Nothing else ends a hold: not `ask`'s steer, not the ring, not a turn the engine started.

No test of one repository reads the other's source. It reads the file backwards from its end in blocks, takes whole lines only, and stops at the first line that parses and names an entrypoint. `OWN_ENTRYPOINT` moves from `src/engine-host/fs/holders.ts` to `src/shared/engine/` so the host, the spine and the transcript's reader (§Y) read one constant.

Started in `startSpine` (`src/services/spine-root.ts`) where the sheet service is, when there is one, and wired there: `dispatch` is `SpineHosts`', `onLink` is the link's state and `hosts.onStreaming('engine-host', …)`, `welcomed` reads the link's snapshot (`peer('engine-host')`) and never dispatches (the session service's `hostLists` does dispatch when there is no welcome, and is not used here), `want` is new (below), a record is the index's (`index.sessions.get`), the transcript's path is its row's `transcript_path`, and `acquire` is `SessionService.acquire`. It writes nothing to disk and hears no event of a session: what a session is doing is the host's to know (F36).

**One pass** (passes never overlap; a trigger during a pass owes one more). What runs it: the teller's start; a change of a record (`sheet.onChange`); a change of the index (`sessions.onChange`); a welcome, and the link's leaving ready; one of its own retries.

1. **The table.** For each record `sheet.list()` names that has an app session: the index's record. None, archived, or holder `terminal` or `background_job`: no row. Otherwise the model: `main` is the keys of the answers pending with recipient `main`; `rest` is the keys of the answers pending for any other recipient, and of the answers queued in the inbox that no queued retract cancels. Both empty: no row. A record that cannot be read keeps the row this teller last built for it, if it built one. A list longer than the command's bound is cut to it and logged.
2. **Telling it.** The host welcomed and listing `engine.questionSheet`: if no table has been acknowledged under this welcome, or the table differs from the one that was, `engine.sheetOwed`. One is in flight at a time. Acknowledged: remembered, for comparison only. Not taken (an error, a timeout): tried again after `TELL_RETRY_MS`, while the link stays ready. No welcome, or no capability: nothing; the welcome is a trigger. A table is never the reason to wake a host.
3. **The starts.** For each row whose session's holder is `none`, the start below; the others are the host's.

**The start**, for one session with no process. `owed` is its `main` and `rest` together. The order is part of the rule: nothing of the host is touched before step c.

- a. Every key of `owed` in `tried`: stop.
- b. `readRecordedRoute(record.gateway)` null: stop. `lastEntrypoint(transcript)` not `OWN_ENTRYPOINT`: stop.
- c. `await host.want('sheet')`. Then the welcome's capability: absent, remember it for this link and stop (an older host; nothing is launched, and the next pass under this link stops before `want`).
- d. Tell the table as it is now, whether or not it differs from the last, and read `held` from that reply and no earlier one. The session is in it: its keys join `tried`, one log line, stop. The telling failed: as a failure that may pass (the table's last row).
- e. `acquire(appSessionId, 'resume', { requireEmptyMessage: true })`, and by its outcome:

| outcome | what it means | the keys | what follows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ready` (launched or joined) | the session is up | spent | the ringer of the new process has the table and rings |
| `refused`, reason `busy`, for a message the person left waiting (`PENDING_MESSAGE_REFUSAL`, `src/services/sessions/start-guards.ts`) | a start here would send that message | not spent | nothing; the person's own continuation starts the session |
| `needs_trust`, `missing_directory`, `archived`, `invalid` | final until the person acts | spent | logged |
| `held` | another process has taken the session | spent | the next pass finds it a terminal's |
| `relay_unreachable`; `failed` (it covers "still stopping", a readiness that timed out, and what the setup could not name); `refused` for any other reason; a thrown error of the transport or the host; a telling that failed in step d | may pass, or cannot be told apart | not spent | a pass after the next of `START_RETRIES_MS`; after the third, the next change of the record or of the index begins the sequence again |

A session with no process cannot be stopped and cannot be written to, so its hold cannot change between step d's reply and step e.

**The wake** (F38). `src/mawsd/spine-seam.ts` and `src/mawsd/spine-hosts.ts`: the adapter gains `want(reason): Promise<void>`.
