- **Purpose:** A session on another machine stops behaving worse than one on this Mac in the places where it does today. Its tab says "Connecting" rather than "offline" while the machine link comes up, keeps the transcript on screen and the reader's place through every reconnect, and says when it is loading. Its row reads Working when its turn starts, not ten seconds later. From this Mac the owner can fork it (from its menu, from an answer, from `/fork`), rename it, and archive and unarchive it, with no Touch ID on a machine they own; a Stop that cannot reach the machine says so; the Touch ID sheet names the machine by its name. One contract (UC-7) settles, for every act on a session, whether it works on another machine or says why not, so the next act somebody adds cannot fail silently there.

  **UC-7.1 The rule.** Every act a person can take on a session is in exactly one of three states for a session on another machine: it works, carried by an offer; main answers it itself; or it is declared unavailable, and then its control is drawn disabled with one sentence. No control is drawn enabled where the act cannot work now, and no act ends without either its effect or one sentence that names the reason. Disabling a control is never the authorisation: the other machine decides every request again (remote layer design §5.5).

  **UC-7.2 The shape of one offer.** An offer is these facts, and a child that asks for one states them:

  | fact | where it lives | what it says |
  |---|---|---|
  | the command | `src/shared/ipc/schema/` | its name and its input as it crosses the link; a new field on an offered command counts as a new offer |
  | the grade | `src/shared/ipc/grades/` | what the caller must hold: a rank on the session (`view` < `converse` < `approve` < `manage`) or a right on the machine (`spawn`, `shell`, `machine-admin`) |
  | the row | `OFFERS` in `src/endpoint/offers.ts` | a `call` (a read) or an `act`; who runs it (`engine-host` or `machine`); how its session is found (`appSessionId`, `each-session`, …); optionally a narrower input (`accepts`) and `owners` (the machine's owners alone) |
  | the device's half | `src/main/lane-b/remote.ts` | for an act, one of two sets. `ENGINE_HOST_ACTS`: sealed for the session's live launch, so a tab must hold the session, and a released session is resumed first. `MACHINE_ACTS`: placed by its arguments, so it works with no tab open and resumes nothing |
  | the capability | `ENDPOINT_CAPABILITIES` in `src/endpoint/capabilities.ts`, and `REMOTE_NEEDS` beside the declared set | the name an endpoint lists in its handshake once it serves the offer; the device sends the offer only to a machine that lists it, and the renderer reads the machine's list from the roster before drawing the control. `RL2_PROTOCOL.minor` moves by one |
  | the words | `src/shared/ipc/remote-unavailable.ts` | what the control shows when the act cannot work, and what a refusal says (UC-7.5) |

  A request a machine makes of a device (show a pane) is not an offer. It travels the other way, on the client port (`src/endpoint/client-port.ts`, behind `rl2.client-port`), to the device that sent the turn's input, and the device answers once. U12 adds its kind there.

  Fork is one act. Its input is today's: `{appSessionId, target, message, rewindTo}`. `rewindTo` is the id of one of the person's prompts, and the fork holds the conversation before that prompt; `null` forks the whole conversation. `message`, when given, is sent as the fork's first message. The renderer's one entry is `forkSession` in `src/renderer/src/sessions/record-ops.ts`; U3 and U4 call it and decide nothing about it but whether the fork's tab opens (the menu's Fork opens it; the engine's own `/fork [prompt]` is "Copy this conversation into a new background session and keep working here", `~/claude-code-bundle/2.1.322/cli.pretty.js:430960`). "Fork from here" on an answer passes the first prompt after that answer, or `null` when none follows, which is Claude Desktop's rule (`readable/app-5.1231.2/web/app/v1/c96d752d0-CjYhuD9j.js:39351`, called from `forkAfterAssistantTurn` at `:142000`). On one of the person's own prompts Claude Desktop forks before that prompt and puts its text in the fork's composer (`:128270`); MAWS's edit-and-fork is that case, and sends the edit.

  A fork is recorded with no starter of its own, as a fork made at the machine is, so it belongs to the machine's owners. That is why only they may fork over the link in this wave: a member who merely holds a `spawn` grant would get a fork they do not own, because the proof that makes a starter the owner is tied to the name `session.spawn` (`SPAWN_ACTIONS` in `apps/org-protocol/src/attest.ts`, read by other machines and by sminos).

  *Not on this route, with the reason.* A terminal session on another machine gets none of Fork, Archive and Rename: on this Mac a terminal session is its terminal's (its menu has only the opens, and an archive answers "held"), and a link act finds its session by a MAWS session id, which a terminal session on another machine does not have on this side.   **UC-7.5 What a control shows, and what a refusal says.** One function in `src/shared/ipc/remote-unavailable.ts` produces both, so every child words them alike. `<Act>` is the control's own label; `<machine>` is the machine's name.

  Before the act, the control is disabled with:

  | when | words |
  |---|---|
  | the act is still declared unavailable | `Not on another machine yet: <what>` (unchanged) |
  | the machine's build does not list the capability | `Can’t <act> on <machine> yet. Update MAWS on <machine> and try again.` |
  | the machine is offline | `<Act> is unavailable while <machine> is offline.` |
  | the link is connecting or reconnecting (menu acts only; Send and Stop are held, see #563) | `Connecting to <machine>…` / `Reconnecting to <machine>…` |
  | Fork, and the member is known not to be an owner of the machine | `Only <machine>’s owners can fork a session there.` |
  | Fork, and the session's turn runs | `Wait for the turn to end, or stop it first` (the composer's existing words) |

  Archive, Rename and Unarchive are drawn only for a session the member owns (the listing's `mine`), since nobody else can hold `manage`.

  After the act, one sentence: `Not <done>: <reason>`, with `<done>` one of forked, archived, unarchived, renamed, stopped, dismissed, sent. It is a notice for a menu or composer act, and the sheet's error line for an act made from a sheet.

  | the machine's answer | `<reason>` |
  |---|---|
  | `offline`, or it never received the act | `<machine> is offline` |
  | `not-offered` | `update MAWS on <machine> and try again` |
  | `owners-only` | `only <machine>’s owners can` |
  | `grade` | for a `spawn` act `you can’t start sessions on <machine>`; for `manage` `only the session’s owners can`; otherwise `you can read this session but not act on it` |
  | `step-up` | `the confirmation was cancelled` |
  | `read-only` | `<machine> is in read-only mode` |
  | `ceiling` | `<machine> doesn’t start sessions in <mode> from another machine` |
  | `unavailable`, `restart`, `busy`, `state-too-old` | `<machine> is busy; try again in a moment` |
  | the machine ran it and it failed | the machine's own sentence (a fork: "the session has a turn running; fork it once the turn ends") |
  | anything else | `<machine> refused it (<code>)` |

## O-11b · A transcript whose first read failed loads by itself
- **Today:** When the engine host's connection closes while a pane's first read is out (a restart, an update's handover), the read fails as "engine-host `<reason>` before answering session.events" (`src/shared/host/host-supervisor.ts`, `failSent`). The pane shows a red line, "Could not load this transcript · …", with Retry (`HYDRATION.failed` in `transcript/model/copy.ts`, drawn by `transcript/view/Transcript.tsx`). Nothing reads again when the host is back: the local source watches the host only to close a process it lost (`transcript/source/ipc.ts`, `onHostLoss`). Only a session MAWS runs on another machine re-reads at its link's return (`lane-b/transcript-hold.ts`).
- **After:** A first read that failed because the engine host was away is tried again at the host's next ready, once per ready. Until then the pane shows "Loading…" (O-39). If no ready comes within 15 s, or the second read fails too, the pane reads "Couldn’t load this conversation" with Retry; the reason stays in the line's hover title and in the log, off the screen. A later ready still loads it and clears the line. Routes: a session MAWS runs and a terminal session on this Mac, whose transcripts both come through this read; a session on another machine heals at its link's return (#565).
- **Why this, not that:** The failure is the app's own restart, not the person's problem, and the remote path already heals itself. Showing the error at once with Retry (today) asks the person to do what the app can see for itself. A retry on a timer was rejected: the host's ready is the event that makes a retry worth making, and one retry per ready cannot loop against a host that keeps failing.
- **Not included:** The tab-strip defect in the same observation is O-03's (U7).

## O-39 · Connecting and loading on another machine: three visible faults, and one measurement
- **Today:** The owner: "connection and loading on remote machine being extremely slow … takes way too long". Three faults are in the code. (1) New chat's project chooser on another machine waits for two reads together, the machine's projects and its home folder's first page, and keeps nothing between opens (`readPlaces` in `sessions/remote-setup.ts`; `RemoteMenu` in `composer/frame/ProjectRow.tsx`; unchanged on `registry-app`, which already takes a registry project's place on a machine from the registry "with no read over the link" in `readMachines`). (2) Until a link is up, every tab and row of that machine says "offline": the roster's states are `connecting`, `up` and `down`, and `selectOffline` in `stores/machines.ts` is "not up", so the seconds after every launch and reconnect read as a fault. (3) Once up, a pane with nothing to draw is blank while its history is read; for a live session main reads the whole launch over the link in pages and assembles them before the pane paints (`src/main/lane-b/remote-history.ts`). How long the real wait is has never been measured on real machines: P5's bound of 1.5 s from open to first row was only ever held on CI over loopback with a stub engine and a one-turn history.
- **After:**
  - *The chooser.* It opens with the projects it already knows for that machine: the registry's locations there, and the last listing read in this run. The machine's project list replaces them when it answers. The folder read no longer holds the projects back; "New project" and "Don't work in a project" stay disabled until it answers. "Reading `<machine>`'s projects…" shows only when nothing is known yet.
  - *The words while a link is not up.* One display phase per machine, derived in `app/machine-mark.ts` from the roster entry:

    | roster state | the machine was up earlier in this run | in that state for | phase | words |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | `up` | | | up | none |
    | `connecting` | | | connecting | `Connecting to <machine>…` |
    | `down` | yes | under 60 s | reconnecting | `Reconnecting to <machine>…` |
    | `down` | yes | 60 s or more | offline | `<machine> is offline · this session reconnects when it’s back` |
    | `down` | no | any | offline | the same |
    | `down`, and the machine removed this Mac | | | removed | `This Mac is no longer allowed on <machine>` (today's words) |

    Sidebar rows and tabs are greyed, with "offline" in the chip's place, only in the offline and removed phases. The 60 s is the hold an act already gets (`REMOTE_ACT_PENDING_MS`), so the words turn to "offline" at the moment acts start to be refused as offline.
  - *A loading pane.* A pane with no rows to draw shows one quiet line in its middle: "Opening on `<machine>`…" while main reads the session's record there (today's words, kept), then "Loading…" once the history read has been out for 300 ms. A link phase's words take that place while the link is not up. On this Mac the same "Loading…" rule applies and normally never shows.
  - *The measurement.* Three log lines land with this child, and U14 runs the sitting (Probes and checks, 1).
- **Why this, not that:** The three faults are defects whatever the measurement says, so they are fixed now; redesigning how history is read before knowing where the time goes would be hasty. The alternative for the words, keeping "offline" for every state that is not up, is what P5 ruled and is true to the roster, but it tells the owner several times a day that a healthy machine is gone. A timer to turn `connecting` into "offline" is not needed: the link itself leaves `connecting` for `down` when its first attempt fails (`src/main/machines/remote/link.ts`). Claude Desktop words the same state as a message and keeps the conversation ("Claude Code on {host} is offline. If {host} is asleep or lost its connection, this session reconnects when it’s back.", `readable/app-5.1231.2/i18n-web.json:17866`, id `Np/jhGUiAt`); the offline line follows it.
- **Not included:** Reading a long live session's history newest window first (it waits for the measurement). The link's own connect path. A fourth roster state from main: the phase is derived in the renderer so that the many "is it up" checks do not change.

## #565 · At a link's return, history is read after the live sessions resume, and a failed transcript heals
- **Today:** P5 made a live remote tab resume by a replay from its held position. Three cases are outside it (the ticket's): a tab that never held anything (restored at launch, or opened while the machine was away) reads its whole history the moment the roster says up, beside the live streams' resubscriptions; a visible tab of an idle session does the same at every return; and when nothing subscribes after a return, a held transcript that failed during the outage is not read again. A first attempt at a signal was built and reverted in P5 (`76ccfafec`, reverted by `276440763`): it was said while main was still opening the session, so the re-read overlapped the opening's own subscription.
- **After:** Main says one word per machine and link generation, *settled*: every subscription the return reopened has been answered, and no session is still being opened; or there was nothing to reopen. A cold tab and an idle tab make their history read at settled, or 2 s after up if settled has not come. A held transcript that is not whole decides at settled: one that failed is read again, once. With #563 the pane is mounted throughout, so the hold no longer has to keep an attachment alive across an unmount; its rule for reading again stays.
- **Why this, not that:** One signal serves the three cases and is what the ticket recommends. Reading at up plus a fixed delay was rejected as the main rule because the delay would be either too short on the relay or a needless wait on the LAN; it remains only as the 2 s backstop so that a stream that never answers cannot starve a tab. The wake case's own bound is 1 s per resubscription, so 2 s does not run beside a healthy resume.
- **Not included:** The narrow window the ticket also names (a loss said while the hold can neither hold nor acquire) is folded into this work's unit cases, not designed separately. The tech-debt tracker's row of 2026-11-11 on the same three reads closes with the ticket.
