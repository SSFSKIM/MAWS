# Round 1: seven experiments

Each takes the owner's brief (BRIEF.md) and commits to one idea of what is central. The ideas differ on where colour
lives, how much of the interface is glass, and how much is said in words.

| id | name | the idea | the question it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| `field` | Field | The app owns a quiet warm colour field; the sidebar is one pane of glass over it; work sits on an opaque sheet. | Does glass over an app-owned colour field feel warm and premium, or decorative? |
| `windows` | Windows | Everything is glass: two or three windows over one designed environment, the transcript read on glass. | Is the most radical reading what the wish for a modern, futuristic look means, and can a transcript be read on it? |
| `desktop` | Desktop | The Mac's own desktop shows through the sidebar, as in native apps; nothing else is translucent. | Is the cheapest glass to ship enough, and does it survive an ugly wallpaper? |
| `instrument` | Instrument | Content edge to edge on a pure ground; the only glass is a small layer of floating controls. | When glass is used only where it has a job, is there enough of it? |
| `signal` | Signal | The colour under the sidebar's glass is the state of each session, so the status words can go. | Can colour and light carry what the app now says in words? |
| `quiet` | Quiet | No glass: roundness, space and fewer words, in the manner of Claude Desktop, with the owner's colours. | How much of the wish is roundness and subtraction, with no material at all? |
| `essential` | Essential | The designer plugin's essentialist persona (Bauhaus, Rams, Ive) decides everything. | What does the wish look like under the strictest "less but better"? |
