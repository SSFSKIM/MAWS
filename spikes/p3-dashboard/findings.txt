QUESTION

Can a DOM-free rendering function meet the grade more simply than an unassisted in-house renderer?

Candidate A: exact-pinned d3-array 3.2.4, d3-scale 4.0.2, d3-shape 3.2.0 and d3-path 3.1.0 (all ISC). It uses
d3-scale's `nice`/`ticks`, `scaleBand` and `scaleUtc`, and d3-shape's line, area, arc, pie and symbol generators with a
null context and `digits(2)`. Those generators return path strings, as https://d3js.org/d3-shape/line documents.
Candidate B: small in-house arithmetic and path writing, no dependency.

METHOD

One shared layout (src/render.ts, text.ts, svg.ts, transform.ts, spec.ts) writes the SVG: frame, legend, axes, labels,
table, the error states and escaping. Everything numerical or geometric goes through one seam, the Kit (src/kit.ts):
nice domains, ticks, bands, lines, areas, pie angles, arcs and markers. kit-d3.ts implements it with d3 and
kit-house.ts in-house. Both candidates therefore receive the same closed input and theme and differ only where the
question asks. Data semantics (filter, then group and aggregate, then a stable sort, then limit; missing values counted,
never zero) are shared, because neither route changes them.

Theme: the house system's resolved document-medium values (HOUSE_TOKENS of
src/artifacts/document/export/design-system.ts), validated before any value enters an attribute.
The MAWS law (src/artifacts/types/design_system/defaults/maws/DESIGN.md) has no categorical palette, so:
- figures are drawn in text ink;
- series are told apart by form, always with a legend (two or more series) or a direct end label:
  - lines by dash and marker shape (circle, square, triangle, diamond, cross, wye);
  - scatter by marker shape;
  - bars by treatment: solid, hollow, 45° or 135° hatch, cross-hatch, dashed hollow;
  - pie wedges by their number in the list beside the pie, with tint as a second cue;
- status colours are never used and the accent is never used;
- gridlines are rule-colour hairlines, and the zero line is one step darker;
- type uses only the caption role (13 px, axes, legend, labels) and the body role (16 px, table cells); mono is used for
  measured values in tables.

Datasets (datasets/SOURCES.txt has URLs, licences, citations and sha256 of the raw and derived files):
- time series: NASA GISTEMP v4 global monthly and annual anomalies, 1880 to September 2026 (public US Government work;
  GISS's citation recorded). Includes negatives and three months not yet available.
- categorical comparison: World Bank WDI NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG, GDP growth of eight economies, 2019 to 2024 (CC BY 4.0).
  Negatives in 2020; the quoted "Korea, Rep." exercises RFC quoting.
- scatter: palmerpenguins, 344 penguins (CC0). NA cells: 2 penguins unmeasured, 11 with no sex.
- Korean country, species and island names are this spike's stated derivative labels. The only synthetic input is the
  10,000-point stress walk in src/fixtures.ts, a seeded LCG, named as synthetic.

Cases (src/fixtures.ts, 26):
- nine real views covering all six kinds: line ×3, area, bar ×2 (one grouped by sex with mean aggregation), scatter,
  pie (count), table;
- edge cases: empty bar, line and table; one row (line, pie, scatter); negative bars; null gaps; missing column; no
  dataset; unknown view kind; hostile labels and spec keys; extreme magnitudes (±1.8e308, 5e-324); long Korean labels
  in a bar chart and a table;
- the two synthetic 10,000-point views (line, and scatter in 3 series).

PREFERENCE AXES (the rule: among passing routes prefer less custom numerical/geometry code, then lower shipped bytes)

1. Custom numerical/geometry code (non-blank, non-comment lines): A's Kit is 56 lines, B's 176. The shared layout is
   the same for both: render.ts 461, text 48, svg 81, transform 77, spec 70.
   - B reached parity only by porting d3's algorithms. A first, naive version (1-2-5 steps, two `nice` passes, an
     approximate interval table) disagreed with d3 on 5 of 2,000 generated linear domains through float drift (for
     example [0.000413, 0.001026] ×10 lost its first tick, 0.0004) and on 60 of 240 time spans.
   - Agreement came only after porting d3-array's tickSpec (integer indexes over an inverse increment below 1), d3-scale's
     `nice` loop, and d3-time's UTC intervals: field-based `every`, Sunday weeks, d3's interval table and its geometric
     choice.
   - After the port: 2,000/2,000 linear domains, 240/240 time spans, and byte-equal lines, markers, bands and pie angles.
   - So B is a re-implementation of d3's numerics. M3 would own it and test it against d3 indefinitely.
2. Shipped bytes (esbuild production minify, the minifier electron-vite runs; ESM browser bundle; gzip -9):

   | | raw | gzip | brotli |

   | A renderer (layout + d3 Kit) | 72,214 | 26,964 | 24,001 |
   | B renderer (layout + house Kit) | 27,031 | 10,981 | 10,094 |
   | A Kit alone | 49,451 | 17,913 | 15,754 |

VERDICT (for review)

Both candidates pass every hard gate, so the rule's first preference decides: candidate A, d3's DOM-free helpers under
the in-house layout. B is the smaller bundle, the rule's second preference, about 16 KB gzip less. It earns that with
3× the custom numerical code: code that turned out to be d3's algorithms re-derived, and wrong in its first honest form.
A third route was not built or measured. M3 may weigh it: the scales could be replaced by d3-array's ticks/nice and
d3-time's UTC intervals (dropping d3-scale, d3-format, d3-time-format, d3-color and d3-interpolate) with a three-line
linear map.
Promote into M3: the shared layout's approach (closed spec, one Kit seam, ink with shapes and dashes, data-tip, no ids,
no references, the error states drawn in place) with kit-d3's d3 usage; exact pins as here.
Discard: kit-house.ts and its parity tests, before the final landing.

DISCOVERIES

- Real datasets do not use empty cells for missing values. GISTEMP writes `***`, palmerpenguins writes `NA`, and
  GISTEMP writes decimals with a leading dot (`-.19`). Under M2's rules ("empty values are null", strict finite
  numbers), the raw files would infer as text with mixed-type warnings unless the strict number grammar accepts a
  leading dot. The spike's derive.mjs maps `***` and `NA` to empty and keeps `-.19` verbatim; the spike's parser
  accepts it.
- A nice() time axis wastes the plot: 1880 to 2026 extends to 2040 at a 20-year step. X axes are therefore never niced
  (y axes are), in both candidates.
- Thinning labels on a band axis drops a category's identity (fixed). Thinning is for continuous axes only.
- A negative bar's value label needs room between the scale's bottom and the category row (fixed).
- d3's area writes the baseline under every point and its wedges start on the rim. The bytes differ from B's while the
  pixels are equal, so byte-identity between candidates is no criterion. Byte-identity within one candidate across
  runtimes held.
- The mini's capture scale changed between runs (1 in 38890516278, 2 in 38891409293). Picture comparisons must resample
  to one size (main's nativeImage does).
- `role="img"` with aria-label gives the figure its name in Chromium; its tree still lists the figure's text as the
  image's content, so the source table stays the accessible data path.
- docx 9.10.0 already writes SVG pictures with a raster fallback (`asvg:svgBlip`). The fallback raster must come from a
  window, as the plan's "picture fallback produced in the capture route" expects.
- d3-shape 3.2.0's `digits` and d3-path 3.1.0's `pathRound` give fixed-precision path strings, so neither candidate
  needs number formatting of its own inside paths.

HOW TO RUN

- Units: `pnpm exec vitest run --config spikes/p3-dashboard/vitest.config.ts`
- Export proof (CI, the Mac lane): `pnpm e2e:dashboard-spike`. It builds out/ with e2e/build.mjs, runs the units, then
  `playwright test -c spikes/p3-dashboard/e2e/playwright.config.ts`. Evidence prints as `SPIKE {json}` lines; pictures
  print as `SPIKE-PNG <name> <i>/<n> <base64>` chunks, decoded from `gh run view <id> --log`.
- Measurement (CI): `pnpm e2e:dashboard-spike-scale`, with MAWS_DASHBOARD_SCALE=1 set inline. It waits up to 5 min for
  the one-minute load to fall below 10 and records each reading; a load still at or above 10 is reported NOT QUIET.
- Data: `node spikes/p3-dashboard/datasets/derive.mjs` regenerates the four tables from raw/ and prints their sha256.
