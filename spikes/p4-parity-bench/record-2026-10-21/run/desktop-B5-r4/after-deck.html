Autumn Workshops 2026

Riverside Community Garden

Workshop sessions

Session lead: Grace Okafor, 21 October

Venue: the polytunnel, by the pond

Time: 10:00 to 11:30, tea afterwards

What we will cover

  • Saving seed from this year's crops
  • Building a leaf-mould bin
  • Planting garlic and broad beans
  • Putting the beds to sleep for winter

What to bring

  • Gloves and boots
  • A labelled jar for seeds
  • A flask if you like a warm drink

Signing up

Put your name on the sheet in the tool shed, or tell the volunteer coordinator. Places are limited to twelve per session.

Questions?

Ask any member of the steering group, or write to the volunteer coordinator.
