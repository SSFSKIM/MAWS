P4-R7: mid-turn messages to the human on MAWS's own wire, and the message tool
(docs/doperpowers/plans/2026-10-19-p4-smoothing.md, Decision Log 46; parent pin 08a31f8df08ed97911398057380acd9d3cd7e687
at master 44e4a3c5a; 2026-10-21)

Question (D-1). On the pinned engine over MAWS's own wire, with MAWS's launch, what does a message the model writes
before a tool call become: text, or a thinking block, and what does that block carry? Then: does `mcp__maws__message`
carry the message whole, where, and at what cost?

Method. scenarios/pass3/p4-r7-message-tool.mjs: MAWS's launch line and initialize (p1-common), the `maws` SDK server,
`--disallowedTools AskUserQuestion`, the to-human text through `--append-system-prompt`, engine 2.1.304 (PROBE_ENGINE),
claude-opus-5-5 at `--effort high`, through the ClaudeUsage relay with a recorder between engine and relay, so each
fixture holds what the engine asked the API for and every block the API streamed back, in order, beside the engine's
frames and the transcript's records. Fixtures fixtures/p4/p4-r7-*.jsonl; tests in fixtures.test.mjs ("p4 R7").

1. MAWS's wire asks for the notes. Every main request carries `thinking: {type: "adaptive", display: "updates"}` and
   the beta `thinking-display-updates-2026-08-18` (the engine sets it; MAWS passes nothing). So a progress note
   arrives as a thinking block holding the API's paraphrase, as in the owner's terminal transcripts.
2. The loss shows where the owner's sessions show it: the model's own words between pieces of work. loss-stages (a
   status after each of four stages, contract 5): the stage 1 and stage 3 statuses came back as `<message>` text; the
   stage 2 status, the one asking for a person to label three calls, came back as a non-empty thinking block, "Stage
   2/4 complete: 34 of 37 missing labels restored from audio, though calls 0412, 0977, and 1203 need manual labeling.
   …", no tags, right before the stage 3 Bash call in the same response. The transcript keeps it as a thinking block:
   every reader of text blocks (MAWS's parser, the report, previews, summaries) misses it.
3. A note the prompt asks for stayed text in every case: two sentences or six, first in the turn or between two calls
   (loss-short, loss, loss-mid). No non-empty thinking block appeared in those runs. loss-notice and loss-dialog,
   where the model chose its message itself, sent nothing mid-turn: the notice waited for the final message, and the
   dialog went through `mcp__maws__ask` (blocking) and a final span.
4. The tool. `mcp__maws__message` listed with `_meta["anthropic/alwaysLoad"]: true` is the one maws tool in the
   request's tools under tool search (`defer_loading` absent); the model called it in the same response as the next
   Bash call each time, its `text` whole in the stream, the frames, the `tools/call` (carrying
   `_meta["claudecode/toolUseId"]`) and on disk, answered `sent`; the final message stayed a span (tool,
   tool-stages). On disk the call's tool_result is written before the next call's tool_use record of the same
   response (the engine runs a call as soon as its block closes).
5. Permission. In default mode the engine asked `can_use_tool` for `mcp__maws__message` (tool); with
   `--allowedTools mcp__maws__message`, as MAWS now launches when it offers the server, it asked none (tool-stages).
6. The real MAWS sessions on this Mac (read-only survey of ~/.claude/projects, entrypoint sdk-ts, engine 2.1.303, the
   last ten days: 6 lane-on sessions, 220 responses with a tool call) held no visible progress note and no span before
   a call: 149 of those responses opened with an empty thinking block. The owner's interactive sessions of the last day
   held 540 non-empty progress notes before a call, 534 of them after a tool result, 44 in the second person, many of
   them messages ("I'll ping you right before the codesign prompt appears so you can enter your password").

The eval of the wording (scenarios/pass3/p4-r7-eval.mjs; counts in fixtures/p4/p4-r7-eval.json). Two tasks whose
work has the model message the human between two calls ("stages", 3 repetitions: status after each of four stages;
"deploy", 2 repetitions: tell me as soon as anything needs my attention, keep going), each under two arms: before
(contract 5, no tool) and after (contract 7, the tool listed and allowed). Counted per message, as doperpowers'
acceptance 6 counts: a mid-turn message is a span before a call (delivered), a tool call (delivered) or a non-empty
progress note the judge rules addressed to the human (lost; doperpowers-f7's prompt word for word, claude-opus-5-5 at
low effort through the relay).
