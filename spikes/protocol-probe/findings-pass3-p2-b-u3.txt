Outcome
-------
Yes, partly. On installed Claude Code 2.1.295, `claude plugin list --json
--available` carries `installCount` for the plugins of the marketplace named
`claude-plugins-official` (313 of its 315 entries) once that marketplace is
added, and for no other source: none of the 3,231 entries of the engine's
built-in directory (`anthropic-plugin-directory`, listed on every home, even an
empty one) and none of a third-party marketplace's. The count is the engine's
own: the CLI reads it from its plugin catalog (`plugin-catalog-cache.json`
under the config home's `plugins/`, fetched from a public GCS URL and kept for
24 h), keyed by `<name>@claude-plugins-official`, and puts it on a row whose
id matches. A home without that marketplace, or an engine that cannot fetch
the catalog, gets no counts at all. MAWS shows a count only on a row that
carries one and never computes or borrows one.

------
