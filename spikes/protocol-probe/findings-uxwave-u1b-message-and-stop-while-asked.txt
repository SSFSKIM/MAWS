UX wave, landing U1b, probe 9: a message sent while a blocking question waits (9a), and Stop while a terminal
session's sminos asynchronous question waits (9b), engine 2.1.322 (U1b, requests drawn in the transcript: the
acceptance lines "While a blocking question waits, a message sent from the composer is delivered as a message and
the question still waits" and "Stop ends the turn and leaves the question open"; asked by the architect 2026-11-15;

Verdict.
  9a, a message while a blocking question waits. The same in 12 of 12 runs, for both question kinds: the engine's
  own AskUserQuestion held as an unanswered can_use_tool, and MAWS's blocking mcp__maws__ask held as an unanswered
  tools/call.
    - The message is QUEUED, not injected. The engine answers it with command_lifecycle `queued` (its
      command_uuid = the message's uuid) within 3 ms. The file writes a `queue-operation` enqueue with its text.
      There is no replay and nothing else. The pending request stays: no control_cancel_request, no tool_result, no
      notifications/cancelled. The question still waits (6 s watched, then answered or stopped).
    - Answered: the tool's tool_result comes first. Then, 10 to 30 ms later, the message's replay (isReplay true,
      same uuid) and lifecycle `started`. The model reads the answer and the message in ONE request and replies to
      both ("Mango\nKIWI", 6 of 6). In the file the message becomes an `attachment` {type: "queued_command",
      source_uuid: the message} placed after the tool_result, plus a queue-operation `remove`. It never becomes a
      `user` row. Both commands end `completed` with one `result success`.
    - Stopped instead, the question never answered (Stop's interrupt {reason: "user-cancel", cancel_queued:
      false}): the interrupt's answer lists the message as still waiting, {still_queued: [<the message's uuid>]}.
      The pending request is withdrawn: for AskUserQuestion by control_cancel_request {request_id}, before the
      interrupt's answer; for mcp__maws__ask by an mcp_message `notifications/cancelled` {requestId: <the
      tools/call id>, reason: "AbortError: remote-cancel"}, before the answer. Then the tool_result is_error ("The
      user doesn't want to proceed with this tool use. …") and "[Request interrupted by user for tool use]", and
      `result error_during_execution` (stop_reason tool_use). The message SURVIVES: at once, with no new prompt,
      the engine starts it as the next turn (lifecycle `started`, its replay, `requesting`), and the model answers it
      (result success, its reply ending KIWI, 6 of 6).
  So the first acceptance line holds as written, with one qualification: the message reaches the model only once
  the question is answered or the turn ends. Until then it is a queued message: a lifecycle `queued` frame and a
  queue-operation row, with no replay.

  9b, Stop while a terminal session's sminos asynchronous question waits (the bridge's Stop, then answer, 3 runs;
  then dismiss, 3 runs; the same each time).
    - Stop ends the turn and LEAVES THE QUESTION OPEN. The bridge's `interrupt` answers {ok: true, result: {turnId,
      stopped: [<the backgrounded Bash task>], running: []}}. The turn ends `aborted` (event `turn` phase complete).
      The session writes three rows of origin plugin sminos: isMeta "[Request interrupted by user]", an isMeta note
      ("The interrupt came from outside the terminal. Task <id>, the Bash command the interrupt moved to the

      bridge's own list (the /events snapshot's `pending`) and at GET /requests/<id> (200, the request), 6 of 6.
      No settlement is written, by anyone.
    - Answered after the Stop (POST /requests/<id>/decision {behavior: "allow", updatedInput: {answers}, by:

      "maws"}. GET /requests/<id> turns 410 with that settlement, and the list moves it from pending to settled. The
      idle session gets the answer as a NEW TURN: queue-operation enqueue and dequeue, then a `user` row of origin
      {kind: "plugin", name: "sminos"} (not isMeta, no asUser): "The sminos plugin sent a message:\nThe human
      responded to your async AskUserQuestion (call <id>): Your questions have been answered: \"…\"=\"Mango\". …".
      Then a bridge `turn` start whose text is that message, and the model takes it up (3 of 3) and says the fruit.
      In r1 it reran the stopped sleep first; in r2 and r3 it answered without a tool.

Inference, in U1b's terms.
  - 9a: a composer send while a blocking question waits is delivered as a queued message. A window can draw it as a
    message waiting (lifecycle `queued`) under a card that stays. It joins the turn right after the answer's
    tool_result. If Stop ends the turn instead, the question's card leaves (control_cancel_request for the engine's
    own question; for MAWS's ask, the host's tools/call is cancelled by notifications/cancelled), and the message
    runs as the next turn by itself. With cancel_queued false, Stop does not drop it.
  - 9b: "Stop ends the turn and leaves the question open" holds for a terminal session's sminos asynchronous
    question. The card should stay after Stop. An answer opens a new turn delivered as a plugin message; a
    dismissal settles it `withdrawn` by maws and the session hears nothing.
