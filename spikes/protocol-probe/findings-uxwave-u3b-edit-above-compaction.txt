Verdict.
  - In the LIVE session that compacted, the engine ACCEPTS a rewind onto a message from before the boundary.
    Onto ONE, the first prompt: {rewound: true, targetMessageUuid: ONE, prefillText: ONE's text,
    precedingAssistantUuid: null}. The next prompt is then written with parentUuid null: the session starts over
    empty, with no summary, no boundary in its chain, and nothing of ONE, /compact or TWO. It answers normally.
    Probe 6's forks behaved differently: they refused the same target (target_not_found).
  - After a RESTART (the same session ended and launched again with `--resume`), the same rewind onto ONE is
    REFUSED: {rewound: false, prefillText: null, precedingAssistantUuid: null, error: "target not found", reason:
    "target_not_found"}. Nothing is written, nothing changes, and the session takes the next prompt and answers it,
    continuing after TWO's answer. So whether an Edit above a compaction works depends on whether the engine process
    that compacted is still the one running.
  - Onto the /compact command's own row (live): accepted. The next prompt hangs under the /compact caveat, so the
    boundary and the summary stay, and the command, its "Compacted" row and TWO are gone. Onto TWO (the control):
    accepted, and the next prompt hangs under "Compacted". In both cases precedingAssistantUuid is ONE's answer,
    which is FROM BEFORE THE BOUNDARY, while the forks of probe 6 answered null for the same targets.
  - A rewind sends nothing on the wire beyond its control_response (3 s watched). In the file it writes only a
    `last-prompt` row; the cut shows in the next prompt's parentUuid.

Inference, for landing 2.
  - An Edit under a message from before a compaction succeeds only in the engine process that compacted. It then
    empties the conversation down to that message, summary included: rewinding to ONE leaves nothing above it, since
    ONE was first. In any process launched later (a restart, a resume) the engine refuses it, and the session goes
    on as before. MAWS must treat "target not found" for such a message as the normal outcome after a restart, and
    should not offer the Edit or should say why. The engine's own words are "target not found".
  - In the live case precedingAssistantUuid can name an answer from before the boundary, one that is no longer in
    the model's context after a compaction. Do not use it to find the row the cut lands on; the next prompt's
    parentUuid is the truth.
  - A refusal leaves the session usable (1 of 1): the next prompt is taken and answered.
