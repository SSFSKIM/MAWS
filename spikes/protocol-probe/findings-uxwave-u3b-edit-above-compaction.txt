    RESTORES the conversation as it stood before that message, from the rows written before the compaction. Probe

    first written, line 3 and 25), with no boundary and no summary in its chain. Asked without a tool for the words

  - Live rewinds onto the /compact row and onto the prompt after the compaction are accepted (probe 7, below).

  - A rewind sends nothing on the wire beyond its control_response, and writes only a `last-prompt` row.

Inference, for landing 2.
  - An Edit under a message from before a compaction succeeds only in the engine process that compacted. It then
    empties the conversation down to that message, summary included: rewinding to ONE leaves nothing above it, since
    ONE was first. In any process launched later (a restart, a resume) the engine refuses it, and the session goes
    on as before. MAWS must treat "target not found" for such a message as the normal outcome after a restart, and
    should not offer the Edit or should say why. The engine's own words are "target not found".
  - In the live case precedingAssistantUuid can name an answer from before the boundary, one that is no longer in
    the model's context after a compaction. Do not use it to find the row the cut lands on; the next prompt's
    parentUuid is the truth.
  - A refusal leaves the session usable (1 of 1): the next prompt is taken and answered.

  Inference, for landing 2.
  - A live Edit under a message from before a compaction is a real rewind across the boundary. The engine rebuilds
    the conversation from the pre-compaction rows up to the target, and drops the summary and everything after it.
    The model then holds those messages verbatim, not the summary (1 of 1, "ONE AFTER").
  - The same Edit after a restart is refused, as probe 7 found. MAWS can only offer it while the session's process
    is the one that compacted, or must report the engine's "target not found" as expected.
