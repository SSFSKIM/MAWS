UX wave U5, P4: `reply` (docs/doperpowers/specs/2026-11-12-ux-wave/U5-review-comments-source-control.md, Probes and
checks, P4; it serves l6-artifacts-20, the agent's reply in its own words; 2026-11-12)

Question. One comment answered by an edit on a scratch document: does the agent give `artifact_edit`'s `reply` from the
tool's description alone? If not, the description's sentence is tightened before the fallback sentence ("Edited this
passage.") is relied on.

Verdict: yes. In 10 of 10 runs on the owner's model (5 with the comment sent to the main conversation, 5 as a side
thread's first message) the agent read the document, then made one artifact_edit carrying answering "t1" and a reply of
one sentence in its own words about what it changed. It never fell back to artifact_reply, never omitted reply, and
never edited without answering. The sentence as the spec words it is enough; no tightening needed. Confidence: high for
this comment on this model (10/10); one comment shape only (below).

Method. scenarios/pass3/uxwave-p4-reply.mjs on devcloud VM dc-uxprobe-dc94 through vm-run.sh (-e UXP4_RUNS=5), engine
2.1.322 (system/init, every fixture), routed through the gateway. Model: the owner's default, `opus` with the owner's
alias ANTHROPIC_DEFAULT_OPUS_MODEL=claude-opus-5-5[1m] and CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_MODEL=opus (system/init reports
claude-opus-5-5). Launch line as MAWS launches when it serves its `maws` server: the line of uxwave-p1-images.mjs
(findings-uxwave-u5-images.txt) plus --disallowedTools AskUserQuestion --allowedTools mcp__maws__ask, and initialize
declaring sdkMcpServers ["maws"]; set_max_thinking_tokens sent after initialize. Probe guard: --max-budget-usd 1.50.
Tools: the repository's own definitions of artifact_read, artifact_edit, artifact_comments, artifact_reply and
artifact_resolve (mawsToolList() at ux-wave/probe-setup 8bd124247, dumped to scenarios/pass3/uxwave-p4-maws-tools.json,
served whole: title, description, input schema, annotations, and _meta anthropic/alwaysLoad, so artifact_edit is not
deferred by tool search), from the harness's in-process MCP server. artifact_edit carries the U5 change as the spec
words it: the optional `reply` {type string, 1 to 2,000 characters} and one sentence placed right after the description's
`answering` sentence: "With answering, give reply: one or two sentences in your own words saying what you changed; it
is posted as your reply in the thread." Nothing else in the description changed (it still says answering "posts the
result into that thread"). The document is the scenario's: "Launch plan", four blocks, one open thread t1 by the user on
"we email all customers" saying "Only customers who opted in to product news should get this email. Please change it.";
the tool answers keep the real tools' shapes. The comment reaches the agent as X10's anchored text built as
src/shared/anchor/message.ts builds it (lead `Comment on "Launch plan" (passage "…" in document <id>, thread t1):`, the
comment, for a side thread the preamble, the <maws_anchor …/> tag). The side-thread runs are fresh sessions whose
first message carries the preamble (no parent conversation was forked).
Fixtures: fixtures/uxwave/uxwave-p4-reply-{main,thread}-{1..5}.jsonl; each has an `outcome` meta row with the tool
calls and their arguments, the thread after the run, the answer and the cost (about US$0.06 a run).

Observed (outcome row seqs 103 to 129). Every run: artifact_read, then artifact_edit {baseRevision 7, a replace_text or
replace_block on p2, answering "t1", reply}. Replies, for instance: "Changed it so the launch email goes only to
customers who opted in to product news, not to all customers." (main 1); "I changed the sentence so the launch-morning
email goes only to customers who opted in to product news." (thread 3). All ten are one sentence of 70 to 120
characters. No run called artifact_comments, artifact_reply or artifact_resolve; each answered the user in chat as well,
quoting the new sentence.

Not established. Other comment shapes (a question rather than a change request; a comment needing no edit; several
comments at once), suggest mode (where the description tells the agent to use artifact_reply instead), and the model
when the owner runs another one. The engine's other maws tools were not served (only the five document tools).
