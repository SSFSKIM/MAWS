UX wave, child U5, probe P7: a message of several comments sent to a terminal session through the sminos bridge, as
MAWS's terminal route sends it, on engine 2.1.322
(docs/doperpowers/plans/2026-11-12-ux-wave-U5a-comment-card.md on ux-wave/U5a, "Probes", P7; the message's text as
Interfaces §A defines it at 71e6f199b; 2026-11-12/13, VM dc-uxwave2-e584, branch ux-wave/probe2-text-defaults)

Question. Does `laneA.send` deliver a text of several lines (blank lines, a `<maws_anchor …/>` line, `&gt;` in a tag's
attribute, a non-ASCII letter, an emoji; and, at 71e6f199b, the framing line `2 comments, in the order made:` and a
comment's own tag-shaped lines escaped with one more leading backslash) to a terminal session intact, idle, during a
turn, after an Esc, and at about 70,000 characters? Which other mods with a prompt or append hook are loaded?

Verdict. Intact, in every state: the document's branch "intact" applies, and the fallback in `sendComments` is not
needed. The row the engine kept in the session's transcript file is byte for byte the text sent (SHA-256 equal) in
12 of 12 deliveries: 8 with the framed text of 71e6f199b (two runs, four states each) and 4 with the earlier form
(one run). The framing line, the blank lines, both escaped lines (`\<maws_anchor…` and `\\<maws_anchor…`), the
`&gt;` in the tags, `é` and the emoji arrive as sent. Every delivery's fate on the bridge's stream was `submitted`,
then `entered` naming that row's uuid; none was refused. MAWS's reader (master's `parseAnchoredText`, which a
framed message's fallback reading is) reads the kept row exactly as it reads the text sent, in every case.

Per state (framed runs r1 and r2; line numbers are the reduced transcripts'):
  1. idle: the text is submitted as the person's prompt with no wrapper. Kept as a `user` row whose content is a
     string, origin {kind: "plugin", name: "sminos", asUser: true} (r1 line 40, r2 line 40). 700 UTF-16 units,
     703 bytes, sha256 478768ca75b4… sent and kept. The model answered as the comments asked (NOTED).
  2. during a turn, the delivery posted 5 s into a running 30 s foreground call (the call was in the transcript with
     no result at the post: `runningAtSend: true`): kept as an appended `user` row, isMeta true, content one text
     block, origin {kind: "plugin", name: "sminos"} with no asUser (r1 51, r2 51); sha256 a4aa38492cd1… both. The
     next request to the model carried it after the call's result (the api-request-blob row, r1 54 and r2 54, holds
     the tool result then this text, equal to it). Asked afterwards to quote the last line of the message, the model
     gave the second comment's tag exactly (2 of 2).
  3. after an Esc during the same 30 s call (the transcript then holds "[Request interrupted by user for tool use]",
     r1 77, r2 76), then a send: the bridge's prompt is refused by the engine after a stop and the bridge appends it
     instead (sminos's documented path, hooks/mods/bridge.tsx `deliver` → `appendDelivery`); the fate still reads
     only `submitted`, `entered`. Kept as an appended isMeta `user` row, origin plugin sminos without asUser
     (r1 78, r2 77), sha256 7fb36c546239… both; then the bridge wakes the stopped session through its inbox: a
     separate `user` row, isMeta, origin {kind: "peer", name: "sminos bridge"}, holding the plugin's own wake text,
     not the comments (r1 83, r2 82). The model's next request carried both (r1 84, r2 83). Asked to quote the last
     line of the comments, the model gave the tag exactly in r2, and in r1 quoted the wake message's last line

  4. 70,081 UTF-16 units (72,533 bytes), idle: kept whole as the prompt row (r1 100, r2 99), sha256 436da1e271a2…;
     the model answered.
  The earlier form (run v1, before 71e6f199b: no framing line, comments without tag-shaped lines) was intact in all
  four too (v1 lines 40, 56, 92, 107), but its steps 2 and 3 did not test what they name: the session refused the
  plain `sleep 30` the document's step names (the model reported a block pointing it to Monitor or a background
  job), so step 2's delivery landed in a short turn (still as an appended isMeta row, line 56) and step 3's Esc hit
  no running call, so its delivery was an idle prompt (line 92). The framed runs use a 30 s foreground `python3`
  wait instead, and check it is running before the post and the Esc.

What the design should know (observed; the inferences marked):
  - The rows differ by state, not the text: idle and long are prompt rows (string content, asUser true); during a
    turn and after an Esc they are isMeta rows of one text block, origin plugin without asUser. A transcript reader
    that skips isMeta rows, or keys "the person's words" on asUser, will not draw the chip for a comment sent during
    a turn or after an Esc (inference; which rows `userTextOf` reads was not checked here; findings-uxwave-u3-sminos.txt
    saw the same isMeta row for a busy delivery).
  - Each appended delivery is followed by a `system` row, subtype informational, level notice, content
    `sminos: delivered: <the text with every newline replaced by a space>` (r1 52 and 79): the mod's log line. It is
    not the message and must not be read as one.
  - After an Esc, the inbox wake row (origin peer, "sminos bridge") follows the delivered row; it carries none of
    the comments' text.
  - Step 5 (65,536 units over the machine link, 65,537 refused) needs a second machine and was not run. On this
    machine 70,081 units passed the bridge and the engine whole.

Other mods loaded (step 6). The managed settings enable and prepend three plugins from the VM's marketplace copy:
to-human 0.7.3, sminos 1.46.3, doperpowers 7.220.0. Loaded in these runs: sminos (its bridge served the deliveries;
its tools in the transcript's mcp_instructions_delta), doperpowers (its tools in the same attachment, its kairos
button in the footer); to-human's load was not seen directly (its message tool registers only under its output
style, not set here). Their hooks on a prompt or an append, read from the plugin sources (nothing else of theirs
touches a prompt's or a row's text):

    reminder.tsx:80 (adds context beside the first prompt past 150k/300k/450k tokens, only while its message tool is
    on), sminos ask.tsx:1601 (adds held answers as context items, not to the text), sminos bridge.tsx:2068 (observes
    only); prompt.submit by origin: doperpowers compact.tsx:601 and :629, interim.ts:232 (task notifications).
  - session.append: sminos bridge.tsx:2489, to-human message.tsx:293, notify.tsx:68, to-human.tsx:1197, doperpowers

  - Command hooks: SessionStart (sminos smns-record.sh, smns-guide.sh; doperpowers kairos.sh), Stop (smns-record.sh),

  Context these add rides as separate attachments; the byte comparison above is of the row itself, with all of them

Method. Engine 2.1.322 (`claude --version` 2.1.322), model haiku (claude-haiku-5-5), interactive in tmux (160 x 48),
under `./vm-run.sh --managed --prefix p2b -e CLAUDE_CODE_ENABLE_FUNCTION_HOOKS=1 run run-pass3.mjs
uxwave-u5-terminal-text` (spikes/protocol-probe/VM.md; the HOME is scratch, so sminos's state was private and
unenrolled: `sminos list --machine <vm>` showed no member before and after). The sender is the scenario,
scenarios/pass3/uxwave-u5-terminal-text.mjs, posting as src/services/lane-a/driver.ts and deliveries.ts do: `GET
/status` for `launch`, then `POST /command?wait=10000` with {op: "deliver", key: {writer: "maws/probe-vm", seq},
text, launch} on $HOME/.claude/sminos/bridge/<session>.sock, the fate read from `GET /events`. The text is built with
master's `anchorLead` and `anchorTag` (src/shared/anchor/message.ts, bundled with esbuild) in §A's framed form, two
comments: a browser element "Menu > Settings" (tag attributes `&gt;`) whose comment has a blank line, `café` and a
line `<maws_anchor kind="none" …/>` (sent as `\<maws_anchor…`); and one.txt line 1, whose comment has 🎉 and a line
`\<maws_anchor …/>` (sent as `\\<maws_anchor…`). The long text replaces the second comment with ~860 numbered lines.
`UXW_FORMAT=v1` sends the earlier form. Comparison: SHA-256 of the UTF-8 text sent and of the kept row's text (a
string, or its text blocks joined), the first differing offset (none), and every other row holding the step's marker.
Runs: v1 (the earlier form, the scenario's first version), r1 and r2 (the framed form, the version committed).
Fixtures, all reduced (the raw transcripts, panes and event streams carry the owner's plugins' hook output and stayed
on the VM under /tmp): fixtures/uxwave/u5-terminal-text-{r1,r2,v1}.json (each step: the text sent, or its head, tail
and hash for the long one; the post's answer; the fates; the kept row's line, type, isMeta, origin, shape, hash,
equality; the reader over both; other rows with the marker; the model's quote) and .rows.ndjson (every transcript row
reduced to type, origin and shape; text kept only for the probe's prompts and the model's short answers; two answers
in r1 that restate the plugin's wake text are replaced by a note saying so).
